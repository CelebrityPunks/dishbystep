Pork Soup Dumplings

China cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup hot water
  • 1/4 tsp fine sea salt

Filling And Serving

  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 2 tsp unflavored gelatin
  • 2 green onions, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 1/4 cup black vinegar
  • 2 tbsp julienned ginger

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 1 1/2 cups flour with 1/2 cup hot water, knead 8 min, then rest the dough 1 hour.

Step 2

Stir 2 tsp gelatin into 1/2 cup chicken stock, chill until firm, then cut into cubes.

Step 3

Combine 1/2 lb ground pork with the ginger, scallions, soy sauce, wine and the aspic cubes.

Step 4

Roll the rested dough into 12 thin rounds, each about 3 1/2 inches wide.

Step 5

Spoon 1 tbsp filling into each wrapper, pleat with about 18 folds, and pinch shut.

Step 6

Set the dumplings on cabbage leaves in a bamboo steamer, spaced apart.

Step 7

Steam over boiling water for 8-10 min, until the skins turn slightly translucent.

Step 8

Serve hot with black vinegar and julienned ginger, and sip the soup first.
