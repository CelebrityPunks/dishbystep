Brazilian Brigadeiros

Brazil cuisine · Serves 20 truffles · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For Rolling And Serving

  • 1/2 cup (60 g) chocolate sprinkles
  • 20 mini paper candy cups, for serving

For The Truffles

  • 1 can (14 oz / 397 g) sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter, plus more for greasing
  • 1 pinch fine sea salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Combine 1 can condensed milk, 2 tbsp cocoa powder, 2 tbsp butter, and a pinch of salt in a small heavy saucepan.

Step 2

Stir constantly over medium-low heat for 10 to 12 minutes until the mixture thickens and bubbles slowly.

Step 3

Keep stirring until the thick chocolate pulls away from the pan and a dragged spoon leaves a clean trail.

Step 4

Spread the chocolate into a buttered dish and chill 30 minutes until firm and scoopable.

Step 5

With buttered hands, roll the firm mixture into 20 one-inch balls and set them on a tray.

Step 6

Roll each ball in 1/2 cup chocolate sprinkles until fully coated.

Step 7

Set the coated truffles into mini paper cups on a serving plate.

Step 8

Serve at room temperature. Cut one open to show the glossy fudgy center.
