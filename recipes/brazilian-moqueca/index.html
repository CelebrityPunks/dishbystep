Creamy Brazilian Moqueca

Brazil cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Seafood

  • 2 lb firm white fish (sea bass, cod, or mahi mahi), cut into 2 to 3 inch chunks
  • 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

For The Stew

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves and 1 red chili pepper, finely chopped
  • 1 large red onion, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper and 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 large tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp dende (red palm oil)
  • 1 large bunch cilantro, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

To Serve

  • Cooked white rice
  • Lime wedges

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss the fish chunks and shrimp with the lime juice, salt, and pepper. Rest 15 minutes.

Step 2

Heat the olive oil in a large pot and saute the garlic and chili 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 3

Layer the onion, peppers, and tomatoes over the garlic, then nestle in the fish and shrimp.

Step 4

Pour in the coconut milk, cover, and bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.

Step 5

Simmer 12 to 15 minutes until the fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

Step 6

Drizzle in the red palm oil and swirl gently through the stew.

Step 7

Shower with fresh cilantro and finish with a squeeze of lime.

Step 8

Ladle over fluffy white rice and serve with extra lime wedges.
