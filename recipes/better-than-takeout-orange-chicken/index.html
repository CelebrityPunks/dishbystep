Better-Than-Takeout Orange Chicken

China cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • 3 cups vegetable oil, for frying

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1 tbsp orange zest
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch plus 2 tbsp water
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 3 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 3 green onions, sliced, plus 1 tsp sesame seeds

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 1 1/2 lb chicken pieces with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper, dip in 2 beaten eggs, then coat in 1 cup cornstarch.

Step 2

Heat 3 cups oil to 350 degrees F, fry half the chicken for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and crisp, then drain.

Step 3

Fry the remaining chicken the same way until golden and crisp, then drain.

Step 4

Whisk 1 cup orange juice, 1 tbsp zest, 1/3 cup sugar, 1/4 cup rice vinegar, and 2 tbsp soy sauce.

Step 5

Cook 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp ginger, and 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes in a skillet for 30 seconds until fragrant.

Step 6

Pour in the orange sauce and simmer 2 to 3 minutes, then stir in the cornstarch slurry and simmer 1 minute until glossy and thick.

Step 7

Toss the crisp chicken in the glossy orange sauce until every piece is coated.

Step 8

Spoon the sauced chicken over warm jasmine rice, scatter green onions and sesame seeds, then serve hot.
