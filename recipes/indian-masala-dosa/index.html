Masala Dosa

India cuisine · Serves 10 dosas (serves 5) · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Batter

  • 1 1/2 cups parboiled idli rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1/4 cup poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 tbsp chana dal
  • 20 fenugreek seeds
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt

Potato Masala

  • 4 medium potatoes (about 300 g), boiled
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 1/4 cups sliced onions (2 medium)
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 8 curry leaves
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 3 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
  • salt to taste

Coconut Chutney

  • 1 cup fresh grated coconut
  • 2 tbsp roasted chana dal
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1/2-inch piece ginger
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • tempering: 1 tsp oil, 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 5 curry leaves, 1 dried red chili

Quick Sambar

  • 1/2 cup toor dal
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans)
  • 1 tbsp sambar powder
  • 1 tsp tamarind paste
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • salt to taste
  • tempering: 1 tbsp oil, 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds, 8 curry leaves, 1 dried red chili

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 1 1/2 cups rice, 1/2 cup urad dal, 1/4 cup poha, 1 tbsp chana dal and 20 fenugreek seeds for 6 hours, then grind to a smooth batter.

Step 2

Ferment the batter 8 to 12 hours in a warm spot until doubled and bubbly, then stir in 1 1/2 tsp salt.

Step 3

Boil 4 potatoes until tender, then temper 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, sliced onions, chilies, ginger, curry leaves and 1/4 tsp turmeric and fold in the potatoes.

Step 4

Grind 1 cup coconut, 2 tbsp roasted chana dal, 2 green chilies, ginger and 1/2 tsp salt with 1/2 cup water into a smooth chutney, then pour over the hot mustard-seed tempering.

Step 5

Simmer 1/2 cup toor dal with the vegetables, 1 tbsp sambar powder, 1 tsp tamarind and 1/4 tsp turmeric for 20 minutes into a quick sambar, then add the tempering.

Step 6

Heat a cast-iron tawa, ladle on the batter and spread it thin in circles, then drizzle oil and cook 2 to 3 minutes until golden and crisp.

Step 7

Spoon the potato masala down the center, fold the dosa over and slide it onto a plate.

Step 8

Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
