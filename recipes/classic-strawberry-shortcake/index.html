Classic Strawberry Shortcake

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 3/4 cup cold heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream, for brushing
  • 1 tbsp coarse sugar, for topping

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cold heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Hull and slice 2 lb strawberries, toss with 1/2 cup sugar and 1 tbsp lemon juice, and rest for 30 minutes until juicy.

Step 2

Whisk 2 cups flour, 1/4 cup sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder and 1/2 tsp salt, then cut in 1/2 cup cold cubed butter.

Step 3

Stir in 3/4 cup cold heavy cream until a soft dough just comes together.

Step 4

Pat the dough 1 inch thick and cut 8 rounds with a floured cutter.

Step 5

Brush the tops with 2 tbsp cream, sprinkle with 1 tbsp coarse sugar, and bake at 425°F for 14 to 16 minutes until golden.

Step 6

Beat 2 cups cold heavy cream with 1/4 cup powdered sugar and 1 tsp vanilla until soft peaks form.

Step 7

Split the warm biscuits and spoon the berries and their juices over the bottom halves.

Step 8

Top with whipped cream and more berries, cap with the biscuit tops, and serve right away.
