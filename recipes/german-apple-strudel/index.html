German Apple Strudel

Germany cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup (120ml) lukewarm water
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp white vinegar

Filling

  • 4 medium tart apples (about 1 1/2 lb), peeled, cored, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup (75g) raisins
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup (50g) plain breadcrumbs
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and divided
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 2 cups flour, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 cup lukewarm water, 2 tbsp oil, and 1 tsp vinegar; knead 8 to 10 minutes. Rest, covered, 30 minutes.

Step 2

Toast 1/2 cup breadcrumbs in 4 tbsp melted butter over medium heat 3 to 4 minutes until golden; cool.

Step 3

Toss 4 thinly sliced tart apples with 1/2 cup sugar, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tbsp lemon juice, and 1/2 cup raisins.

Step 4

Stretch the rested dough on a floured cloth into a paper-thin rectangle about 24 by 16 inches.

Step 5

Brush with 2 tbsp melted butter and scatter the toasted breadcrumbs over two thirds of the dough.

Step 6

Spread the apple filling along one long edge, leaving a 2 inch border.

Step 7

Roll up tightly with the help of the cloth; pinch the ends and place seam side down on a lined baking sheet.

Step 8

Bake at 375°F for 35 to 40 minutes until deep golden; dust with powdered sugar and serve warm.
