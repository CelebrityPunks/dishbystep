General Tso's Chicken

China cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1 cup cornstarch, for coating
  • vegetable oil, for frying

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup water or chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Ingredients

  • 5 to 6 dried red chilies
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • steamed jasmine rice, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs into 1-inch chunks and toss with 2 egg whites, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp white pepper.

Step 2

Dredge every chunk in 1 cup cornstarch, shake off the excess, and rest 10 minutes.

Step 3

Fry the chicken in batches in 350 F oil for 5 to 6 minutes until deep golden and crisp.

Step 4

Whisk 1/4 cup soy sauce, 1/4 cup rice vinegar, 3 tbsp hoisin, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 1/4 cup water, 1 tbsp cornstarch, and 1 tsp sesame oil.

Step 5

Stir-fry the dried chilies, 3 minced garlic cloves, and 1 tbsp minced ginger in 1 tbsp hot oil for 30 seconds.

Step 6

Pour in the sauce and boil 1 to 2 minutes until thick and glossy.

Step 7

Return the crispy chicken to the wok and toss until every piece is lacquered in sauce.

Step 8

Serve over steamed jasmine rice with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.
