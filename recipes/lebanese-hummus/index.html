Classic Creamy Hummus

Lebanon cuisine · Serves 8 as a dip (makes about 2 cups) · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Hummus Base

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (about 1 large lemon)
  • 1/4 cup well-stirred tahini
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for serving
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 2 to 3 tbsp ice water

To Serve

  • Warm pita bread
  • Smoked paprika for dusting
  • Whole chickpeas for topping
  • Chopped fresh parsley

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whip 1/4 cup tahini with 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice in the food processor until pale and fluffy, about 1 minute.

Step 2

Add 1 minced garlic clove, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp cumin, and 3/4 tsp salt, then process 30 seconds.

Step 3

Add the 15 oz drained chickpeas and process 2 full minutes, scraping down the sides once.

Step 4

With the processor running, drizzle in 2 to 3 tbsp ice water, one spoon at a time, until silky.

Step 5

Keep blending 3 to 4 more minutes until completely smooth and airy.

Step 6

Taste and adjust with more salt or lemon juice until the flavor shines.

Step 7

Spoon onto a wide plate and swirl a deep well with the back of a spoon.

Step 8

Fill the well with olive oil, dust with paprika, top with chickpeas and parsley, and serve with warm pita.
