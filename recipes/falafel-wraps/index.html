Falafel Wraps

Lebanon cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Falafel

  • 8 oz dried chickpeas (1 cup), soaked overnight
  • 3/4 cup fresh cilantro leaves and stems
  • 3/4 cup fresh parsley leaves
  • 1/2 onion, chopped fine
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 quarts vegetable oil, for frying

Tahini Sauce + To Serve

  • 1/3 cup tahini
  • 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice (2 lemons)
  • 1/4 cup water
  • salt, to taste
  • 4 large pita breads, warmed
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 2 medium tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced

Method, in pictures

Step 1

The night before, soak 1 cup dried chickpeas in plenty of cold water for 8 to 12 hours.

Step 2

Drain the chickpeas and pulse with the herbs, onion, garlic, and spices to a coarse meal.

Step 3

Mix in the flour and baking powder, then chill the mixture for 1 to 2 hours.

Step 4

With wet hands, roll the mixture into 20 to 24 golf ball sized falafel.

Step 5

Heat 2 quarts vegetable oil in a deep pot to 350F.

Step 6

Fry in small batches for 4 to 5 minutes until deep golden, then drain on paper towels.

Step 7

Whisk the tahini, yogurt, lemon juice, water, and salt into a smooth sauce.

Step 8

Stuff warm pitas with falafel, salad, and plenty of tahini sauce, then serve.
