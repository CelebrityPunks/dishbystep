Classic Mexican Flan

Mexico cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Caramel

  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup water

Custard

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Melt 3/4 cup sugar with 1/4 cup water until deep amber.

Step 2

Swirl the hot caramel to coat the bottom of a 9 inch round mold.

Step 3

Blend the condensed milk, evaporated milk, eggs, and vanilla until smooth.

Step 4

Pour the custard over the cooled caramel in the mold.

Step 5

Bake in a hot water bath at 350F for 50 to 60 minutes until set.

Step 6

Chill at least 4 hours or overnight until firm.

Step 7

Run a knife around the edge and flip the flan onto a plate.

Step 8

Slice and drizzle with the golden caramel sauce.
