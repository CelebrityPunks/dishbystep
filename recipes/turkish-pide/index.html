Turkish Pide

Turkey cuisine · Serves 4 (4 boats) · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Dough

  • 500g all-purpose flour
  • 7g instant dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300ml warm water
  • 3 tbsp olive oil

For The Filling

  • 400g ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika

To Finish

  • 150g shredded mozzarella or kasar cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, warm water and oil, then knead for 8 minutes until smooth.

Step 2

Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour until doubled in size.

Step 3

Brown 400g beef with the onion and green pepper, then stir in the tomato paste, tomatoes and spices.

Step 4

Divide the dough into 4 pieces and roll each into a long oval.

Step 5

Spread the meat filling down the center and fold the edges up into boat shapes.

Step 6

Bake at 230C (450F) for 12 minutes until the crusts turn golden.

Step 7

Crack an egg over each pide and bake 3 more minutes until the white just sets.

Step 8

Serve: brush the crusts with melted butter, scatter parsley, slice and serve hot.
