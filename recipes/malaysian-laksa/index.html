Malaysian Chicken Laksa

Malaysia cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Laksa Paste

  • 4 shallots, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, white parts sliced
  • 6 dried red chiles, soaked 15 minutes and drained
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp shrimp paste
  • 4 candlenuts or macadamia nuts
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil

Soup

  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 1/4 lb (560 g) chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 4 kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
  • 4 cups (1 L) chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp salt

Noodles and Toppings

  • 8 oz (225 g) rice vermicelli noodles
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • 8 fried tofu puffs, halved
  • 4 soft-boiled eggs, halved
  • 1/2 cup cilantro leaves
  • 1/2 cup mint leaves
  • 1 red chile, thinly sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Blend the shallots, garlic, ginger, lemongrass, soaked chiles, turmeric, shrimp paste, candlenuts, and oil into a smooth paste.

Step 2

Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large pot and fry the paste 2 to 3 minutes until deeply fragrant.

Step 3

Add the sliced chicken and kaffir lime leaves; stir-fry 2 minutes until the edges turn opaque.

Step 4

Pour in 400 ml coconut milk and 4 cups chicken stock; simmer gently 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

Step 5

Stir in the fish sauce, lime juice, brown sugar, and salt; simmer 1 more minute.

Step 6

Soak 8 oz rice vermicelli 8 minutes in hot water, drain, and blanch 2 cups bean sprouts 30 seconds.

Step 7

Divide the noodles and sprouts among 4 bowls and ladle over the hot laksa broth.

Step 8

Top with tofu puffs, halved eggs, herbs, chile, and lime wedges; serve steaming hot.
