Japanese Beef Udon Noodle Soup

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 6 cups dashi stock
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp sake
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 lb thinly sliced beef (ribeye or sirloin)

Ingredients

  • 4 packs udon noodles (about 800g total)
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Chili oil, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Boil 4 eggs for 6 1/2 minutes, chill in ice water, then peel and halve them.

Step 2

Simmer 6 cups dashi with 1/4 cup soy sauce, 2 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp sake and 1 tsp sugar for 5 minutes.

Step 3

Add 1 sliced onion and 1 lb thinly sliced beef, simmering 3-4 minutes until just cooked.

Step 4

Boil 4 packs of udon noodles for 2-3 minutes, then drain well.

Step 5

Divide the drained noodles evenly among 4 deep bowls.

Step 6

Ladle the hot broth, beef and onion generously over each bowl of noodles.

Step 7

Top each bowl with a halved soft egg, sliced green onions and toasted sesame seeds.

Step 8

Serve hot right away with chopsticks and a drizzle of chili oil if you like heat.
