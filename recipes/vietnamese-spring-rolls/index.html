Vietnamese Fresh Spring Rolls

Vietnam cuisine · Serves 4 (makes 12 rolls) · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

ROLLS

  • 12 rice paper wrappers (8.5 inch)
  • 4 oz rice vermicelli noodles
  • 12 large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 cup julienned cucumber
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrot
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 cup Thai basil

NUOC CHAM SAUCE

  • 1/4 cup fish sauce
  • 1/4 cup fresh lime juice
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 Thai chili, sliced (optional)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the rice noodles 4 to 5 minutes until tender, then rinse under cold water.

Step 2

Add the shrimp to the boiling water and cook 2 to 3 minutes until pink, then slice each one in half lengthwise.

Step 3

Whisk together the fish sauce, lime juice, water, sugar, minced garlic, and sliced chili to make the nuoc cham.

Step 4

Dip one rice paper in warm water for 5 seconds, then lay it flat on a damp kitchen towel.

Step 5

Layer lettuce, a small handful of noodles, mint, cilantro, basil, carrot, and cucumber across the lower third.

Step 6

Fold the bottom edge snugly over the filling, then fold in the left and right sides.

Step 7

Lay 3 shrimp halves cut side down in a row and roll the wrapper up tightly into a cylinder.

Step 8

Serve the 12 spring rolls whole with the nuoc cham for dipping.
