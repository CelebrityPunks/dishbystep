Easy Australian Lamingtons

Australia cuisine · Serves 16 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chocolate Icing

  • 1 1/2 cups (240g) pure icing sugar
  • 1/4 cup (25g) cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 2 tbsp boiling water
  • 2 cups (140g) desiccated coconut

Vanilla Sponge

  • 3 eggs, at room temperature
  • 1/2 cup (110g) caster sugar
  • 1/3 cup (50g) self-raising flour
  • 1/4 cup (35g) cornflour

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Beat 3 eggs and 1/2 cup caster sugar on high for 8-10 min until thick and ribbon-like.

Step 2

Sift 1/3 cup self-raising flour with 1/4 cup cornflour three times, then gently fold into the egg mixture.

Step 3

Pour into a lined 20cm square pan and bake at 180°C for 15-18 min until it springs back.

Step 4

Cool completely, then cut the sponge into 16 even squares with a serrated knife.

Step 5

Whisk 1 1/2 cups icing sugar, 1/4 cup cocoa, 2 tbsp milk and 2 tbsp boiling water until smooth.

Step 6

Dip each square in the chocolate icing with two forks, letting the excess drip off.

Step 7

Roll the coated squares in 2 cups coconut, then set on a wire rack for 15 minutes.

Step 8

Stack the lamingtons on a plate and serve at room temperature.
