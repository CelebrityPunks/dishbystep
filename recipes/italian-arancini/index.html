Crispy Golden Arancini

Italy cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Risotto base

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped (150g)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 350g arborio rice (1 3/4 cups)
  • 120ml dry white wine (1/2 cup)
  • 1.2L warm chicken stock (5 cups)
  • 40g grated Parmesan (1/2 cup)
  • 30g butter (2 tbsp)
  • salt and black pepper

Filling and coating

  • 170g fresh mozzarella (6 oz), cut into small cubes
  • 65g all-purpose flour (1/2 cup)
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 120g Italian breadcrumbs (1 1/2 cups)

To fry and serve

  • vegetable oil, for deep frying
  • 360g warm marinara sauce (1 1/2 cups), for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soften the onion and garlic in 2 tbsp olive oil over medium heat for 4 minutes until translucent.

Step 2

Add the 350g arborio rice and stir for 2 minutes to toast, then pour in the 120ml white wine.

Step 3

Add the warm stock one ladle at a time, stirring often, for about 20 minutes until creamy and tender.

Step 4

Stir in the Parmesan and butter, season with salt and pepper, then chill the risotto at least 2 hours.

Step 5

Scoop the cold risotto, tuck a mozzarella cube into the center of each scoop, and roll into balls.

Step 6

Coat each ball in flour, dip into the 2 beaten eggs, then roll firmly in the breadcrumbs.

Step 7

Deep fry in 350F (175C) oil for 3 to 4 minutes until deep golden all over.

Step 8

Drain on a rack and serve hot with the warm marinara sauce for dipping.
