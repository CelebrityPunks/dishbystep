Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 12 oz linguine or spaghetti
  • 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, juiced (about 2 tbsp)
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Boil 12 oz linguine in salted water until al dente, about 9 minutes, reserving 1 cup pasta water.

Step 2

Season 1 lb shrimp with 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper and 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes.

Step 3

Sear the shrimp in 2 tbsp hot olive oil for 1-2 minutes per side until pink, then set aside.

Step 4

Melt 4 tbsp butter in the same pan and cook 6 minced garlic cloves for 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 5

Pour in 1/2 cup chicken broth and 2 tbsp lemon juice, simmering for 2 minutes.

Step 6

Toss the drained pasta into the sauce with 1/2 cup pasta water until glossy.

Step 7

Return the shrimp and stir in 1/2 cup Parmesan and 1/4 cup parsley.

Step 8

Serve hot with extra Parmesan, parsley and lemon wedges.
