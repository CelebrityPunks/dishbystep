Thai Pad See Ew

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Noodles

  • 400g fresh flat rice noodles (sen yai)
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, for loosening

Sauce

  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar

Stir-fry

  • 350g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 250g Chinese broccoli (gai lan), chopped
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
  • chili flakes, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk the 3 tbsp oyster sauce, 2 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tbsp dark soy sauce and 1 tbsp sugar in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves.

Step 2

Separate the 400g rice noodles into strands and toss with 1 tbsp oil so they do not clump.

Step 3

Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok over very high heat and sear the 350g sliced chicken for 2 to 3 minutes until cooked through, then set aside.

Step 4

Add the last 1 tbsp oil and the 3 minced garlic cloves, stir for 20 seconds, then scramble the 2 beaten eggs for 30 seconds.

Step 5

Toss in the Chinese broccoli stems and stir-fry for 1 minute until bright green.

Step 6

Add the noodles and all the sauce, tossing for 2 to 3 minutes until the edges char and the noodles turn glossy.

Step 7

Return the chicken with the broccoli leaves and toss for 1 more minute until the leaves wilt.

Step 8

Slide onto warm plates, dust with the white pepper and chili flakes, and serve immediately.
