Japanese Takoyaki

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

THE BATTER

  • 500ml dashi stock, cooled
  • 1 medium egg
  • 100g cake flour (or all-purpose)
  • 1 tsp tsuyu sauce (or soy sauce)

THE FILLING

  • 120g boiled octopus, cut into small pieces
  • 4 tbsp red pickled ginger (beni shoga), chopped
  • 50g green onion, chopped
  • 30g tempura flakes (tenkasu)
  • Sesame oil, for the pan

TOPPINGS

  • Takoyaki sauce, to taste
  • Japanese mayonnaise, to taste
  • Aonori (dried green laver), to sprinkle
  • Katsuobushi (bonito flakes), to sprinkle

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 500ml cooled dashi with the egg, 100g flour and 1 tsp tsuyu sauce until smooth, then rest 10 minutes.

Step 2

Dice 120g boiled octopus and chop the green onion and beni shoga, then measure out 30g tenkasu.

Step 3

Brush a takoyaki pan generously with sesame oil and heat it over medium heat for 2 minutes.

Step 4

Fill each cavity to the brim with batter, then drop in the octopus, ginger, onion and tenkasu.

Step 5

After 2 to 3 minutes, cut around each ball with a skewer and turn it 90 degrees.

Step 6

Keep turning the balls every 30 seconds for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and round.

Step 7

Lift the golden balls from the pan and pile them onto a serving plate.

Step 8

Drizzle with takoyaki sauce and Japanese mayonnaise, shower with aonori and dancing bonito flakes, and serve piping hot.
