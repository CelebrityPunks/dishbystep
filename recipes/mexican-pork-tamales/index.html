Pork Tamales

Mexico cuisine · Serves 6 (makes about 12 tamales) · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For the pork filling

  • 1.5 lb pork shoulder
  • 1 white onion (half for braising, half for the sauce)
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 8 dried guajillo chiles
  • 4 dried ancho chiles
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
  • 2 cups pork broth or water
  • salt to taste

For the masa

  • 4 cups masa harina
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp fine salt
  • 1 1/3 cups pork lard (or vegetable shortening), warmed
  • 4 cups warm pork broth

For wrapping and serving

  • 1 package (about 24) dried corn husks
  • optional garnish: sour cream, crumbled cotija, chopped cilantro, lime wedges (no fixed quantity)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 24 dried corn husks in very hot water for 30 minutes until pliable.

Step 2

Toast 8 guajillos and 4 anchos 30 seconds per side, then soak in hot water 20 minutes.

Step 3

Blend the soaked chiles with 1/2 onion, 4 garlic cloves, cumin, oregano, 2 cups broth, and salt until smooth.

Step 4

Simmer 1.5 lb pork shoulder in the red sauce 1.5 to 2 hours until it shreds easily.

Step 5

Beat 4 cups masa harina with baking powder, salt, 1 1/3 cups warm lard and 4 cups warm broth into a soft dough.

Step 6

Spread 1/4 cup masa on each husk, add 2 tbsp pork filling, and fold into a packet.

Step 7

Stand the tamales upright in a steamer over 2 inches of boiling water.

Step 8

Steam 1 hour 15 minutes until the masa pulls cleanly away from the husk. Serve with sour cream, cotija, cilantro, and lime.
