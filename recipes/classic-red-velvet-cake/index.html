Classic Red Velvet Cake

United States cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cake

  • 2 1/2 cups cake flour
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk, room temperature
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp red gel food coloring

Cream Cheese Frosting

  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 cups powdered sugar, sifted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 1/2 cups cake flour, 1 1/2 cups sugar, 2 tbsp cocoa, 1 tsp baking soda, and 1 tsp salt.

Step 2

Whisk 1 cup buttermilk, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup oil, 1 tbsp vinegar, 2 tsp vanilla, and red coloring.

Step 3

Pour the wet mix into the dry and whisk just until smooth.

Step 4

Divide into three 8-inch parchment-lined pans and bake at 350°F for 22-25 min.

Step 5

Cool 10 min in the pans, then turn out and cool completely on a rack.

Step 6

Beat 16 oz cream cheese with 1/2 cup butter, 4 cups powdered sugar, and 1 tsp vanilla.

Step 7

Stack the layers with frosting between, then frost the top and sides.

Step 8

Slice and serve the velvety red cake with its creamy frosting.
