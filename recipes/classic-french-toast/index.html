Classic French Toast

France cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp (12 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 pinch kosher salt
  • 8 slices thick-cut brioche (about 1 inch thick)

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp (28 g) unsalted butter, divided
  • Warm maple syrup, to serve
  • Fresh berries, to serve
  • Powdered sugar, to dust

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 4 eggs, 1 cup milk, vanilla, cinnamon, sugar, and salt in a shallow dish.

Step 2

Soak each brioche slice 30 seconds per side until coated but not soggy.

Step 3

Melt 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium heat.

Step 4

Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp at the edges.

Step 5

Repeat with the remaining slices, adding the second tbsp butter.

Step 6

Stack the toast on a warm plate.

Step 7

Pour warm maple syrup over the stack.

Step 8

Top with berries, dust with powdered sugar, and serve right away.
