Thai Green Curry

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Curry

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 tbsp green curry paste
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 cup red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup bamboo shoots, drained
  • 4 makrut lime leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar

To Serve

  • 1/2 cup fresh Thai basil leaves
  • Steamed jasmine rice
  • 1 red Thai chile, sliced (optional)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Slice 1 lb chicken thighs into 1-inch pieces and prep the vegetables.

Step 2

Fry 3 tbsp green curry paste in 1 tbsp oil for 2 minutes until fragrant.

Step 3

Add the chicken and toss to coat, cooking 3 minutes until no longer pink.

Step 4

Pour in 13.5 oz coconut milk, then stir in 2 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tbsp brown sugar.

Step 5

Add 1 cup bell pepper, 1 cup bamboo shoots, and 4 torn lime leaves.

Step 6

Simmer gently for 10 to 12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

Step 7

Steam the jasmine rice and warm your serving bowls.

Step 8

Ladle the curry over rice, top with Thai basil, and serve.
