Spanish Patatas Bravas

Spain cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 14 oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp sherry vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes into 1-inch cubes, keeping them even so they roast at the same rate.

Step 2

Parboil the cubes in well-salted water for 5 to 6 minutes until just tender at the edges, then drain and let them steam dry.

Step 3

Toss with 3 tbsp olive oil and 1 tsp salt, then roast at 425F for 35 to 40 minutes until deep golden and crisp.

Step 4

Soften 1 small chopped onion in 3 tbsp olive oil for 5 minutes, then stir in 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 1/4 tsp cayenne.

Step 5

Add 1 tbsp tomato paste, 14 oz crushed tomatoes, 1 tsp sherry vinegar, 1/2 tsp sugar, and 1/2 tsp salt; simmer 10 minutes until pulpy.

Step 6

Whisk 1/2 cup mayo with 2 grated garlic cloves, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, and 1/4 tsp salt until smooth and creamy.

Step 7

Pile the hot potatoes in a warm bowl and spoon the brava sauce generously over the top.

Step 8

Drizzle with alioli, scatter 2 tbsp chopped parsley, and serve immediately with cocktail sticks.
