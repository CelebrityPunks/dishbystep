Garlic Butter Steak Bites

United States cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin steak
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 4 cups warm mashed potatoes, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 1 1/2 lb sirloin into 1-inch cubes, pat very dry, and season with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper.

Step 2

Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over high heat until just smoking.

Step 3

Sear the steak in a single layer for 2 minutes per side until deep golden brown, then remove it from the pan.

Step 4

Reduce the heat to medium and melt 4 tbsp butter in the same skillet.

Step 5

Add 4 minced garlic cloves and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.

Step 6

Return the steak to the skillet and toss it in the garlic butter for 1 minute.

Step 7

Stir in 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley and rest the steak for 3 minutes.

Step 8

Serve over warm mashed potatoes with the garlic butter spooned on top.
