Silky Italian Gelato

Italy cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Whole milk - 1 1/2 cups (350 ml)
  • Heavy cream - 1 cup (250 ml)
  • Large egg yolks - 4
  • Granulated sugar - 3/4 cup (150 g)
  • Pure vanilla extract - 2 tsp
  • Pinch of fine salt

Ingredients

  • Waffle cones
  • Fresh berries
  • Chopped pistachios, optional

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 4 egg yolks with 3/4 cup sugar until pale and thick, about 2 minutes.

Step 2

Warm 1 1/2 cups milk and 1 cup cream in a saucepan until steaming, never boiling.

Step 3

Slowly whisk the hot milk mixture into the yolks, pouring in a thin stream.

Step 4

Return to low heat and stir constantly until it coats a spoon, about 8 to 10 minutes, never boiling.

Step 5

Strain into a bowl, stir in 2 tsp vanilla and a pinch of salt, then chill at least 4 hours or overnight.

Step 6

Churn in an ice cream maker until thick and creamy, about 30 to 40 minutes.

Step 7

Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze at least 2 hours until scoopable.

Step 8

Scoop into chilled bowls or waffle cones and serve with fresh berries.
