Classic S'mores Bars

United States cuisine · Serves 16 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2/3 cup butter, melted

Ingredients

  • 3 milk chocolate bars, broken
  • 10 oz mini marshmallows
  • 1 graham cracker, broken into pieces

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 350F and line a 9x13 inch pan with foil, lightly greased.

Step 2

Stir 2 cups graham crumbs, 1/2 cup powdered sugar and 2/3 cup melted butter until sandy.

Step 3

Press the crumb mixture firmly into the pan in an even layer.

Step 4

Lay the chocolate bars side by side over the crust, trimming to fit.

Step 5

Spread 10 oz mini marshmallows evenly over the chocolate.

Step 6

Sprinkle broken graham pieces over the marshmallows.

Step 7

Bake 12 to 15 minutes until the marshmallows are light golden brown.

Step 8

Cool completely, lift out with foil, and slice into 16 bars.
