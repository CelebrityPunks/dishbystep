Nepalese Chicken Momos

Nepal cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour [250g]
  • 0.5 tsp fine salt
  • 0.75 cup water [180ml]

Filling

  • 1 lb ground chicken [450g]
  • 1 cup finely chopped cabbage [75g]
  • 0.5 cup chopped red onion
  • 0.25 cup chopped green onions
  • 1 tbsp each minced garlic and grated ginger
  • 1 tsp each turmeric, chili powder and garam masala
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter or ghee
  • 0.25 cup water (for juiciness)

Achar

  • 4 medium tomatoes (chopped) [400g]
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 1 tbsp each minced garlic and grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 0.5 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 0.25 cup chopped cilantro
  • 0 salt (to taste)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 cups (250g) flour with 1/2 tsp salt, then knead in 3/4 cup (180ml) water; rest the dough 30 minutes.

Step 2

Mix 1 lb (450g) ground chicken with the cabbage, onion, ginger, garlic, spices and 1/4 cup water for juiciness.

Step 3

Divide the dough into 24 balls and roll each into a 3 to 4 inch wrapper, thicker in the middle.

Step 4

Spoon 1 tbsp filling into each wrapper, pleat the edges and pinch to seal.

Step 5

Oil the steamer basket and arrange the momos with space between them.

Step 6

Simmer 4 chopped tomatoes with the chilies, garlic and ginger for 5 minutes, then blend with 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds and cilantro.

Step 7

Steam over a rolling boil for 10 to 12 minutes until the wrappers turn translucent.

Step 8

Serve steaming hot with the spicy tomato sesame achar for dipping.
