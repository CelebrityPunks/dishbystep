Hawaiian Poke Bowl

Hawaii cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Poke

  • 1 1/2 lb sushi-grade ahi tuna
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce (shoyu)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp sriracha
  • 1/2 sweet onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp Hawaiian sea salt

Rice + Toppings

  • 1 1/2 cups short-grain rice (makes about 3 cups cooked)
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup shelled edamame
  • 2 tbsp chopped macadamia nuts

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cook 1 1/2 cups rinsed short-grain rice with 2 cups water for 15 minutes, then rest 10 minutes.

Step 2

Fold 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, and 1/2 tsp salt into the warm rice.

Step 3

Cut 1 1/2 lb sushi-grade ahi tuna into 3/4 inch cubes and chill.

Step 4

Whisk 1/4 cup soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp sriracha, and 1 tsp Hawaiian sea salt.

Step 5

Toss the tuna with the sauce, 1/2 sliced sweet onion, and 2 sliced green onions; chill 15 minutes.

Step 6

Dice 1 avocado, slice 1 cucumber, julienne 1 carrot, and warm 1 cup edamame.

Step 7

Spoon the seasoned rice into bowls and arrange the poke and toppings on top.

Step 8

Sprinkle with 1 tbsp sesame seeds, extra green onions, and 2 tbsp chopped macadamia nuts; serve immediately.
