Chole Bhature

India cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 1 black tea bag
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 to 3 green chilies, slit
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 tbsp chole masala
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp amchur
  • salt and water, as needed

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup semolina (sooji)
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil, plus more for deep frying
  • warm water, as needed

Ingredients

  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced, for serving
  • chopped cilantro, for serving
  • lemon wedges, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 1 cup dried chickpeas in plenty of water overnight, then simmer with 1 tea bag and salt 25 to 30 min until tender; discard the bag.

Step 2

Knead 2 cups flour, 1/4 cup semolina, 1/2 cup yogurt, 1 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1/4 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt, and 2 tbsp oil; rest 2 hours.

Step 3

Sizzle 1 tsp cumin seeds and 1 bay leaf in 2 tbsp hot oil, add 2 chopped onions, and cook 6 min until golden.

Step 4

Add 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste and 2 slit green chilies, then 2 pureed tomatoes; cook 7 min until the oil separates.

Step 5

Stir in 2 tbsp chole masala, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp chili powder, 1/2 tsp amchur, then the chickpeas; simmer 15 min.

Step 6

Roll the rested dough into 6-inch discs.

Step 7

Deep-fry the discs in 350 F oil 30 sec per side until puffed and golden.

Step 8

Serve the chole hot with sliced onion, cilantro, and lemon, plus warm bhature on the side.
