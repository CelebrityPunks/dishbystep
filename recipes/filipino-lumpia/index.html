Crispy Filipino Lumpia

Philippines cuisine · Makes 24 rolls · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For the filling

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled and finely chopped
  • 1 cup minced carrots and 1/2 cup minced onion
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper

For rolling and serving

  • 24 square lumpia wrappers
  • 1 large egg, beaten, for sealing
  • 4 cups cooking oil, for frying
  • 1/2 cup sweet chili sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar and 2 sliced green onions

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Combine 1 lb ground pork, 1/2 lb chopped shrimp, the minced vegetables, garlic, 2 tbsp soy sauce and the seasonings.

Step 2

Spoon 2 tbsp filling near one corner of each wrapper and shape it into a tight log.

Step 3

Fold the sides in, roll tight, and brush the far edge with beaten egg to seal.

Step 4

Heat 4 cups oil in a deep pan to 350F over medium-high heat.

Step 5

Fry the lumpia in batches for 3 to 4 minutes, turning once, until deep golden.

Step 6

Lift out with tongs and drain on a wire rack for 2 minutes.

Step 7

Stack the lumpia on a platter with a bowl of sweet chili sauce.

Step 8

Serve hot and crunchy with extra sauce for dipping.
