Cantonese Char Siu

China cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lb (1.1 kg) pork shoulder (pork butt), trimmed of excess fat

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup hoisin sauce
  • 1/4 cup light soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tbsp honey and 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp Chinese five-spice powder
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp reserved marinade
  • steamed jasmine rice, to serve
  • 2 sliced scallions, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Trim excess fat and cut 2 1/2 lb pork shoulder into 4 to 5 long strips, about 2 inches thick.

Step 2

Whisk the hoisin sauce, soy sauce, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, honey, sugar, sesame oil, five-spice, white pepper and minced garlic into a smooth marinade.

Step 3

Coat the pork strips in the marinade, cover, and refrigerate 8 hours or overnight, turning once.

Step 4

Heat the oven to 400 F (200 C); line a rimmed sheet pan with foil, set a wire rack on top, and arrange the pork strips on the rack.

Step 5

Roast 20 minutes, brushing once with the marinade halfway through.

Step 6

Flip the strips, brush again with the marinade, and roast 15 to 20 more minutes until the edges char and the thickest part reaches 165 F.

Step 7

Simmer 3 tbsp honey with 2 tbsp reserved marinade for 1 minute, brush it over the pork, and broil 2 to 3 minutes per side until glossy and lightly charred.

Step 8

Rest 10 minutes, slice thin against the grain, and serve over steamed jasmine rice with scallions and extra glaze.
