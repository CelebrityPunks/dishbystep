Classic Tomato Bruschetta

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf ciabatta (about 12 oz)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, for brushing
  • 1 garlic clove, halved

Ingredients

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, diced (about 2 lb)
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

  • Flaky sea salt, to taste
  • Extra basil leaves, for garnish
  • Extra virgin olive oil, for drizzling

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Dice 4 large ripe tomatoes and place them in a large bowl.

Step 2

Add 1/4 cup torn basil, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, 1 minced garlic clove, 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp pepper, and toss gently.

Step 3

Slice 1 loaf ciabatta into 8 thick slices, about 3/4 inch each.

Step 4

Brush both sides of the slices with 3 tbsp olive oil and grill or toast for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and crisp.

Step 5

Rub the cut side of 1 halved garlic clove over the hot toasted bread.

Step 6

Spoon the tomato topping generously over each slice, letting the juices soak in lightly.

Step 7

Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil, scatter extra basil, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.

Step 8

Serve the bruschetta immediately while the bread is still crisp.
