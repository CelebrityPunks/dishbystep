Japanese Strawberry Daifuku

Japan cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Mochi dough

  • 120 g (1 cup) shiratamako (sweet glutinous rice flour)
  • 30 g (2 1/2 tbsp) granulated sugar
  • 180 ml (3/4 cup) water
  • 3 tbsp potato starch (katakuriko), for dusting

Filling

  • 240 g (about 1 1/2 cups) sweet red bean paste (anko), 30 g per piece
  • 8 large fresh strawberries, washed and hulled

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 120 g shiratamako, 30 g sugar and 180 ml water until smooth.

Step 2

Cook the mochi mixture 3-4 min, stirring halfway, until translucent and stretchy.

Step 3

Dust the counter with 3 tbsp potato starch and turn the hot mochi out onto it.

Step 4

Flatten 30 g red bean paste into a disc and wrap it around each strawberry.

Step 5

Divide the mochi dough into 8 equal pieces with a starch-dusted scraper.

Step 6

Stretch one mochi piece thin and set an anko-wrapped strawberry in the center.

Step 7

Pull the mochi up and around the filling, pinch firmly to seal.

Step 8

Roll each daifuku in potato starch, cut one open, and serve fresh.
