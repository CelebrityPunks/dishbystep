Classic Pavlova

Australia cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cream And Topping

  • 600ml double cream
  • 50g icing sugar, sifted
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 200g strawberries, hulled and quartered
  • 300g raspberries
  • 200g blueberries
  • 50g pomegranate seeds
  • A few mint leaves, to decorate (optional)

Meringue

  • 6 large egg whites, at room temperature
  • 350g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Beat 6 egg whites in a clean bowl until they hold stiff peaks.

Step 2

Add 350g caster sugar a spoonful at a time, beating until thick and glossy.

Step 3

Gently fold in 1 tsp cornflour and 1 tsp white wine vinegar.

Step 4

Spoon onto a lined tray and shape into an 8 inch nest with a shallow dip.

Step 5

Bake at 150C for 1 hour, then switch off and cool fully in the oven.

Step 6

Whip 600ml double cream with 50g icing sugar and 1 tsp vanilla until soft peaks.

Step 7

Spoon the whipped cream into the cooled meringue nest.

Step 8

Top with the berries and pomegranate, add mint, slice and serve.
