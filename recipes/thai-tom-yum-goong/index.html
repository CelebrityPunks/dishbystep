Thai Tom Yum Goong

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 lb large shrimp (21 to 25 count), peeled and deveined, shells reserved
  • 5 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt

Ingredients

  • 4 lemongrass stalks, tough outer leaves removed, bruised and cut into 3-inch pieces
  • 1-inch piece fresh galangal, sliced
  • 8 kaffir lime leaves, torn
  • 6 Thai bird's eye chilies, bruised

Ingredients

  • 8 oz oyster mushrooms, halved
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp Thai roasted chili paste (nam prik pao)
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro leaves
  • Steamed jasmine rice

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Simmer the shrimp shells in 5 cups water with 1 tsp salt for 15 minutes, then strain for a rich stock.

Step 2

Bring the stock to a boil with the lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves; simmer 5 minutes.

Step 3

Add the oyster mushrooms and tomato wedges; cook 2-3 minutes.

Step 4

Stir in 2 tbsp nam prik pao and the 6 Thai chilies; boil 1 minute.

Step 5

Add the shrimp and cook 2 minutes, until pink and curled.

Step 6

Season with 3 tbsp fish sauce and 2 tsp sugar; taste and adjust.

Step 7

Take off the heat and stir in 1/4 cup fresh lime juice.

Step 8

Ladle into bowls, scatter with cilantro, and serve with steamed jasmine rice.
