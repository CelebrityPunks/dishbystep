Classic Potato Gnocchi

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 1 tsp fine salt

Ingredients

  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 12 fresh sage leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus more to serve
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Boil 2 lb russet potatoes in salted water for 25 to 30 minutes until very tender, then drain well.

Step 2

Peel the warm potatoes, rice them onto a clean surface, and let cool for 5 minutes.

Step 3

Add 1 beaten egg, 1 tsp salt and 1 1/2 cups flour and gently knead into a soft dough.

Step 4

Roll the dough into ropes, cut into 1 inch pieces, and press each lightly with a fork.

Step 5

Boil the gnocchi in salted water in batches for 2 to 3 minutes until they float.

Step 6

Melt 6 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium heat until golden and nutty, about 3 minutes.

Step 7

Add 12 sage leaves and 2 sliced garlic cloves and cook 1 minute until crisp and fragrant.

Step 8

Toss the drained gnocchi in the brown butter with 1/2 cup Parmesan and 1/4 tsp black pepper, and serve hot.
