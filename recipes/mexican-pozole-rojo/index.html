Mexican Pozole Rojo

Mexico cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless pork shoulder, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 white onion, halved
  • 1 whole garlic head, halved crosswise
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 10 cups water

Ingredients

  • 4 dried guajillo chiles, stems and seeds removed
  • 2 dried ancho chiles, stems and seeds removed
  • 2 dried chiles de arbol
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin

Ingredients

  • 2 (28-oz) cans white hominy, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 6 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Dried Mexican oregano, to taste
  • Tostadas, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Add 2 lb pork shoulder chunks, halved onion, garlic head, bay leaves, 2 tsp salt, and 10 cups water to a large pot. Boil, skim the foam, then simmer 90 minutes until fork-tender.

Step 2

Toast 4 guajillo, 2 ancho, and 2 chile de arbol chiles in a dry skillet over medium heat, 30 seconds per side, until fragrant.

Step 3

Soak the toasted chiles in 2 cups of the hot pork broth for 20 minutes until soft.

Step 4

Blend the softened chiles with 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp Mexican oregano, 1/2 tsp cumin, and 1 cup of the soaking liquid until silky smooth.

Step 5

Strain the red sauce through a fine sieve back into the pot, pressing the pulp to extract every drop.

Step 6

Stir in 2 cans of drained hominy and simmer 20 minutes more so the flavors marry. Season with salt to taste.

Step 7

Shred the tender pork into bite-size pieces and stir it back into the pot.

Step 8

Ladle into bowls and top with shredded cabbage, radishes, onion, cilantro, lime wedges, and a pinch of oregano.
