Classic French Coq au Vin

France cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skinless chicken thighs (2 1/2 lb)
  • 4 oz bacon lardons

Ingredients

  • 12 pearl onions (8 oz), peeled
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, quartered
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups dry red wine (Burgundy)
  • 1/4 cup Cognac
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 thyme sprigs, 2 bay leaves and parsley stems (bouquet garni)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, to finish

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Render the bacon lardons in a Dutch oven until crisp, 6-8 minutes, then lift them out.

Step 2

Brown the chicken thighs in the bacon fat, 5-6 minutes per side, until deeply golden, then set aside.

Step 3

Sauté the pearl onions and mushrooms in the same pot until golden, 6-8 minutes.

Step 4

Pour in the Cognac to deglaze, then add the red wine, scraping up all the browned bits.

Step 5

Stir in the tomato paste, garlic, flour and chicken stock, tuck in the bouquet garni, and return the chicken.

Step 6

Cover and braise gently 35-40 minutes until the chicken is tender and cooked through.

Step 7

Uncover, stir in the butter and the reserved bacon, and simmer 5 minutes until the sauce turns glossy.

Step 8

Spoon the coq au vin over creamy mashed potatoes and finish with fresh parsley.
