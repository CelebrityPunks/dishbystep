Sichuan Mapo Tofu

China cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Aromatics & Heat

  • 1/2 cup neutral oil, divided
  • 1-2 fresh Thai bird chili peppers, thinly sliced
  • 6-8 dried red chilies, roughly chopped
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns, ground (reserve 1/4 tsp for garnish)
  • 3 tbsp fresh ginger, finely minced
  • 3 tbsp garlic, finely minced

Tofu & Sauce

  • 8 oz ground pork
  • 2 tbsp spicy bean sauce (doubanjiang)
  • 2/3 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 lb silken tofu, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 1/2 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1/4 cup water
  • 1/4 tsp sesame oil (optional)
  • 1/4 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 1 scallion, finely chopped

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toast the fresh and dried chilies in half the oil over low heat until fragrant, about 5 minutes.

Step 2

Fry 3 tbsp ginger and 3 tbsp garlic for 2 minutes, then brown 8 oz ground pork over high heat.

Step 3

Stir in 1 1/2 tbsp ground Sichuan peppercorns for 15-30 seconds. Do not let it burn.

Step 4

Add 2 tbsp spicy bean sauce and 2/3 cup chicken broth, then simmer 1 minute.

Step 5

Whisk 1 1/2 tsp cornstarch with 1/4 cup water, stir it in, and simmer until the sauce thickens.

Step 6

Add the cubed tofu and simmer 3-5 minutes, gently tossing so it soaks up the sauce.

Step 7

Finish with 1/4 tsp sesame oil, 1/4 tsp sugar, and the chopped scallion.

Step 8

Serve over steamed rice with a final pinch of Sichuan pepper on top.
