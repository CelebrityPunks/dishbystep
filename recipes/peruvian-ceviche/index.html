Peruvian Ceviche

Peru cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ceviche

  • 1 lb firm white fish (corvina, sea bass or halibut), cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 1 cup fresh lime juice (about 10 limes)
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 1 to 2 aji amarillo or limo chiles, seeded and minced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro

To serve

  • 1 medium sweet potato, peeled, boiled and sliced
  • 1 cup cooked choclo or sweet corn kernels

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 1 lb firm white fish into even 1/2-inch cubes and place in a glass bowl.

Step 2

Slice 1 small red onion very thin, rinse under cold water, and drain well.

Step 3

Seed and mince 1 to 2 aji chiles and 1 clove garlic, and chop 1/4 cup cilantro.

Step 4

Squeeze 1 cup fresh lime juice, straining out the seeds.

Step 5

Pour the lime juice over the fish and sprinkle with 1/2 tsp salt, then toss gently.

Step 6

Chill 15 to 20 minutes until the fish turns opaque and firms up.

Step 7

Fold in the onion, chiles, garlic, and cilantro, then taste and adjust the salt.

Step 8

Serve in chilled bowls with sweet potato slices and corn kernels.
