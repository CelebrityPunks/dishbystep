Japanese Shrimp Tempura

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Batter And Tentsuyu

  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup cake flour (plus 1/2 cup for dusting) [120g]
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 inches daikon (grated, for serving)
  • 1 cup dashi (Japanese soup stock) [240ml]
  • 0.25 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 cup ice water [240ml]
  • 1 large egg (cold)
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 0.25 cup soy sauce [60ml]
  • 2 tsp sugar

Shrimp And Vegetables

  • 8 fresh shiitake mushrooms (stems removed)
  • 12 large shrimp (peeled and deveined, tails left on)
  • 1 medium sweet potato (cut into 1/4-inch slices)
  • 6 cups neutral oil (for deep frying) [about 1.5 L]
  • 1 small Japanese eggplant (cut into 1/2-inch rounds)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel 12 large shrimp, tails on, and score the undersides. Slice the sweet potato and eggplant; stem 8 shiitakes.

Step 2

Simmer 1 cup dashi with 1/4 cup soy sauce, 3 tbsp mirin, and 2 tsp sugar for 2 minutes, then keep warm.

Step 3

Whisk 1 cold egg into 1 cup ice water. Stir in 1 cup cake flour, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp baking powder, 1/4 tsp salt. Keep it lumpy.

Step 4

Heat 2 to 3 inches of neutral oil in a deep heavy pan to 340-350F.

Step 5

Dust the shrimp and vegetables in 1/2 cup flour, shake off the excess, then dip into the cold batter.

Step 6

Fry in small uncrowded batches for 2 to 3 minutes, until pale gold and crisp.

Step 7

Lift the tempura out with a wire skimmer and drain on a wire rack for 1 minute.

Step 8

Plate the tempura with grated daikon and the warm tentsuyu, and serve right away.
