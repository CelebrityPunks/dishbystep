Marry Me Chicken

United States cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Ingredients

  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • Fresh basil, to serve
  • Cooked pasta or rice, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Season 1 1/2 lb chicken with salt, pepper and garlic powder, then lightly coat in 1/4 cup flour.

Step 2

Sear the chicken in 2 tbsp hot olive oil for 5-6 minutes per side until golden, then set aside.

Step 3

Cook 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tsp Italian seasoning and 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes for 1 minute.

Step 4

Pour in 1 cup heavy cream and 1/2 cup chicken broth, scraping up the browned bits.

Step 5

Stir in 1/2 cup Parmesan and 1/2 cup sliced sun-dried tomatoes until the sauce thickens slightly.

Step 6

Return the chicken to the pan and simmer on low for 8-10 minutes until it reaches 165 degrees.

Step 7

Stir in 2 cups baby spinach until just wilted, about 1 minute.

Step 8

Serve hot over pasta or rice, spooning over the creamy sauce and fresh basil.
