Air Fryer Pumpkin Spice Donuts

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice, 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup canned pumpkin puree
  • 1/3 cup neutral oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk, 1 tsp vanilla extract

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 3/4 cups flour, the baking powder, baking soda, pumpkin pie spice, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl.

Step 2

Whisk 1/2 cup pumpkin puree, 3/4 cup sugar, 1/3 cup oil, 2 eggs, 1/4 cup milk, and the vanilla until smooth.

Step 3

Fold the wet mix into the dry mix just until no streaks of flour remain.

Step 4

Pipe the batter into a greased donut pan, filling each mold two-thirds full.

Step 5

Air fry at 350°F for 9 to 10 minutes, until the tops spring back when touched.

Step 6

Cool 3 minutes in the pan, then turn the donuts out onto a rack.

Step 7

Brush the warm donuts with 4 tbsp melted butter, then toss in the cinnamon sugar.

Step 8

Stack them warm with coffee and extra cinnamon sugar. Save this cozy fall bake.
