Ultimate Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup

United States cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves

Ingredients

  • 8 slices sourdough bread
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cook 1 diced onion and 3 minced garlic cloves in 2 tbsp olive oil for 5 minutes until soft.

Step 2

Add 2 cans crushed tomatoes, 2 cups vegetable broth, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper, then simmer for 15 minutes.

Step 3

Blend the soup until silky smooth, then stir in 1/2 cup heavy cream and 1/4 cup basil leaves.

Step 4

Butter 8 slices of sourdough with 4 tbsp softened butter and layer 2 cups cheddar and 1 cup mozzarella inside.

Step 5

Griddle the sandwiches over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp.

Step 6

Cut each sandwich diagonally so the melted cheese pulls apart.

Step 7

Ladle the hot tomato soup into 4 warm bowls.

Step 8

Serve the crispy sandwiches with the soup for dipping.
