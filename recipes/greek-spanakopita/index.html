Greek Spanakopita

Greece cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 box (16 oz), thawed phyllo dough
  • 1/2 cup, plus more for brushing extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 medium, finely chopped yellow onion
  • 3 cloves, minced garlic
  • 1 lb (or 10 oz frozen, thawed and squeezed dry) fresh spinach
  • 1 cup (6 oz), crumbled feta cheese
  • 1/2 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2, beaten large eggs
  • 1/4 cup, chopped fresh dill
  • 1/4 cup, chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 pinch nutmeg

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Saute 1 chopped onion and 3 cloves garlic in 2 tbsp olive oil, 3-4 min until soft.

Step 2

Add 1 lb spinach and wilt 2-3 min, then squeeze out all the liquid.

Step 3

Mix spinach with 1 cup feta, 1/2 cup ricotta, 2 beaten eggs, dill, parsley and nutmeg.

Step 4

Layer 6 phyllo sheets in an oiled 9x13 dish, brushing each with olive oil.

Step 5

Spread the spinach-feta filling evenly over the phyllo base.

Step 6

Top with 6 more phyllo sheets, brushing each with oil, then score into squares.

Step 7

Bake at 375°F for 35-40 min until deep golden and crisp.

Step 8

Rest 10 min, then cut along the scored lines and serve warm.
