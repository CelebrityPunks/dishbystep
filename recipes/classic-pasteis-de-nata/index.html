Classic Pasteis de Nata

Portugal cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Pastry + Finish

  • 1 sheet (9 oz / 250 g) all-butter puff pastry, thawed
  • Ground cinnamon, for dusting

Custard

  • 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk
  • 3/4 cup (180 ml) heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup (30 g) all-purpose flour
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 strip (2 inches) lemon peel
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 pinch (1/8 tsp) fine sea salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Simmer 1 cup milk and 3/4 cup cream with the cinnamon stick and lemon peel for 5 minutes, then steep 10 minutes.

Step 2

Whisk 1/2 cup sugar with 1/4 cup flour, then whisk in a splash of the warm milk into a smooth paste.

Step 3

Whisk everything back into the saucepan and cook 3 to 4 minutes until thick and glossy.

Step 4

Off the heat, whisk in 4 egg yolks and a pinch of salt, then strain the custard smooth.

Step 5

Heat the oven to 500F with a metal muffin tin inside. Cut 12 pastry discs and press into the hot cups.

Step 6

Fill each pastry cup three-quarters full with the warm custard.

Step 7

Bake 12 to 15 minutes until the custard is blistered and deeply caramelized.

Step 8

Serve warm, dusted with ground cinnamon.
