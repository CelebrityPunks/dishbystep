Classic Italian Calzone

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 1.5 fine salt
  • 7 g instant yeast [2 1/4 tsp]
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 325 ml warm water [1 1/3 cups]

Filling

  • 0.5 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 50 g grated Parmesan
  • 200 g shredded low-moisture mozzarella
  • 100 g sliced pepperoni
  • 250 g whole-milk ricotta

Flour

  • amount: 500
  • unit: g

To Finish

  • 240 ml marinara sauce (warmed, for dipping) [1 cup]
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (for brushing)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Stir 500g flour, 7g yeast, 1 tsp sugar and 1 1/2 tsp salt, then mix in 325ml warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil until a shaggy dough forms.

Step 2

Knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and elastic, then rise in an oiled bowl for 1 hour until doubled.

Step 3

Divide the dough into 4 pieces and roll each into a 25cm (10 inch) circle on a floured surface.

Step 4

Spread 60g ricotta on one half of each circle, then add 50g mozzarella, 12g Parmesan, pepperoni and a pinch of oregano.

Step 5

Fold the bare half over the filling and crimp the edges firmly to seal, pressing out any air pockets.

Step 6

Brush each calzone with olive oil, cut two steam vents, and bake at 230C (450F) for 15 to 18 minutes until deeply golden.

Step 7

Let the golden calzones rest 5 minutes so the molten cheese sets before slicing.

Step 8

Slice and serve warm with 1 cup of warm marinara sauce for dipping.
