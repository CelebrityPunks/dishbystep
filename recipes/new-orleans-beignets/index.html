New Orleans Beignets

United States cuisine · Serves 24 beignets (serves 6) · 6 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 3/4 cup warm water (110°F)
  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast (1 envelope)
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Frying and Finish

  • 4 cups vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 cups powdered sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Stir 3/4 cup warm water, 2 1/4 tsp yeast and 1/2 cup sugar; rest 10 minutes until foamy.

Step 2

Whisk in 2 tbsp oil, 1/2 cup evaporated milk and the beaten egg; stir in 3 1/2 cups flour and 1/2 tsp salt into a soft dough.

Step 3

Knead 5 minutes, roll 1/4 inch thick, and cut into 2 1/2 inch squares.

Step 4

Heat 4 cups oil to 350 F; fry the squares 2 to 3 minutes, flipping once, until deep golden and puffed.

Step 5

Drain on a rack, then shower with 2 cups powdered sugar.

Step 6

Serve warm with strong coffee.
