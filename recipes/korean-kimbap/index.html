Korean Kimbap

South Korea cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

RICE

  • 2 cups cooked short-grain white rice (warm)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

FILLINGS

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt, divided
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, plus 1 tsp for spinach
  • 8 oz fresh baby spinach
  • 2 medium carrots, julienned
  • 8 strips danmuji (pickled daikon radish)
  • 8 imitation crab sticks

TO ROLL

  • 4 full-size sheets roasted gim (dried seaweed)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil, for brushing

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cook 2 cups short-grain rice, then toss while warm with 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1/2 tsp salt and 1 tbsp sesame seeds.

Step 2

Beat 4 eggs with a pinch of salt, cook into a thin omelet over medium heat, and slice into long strips.

Step 3

Blanch 8 oz spinach 1 minute, squeeze very dry, and season with 1 tsp sesame oil and a pinch of salt.

Step 4

Saute 2 julienned carrots in 1 tbsp neutral oil with a pinch of salt for 2 minutes until just tender.

Step 5

Lay 1 gim sheet shiny side down and spread 3/4 cup seasoned rice thinly, leaving 1 inch bare at the top edge.

Step 6

Line egg strips, spinach, carrots, 2 pickled radish strips and 2 crab sticks across the lower third.

Step 7

Roll tightly with a bamboo mat, pressing and tucking as you go, and seal the bare edge with a dab of water.

Step 8

Brush each roll with sesame oil, slice into 10 to 12 bite size pieces, and fan them cut side up.
