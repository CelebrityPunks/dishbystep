Classic American BBQ Ribs

United States cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 racks baby back pork ribs (about 4 to 5 lb total)
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp coarse black pepper
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp dry mustard powder
  • 1 tsp chili flakes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup apple juice
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, cut into pats

Ingredients

  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel the thin membrane off the back of each rack with a paper towel, then trim any excess fat so the rub can soak in.

Step 2

Slather the ribs with 2 tbsp mustard and coat both sides generously with the dry rub, then rest 30 minutes at room temperature.

Step 3

Wrap each rack tightly in foil with 1/4 cup apple juice and 1 tbsp butter, then bake at 275F for 2 1/2 to 3 hours.

Step 4

While the ribs bake, simmer all the sauce ingredients for 10 minutes until thick and glossy.

Step 5

Unwrap the ribs, brush a thick layer of BBQ sauce over both sides, and save the rest for serving.

Step 6

Return the ribs to a 400F oven for 20 minutes until the sauce caramelizes and turns sticky.

Step 7

Broil 3 to 5 minutes for charred edges, then rest the ribs 10 minutes so the juices settle.

Step 8

Slice between the bones and serve hot with the extra BBQ sauce for dipping.
