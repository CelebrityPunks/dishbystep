Tres Leches Cake

Mexico cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 5 large eggs, separated
  • 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar, divided
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Milk soak

  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) heavy cream

Topping

  • 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) heavy whipping cream, cold
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Fresh strawberries, for serving
  • Ground cinnamon, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 cup flour, 1 1/2 tsp baking powder and 1/4 tsp salt in a large bowl.

Step 2

Beat 5 egg yolks with 3/4 cup sugar until pale and thick, then stir in 1/3 cup milk and 1 tsp vanilla.

Step 3

Whip 5 egg whites with the remaining 1/4 cup sugar to stiff, glossy peaks.

Step 4

Fold the whites into the batter in three additions until just combined.

Step 5

Spread into a greased 9x13 pan and bake at 350°F for 25-30 minutes until golden.

Step 6

Poke holes all over the warm cake with a fork.

Step 7

Whisk the evaporated milk, condensed milk and 1/4 cup cream, pour it over the cake, then chill at least 4 hours.

Step 8

Whip 1 1/2 cups cream, 3 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp vanilla. Spread over the cake, add strawberries and cinnamon, then slice to serve.
