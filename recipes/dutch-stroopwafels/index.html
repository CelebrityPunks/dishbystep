Dutch Stroopwafels

Netherlands cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Caramel Filling

  • 1 1/2 cups packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 6 tbsp dark corn syrup

Waffle Dough

  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 (1/4 oz) packet active dry yeast
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Dissolve 1 packet yeast in 1/2 cup warm water, then mix in 4 cups flour, 1/2 cup sugar, 2 eggs, and 1 cup melted butter.

Step 2

Knead the dough until smooth, cover, and let it rise for 1 hour until doubled.

Step 3

Divide the dough into 12 balls and press one in a hot waffle iron until golden, about 1 minute.

Step 4

Slice the hot waffle horizontally into two thin rounds with a sharp knife.

Step 5

Boil 1 1/2 cups brown sugar, 1 cup butter, 1 tsp cinnamon, and 6 tbsp dark corn syrup to 234 to 240F.

Step 6

Spread the warm caramel over the bottom waffle half while it is still warm.

Step 7

Press the top waffle half over the caramel and cool 15 minutes until set.

Step 8

Serve over a warm cup of coffee so the caramel turns soft and gooey.
