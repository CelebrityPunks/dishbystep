Jamaican Beef Patties

Jamaica cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Flaky Turmeric Crust

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp Jamaican curry powder
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 cup cold unsalted butter, cut in cubes
  • 1/2 cup ice-cold water
  • 1 large egg, beaten, for brushing

Spiced Beef Filling

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20)
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper, seeded and minced
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tsp Jamaican curry powder

Seasonings

  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup beef broth
  • 1/3 cup plain breadcrumbs

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub the cold butter into the flour, turmeric, curry powder, and salt, then stir in the ice water to make a firm dough.

Step 2

Wrap the dough and chill it in the fridge for 30 minutes.

Step 3

Brown 1 lb ground beef in hot oil with the diced onion until no pink remains, about 6 minutes.

Step 4

Stir in the garlic, scotch bonnet, green onions, curry powder, thyme, allspice, paprika, salt, and pepper.

Step 5

Pour in 1/2 cup beef broth and simmer 5 minutes, then stir in the breadcrumbs until thick. Cool completely.

Step 6

Roll the dough 1/8 inch thick and cut 6-inch circles. Spoon 2 tbsp filling on one half of each.

Step 7

Fold over, seal the edges with a fork, brush with beaten egg, and bake at 375°F for 25 to 30 minutes.

Step 8

Cool 5 minutes on the pan. Serve warm and watch them disappear.
