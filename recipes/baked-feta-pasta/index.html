Baked Feta Pasta

Finland cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 10 oz short pasta, such as rigatoni
  • 2 pints cherry tomatoes, about 4 cups
  • 1 block (8 oz) feta cheese
  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water, as needed
  • Extra olive oil, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 400F and set an 8 oz block of feta in the center of a baking dish.

Step 2

Surround the feta with 2 pints cherry tomatoes, 4 sliced garlic cloves, 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes and 1/2 cup olive oil.

Step 3

Season with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper, then toss the tomatoes in the oil.

Step 4

Bake at 400F for 30 to 35 minutes until the tomatoes burst and the feta turns golden.

Step 5

Meanwhile, boil 10 oz rigatoni in salted water until just al dente, saving 1/2 cup pasta water.

Step 6

Mash the hot feta and tomatoes with a fork into a creamy, silky sauce.

Step 7

Toss in the drained pasta with splashes of reserved pasta water until glossy and coated.

Step 8

Serve topped with torn basil and a drizzle of olive oil.
