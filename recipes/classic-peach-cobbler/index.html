Classic Peach Cobbler

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 ripe peaches, peeled and sliced, about 3 lb
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2/3 cup whole milk
  • 1 tbsp coarse sugar, for sprinkling
  • Vanilla ice cream, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel 8 ripe peaches and slice them into a large bowl.

Step 2

Toss the peaches with 3/4 cup sugar, 1/4 cup brown sugar, 1 tbsp cornstarch, lemon juice, vanilla and cinnamon, then rest 15 minutes.

Step 3

Whisk 1 1/2 cups flour, 1/2 cup sugar, 2 tsp baking powder and 1/2 tsp salt in a bowl.

Step 4

Cut in 1/2 cup cold cubed butter until pea-sized crumbs form.

Step 5

Stir in 2/3 cup milk just until a soft dough comes together.

Step 6

Spoon the peaches into a buttered baking dish, drop spoonfuls of dough on top and sprinkle with 1 tbsp coarse sugar.

Step 7

Bake at 375F for 40 to 45 minutes until golden and bubbling, then cool 10 minutes.

Step 8

Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
