Viral Dubai Chocolate

United Arab Emirates cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chocolate Shell

  • 340 g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp coconut oil (optional)
  • 2 tbsp chopped pistachios (optional)

Pistachio Filling

  • 100 g kataifi pastry, thawed and chopped
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 200 g sweetened pistachio cream
  • 1 tbsp tahini
  • Pinch of fine sea salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Melt 2 tbsp butter and toast 100g kataifi 6-8 minutes until deep golden.

Step 2

Stir the cooled kataifi with 200g pistachio cream, 1 tbsp tahini and salt.

Step 3

Melt 2/3 of the 340g chocolate in 20-second bursts, stirring between each.

Step 4

Coat the bar mold with chocolate, tip out the excess, chill 10 minutes.

Step 5

Press the pistachio kataifi filling into the set shell evenly.

Step 6

Melt the remaining chocolate, spread over the filling and smooth flat.

Step 7

Chill 1 hour until firm, then gently pop the bar out of the mold.

Step 8

Rest 10 minutes, snap into pieces, and enjoy the crunch.
