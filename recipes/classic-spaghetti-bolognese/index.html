Classic Spaghetti Bolognese

Italy cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Pasta And Serving

  • 1 lb (450 g) spaghetti
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more to serve
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Salt for the pasta water

The Ragu

  • 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
  • 1/2 lb (225 g) ground pork
  • 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 1 celery stalk, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2/3 cup (150 ml) dry red wine
  • 28 oz (800 g) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soften 1 chopped onion, 1 diced carrot, 1 diced celery, and 3 minced garlic cloves in 2 tbsp olive oil for 8 min.

Step 2

Add 1 lb ground beef and 1/2 lb ground pork; break up and brown for 8-10 min.

Step 3

Pour in 2/3 cup dry red wine and simmer 3 min, scraping the pot.

Step 4

Stir in 28 oz crushed tomatoes, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 2 bay leaves, 1 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper.

Step 5

Cover and simmer on low 1 1/2 to 2 hours, stirring now and then.

Step 6

Cook 1 lb spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until just al dente.

Step 7

Toss the drained spaghetti with the ragu and 3/4 cup Parmesan.

Step 8

Plate with more Parmesan and fresh basil, and serve hot.
