Homemade Turkish Doner Kebab

Turkey cuisine · Serves 6 wraps · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Boneless skinless chicken thighs - 2 lb (900 g)
  • Plain whole-milk yogurt - 1 cup (240 g)
  • Olive oil - 1/4 cup (60 ml)
  • Tomato paste - 2 tbsp
  • Garlic, minced - 4 cloves
  • Ground cumin - 2 tsp
  • Paprika - 2 tsp
  • Dried oregano - 1 tsp
  • Fine salt - 1 tsp
  • Black pepper - 1/2 tsp
  • Cayenne pepper - 1/4 tsp

Ingredients

  • Plain whole-milk yogurt - 1 cup (240 g)
  • Garlic, minced - 2 cloves
  • Lemon juice - 2 tbsp
  • Olive oil - 2 tbsp
  • Fine salt - 1/2 tsp

Ingredients

  • Large pita or flatbreads - 6
  • Red cabbage, thinly sliced - 2 cups
  • Tomato, sliced - 1 large
  • Cucumber, sliced - 1/2
  • Red onion, thinly sliced - 1/2
  • Shredded lettuce - 2 cups

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 cup yogurt, 1/4 cup olive oil, tomato paste, garlic, and all the spices into a smooth marinade.

Step 2

Coat 2 lb chicken thighs in the marinade and refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight.

Step 3

Thread the thighs onto a vertical skewer stuck in a halved onion, packing them into a tight stack.

Step 4

Roast at 400°F for 60 to 70 minutes until the edges are deeply charred.

Step 5

Rest 10 minutes, then shave thin slices off the hot stack.

Step 6

Whisk the yogurt, garlic, lemon, and olive oil into the garlic sauce.

Step 7

Warm the pita, then pile on shaved chicken with cabbage, tomato, cucumber, onion, and lettuce.

Step 8

Drizzle generously with garlic yogurt sauce, roll up, and serve hot.
