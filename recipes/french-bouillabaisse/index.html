French Bouillabaisse

France cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Saffron broth base

  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 4 leeks, white parts only, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 fennel bulb, thinly sliced
  • 6 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp saffron threads
  • 2 tsp orange zest
  • 1 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups dry white wine
  • 6 cups fish stock

Seafood

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish (cod or halibut), cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 3/4 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined

Rouille

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp saffron threads
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste

To serve

  • 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat 1/3 cup olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Soften the onions, leeks, and fennel for 6 minutes until tender.

Step 2

Stir in the garlic, chopped tomatoes, thyme, bay leaves, saffron, and orange zest. Cook 5 minutes, stirring.

Step 3

Pour in 1 1/2 cups white wine and 6 cups fish stock. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 20 minutes.

Step 4

Add the 1 1/2 lb fish chunks. Simmer gently for 5 minutes, until just opaque.

Step 5

Add the mussels and shrimp. Cover and cook 4 minutes, until the mussels open.

Step 6

Whisk the mayonnaise, garlic, lemon juice, saffron, cayenne, and olive oil into a smooth, golden rouille.

Step 7

Slice the baguette and toast until golden and crisp for dunking.

Step 8

Ladle the saffron broth over the seafood, dollop with rouille, and serve with the toasted baguette.
