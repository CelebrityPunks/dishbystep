Chicken Biryani

India cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 lbs bone-in chicken pieces
  • 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt

Layers

  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup ghee
  • 1/2 tsp saffron in 1/4 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro + mint
  • Crispy onions, to serve

Rice

  • 3 cups basmati rice, soaked 30 min
  • 6 cups water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 green cardamom pods
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tsp salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 cup yogurt, 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 3 tbsp lemon juice and all the spices, then coat 2 lbs chicken.

Step 2

Cover and rest the marinated chicken 1 hour, or overnight in the fridge.

Step 3

Boil 3 cups soaked basmati in 6 cups salted water with the whole spices for 5 minutes, then drain.

Step 4

Fry 2 sliced onions in 1/2 cup hot ghee until deep golden and crisp, then lift out.

Step 5

Sear the marinated chicken in the ghee 4 minutes per side until nicely browned.

Step 6

Layer half the rice, all the chicken with its juices, half the crispy onions, then the rest of the rice.

Step 7

Drizzle the saffron milk over the top, cover tightly, and steam on low heat 20 minutes.

Step 8

Fluff gently, shower with crispy onions and herbs, and serve with cool raita.
