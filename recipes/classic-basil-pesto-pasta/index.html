Classic Basil Pesto Pasta

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredient list coming soon.

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Blanch 4 cups basil leaves for 30 seconds, shock in ice water, and squeeze dry.

Step 2

Toast 1/3 cup pine nuts in a dry skillet for 3-4 minutes until golden.

Step 3

Blend the basil, pine nuts, 3 garlic cloves, salt, and pepper with half the oil.

Step 4

Stream in the remaining 1/4 cup olive oil and blend until silky.

Step 5

Stir 1/2 cup grated Parmesan through the pesto by hand.

Step 6

Boil 12 oz spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente; reserve 1 cup pasta water.

Step 7

Toss the hot pasta with the pesto, loosening with splashes of pasta water.

Step 8

Twirl into warm bowls and finish with Parmesan, basil, and a drizzle of olive oil.
