Key Lime Pie

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Graham Crust

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs (about 12 full sheets)
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted

Lime Filling

  • 2 cans (14 oz each) sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup fresh key lime juice (about 12 limes)
  • 1 tbsp key lime zest

Topping

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • Thin lime slices, to garnish

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 1 1/2 cups graham crumbs, 1/3 cup sugar, and 6 tbsp melted butter until sandy.

Step 2

Press firmly into a 9-inch pie dish and bake at 350 F for 8 minutes.

Step 3

Whisk the sweetened condensed milk, 4 egg yolks, 1/2 cup key lime juice, and 1 tbsp zest until smooth.

Step 4

Pour the filling into the warm crust and bake at 350 F for 15 to 17 minutes until just set.

Step 5

Cool 1 hour on the counter, then chill at least 4 hours or overnight.

Step 6

Whip 1 cup heavy cream with 2 tbsp powdered sugar to soft peaks.

Step 7

Spread the whipped cream over the cold pie and top with lime slices.

Step 8

Slice with a hot dry knife and serve ice cold.
