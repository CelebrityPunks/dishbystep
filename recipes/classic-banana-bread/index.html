Classic Banana Bread

United States cuisine · Serves Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Wet

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup sour cream

Dry

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Optional topping

  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 tbsp cinnamon sugar

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Preheat the oven to 350F. Grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan.

Step 2

Whisk 2 cups flour, 1 tsp baking soda and 1/4 tsp salt in a bowl.

Step 3

Cream 1/2 cup softened butter with 1 cup sugar until light and fluffy, 2 minutes.

Step 4

Beat in 2 eggs one at a time, then 1 tsp vanilla, 1/2 cup sour cream and the mashed bananas.

Step 5

Fold the dry mix in just until combined, then fold in 1/2 cup walnuts.

Step 6

Pour into the pan and bake 60 to 70 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

Step 7

Cool 15 minutes in the pan, then turn the loaf out onto a wire rack.

Step 8

Slice thick and serve warm with butter.
