Classic Buttermilk Fried Chicken

United States cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Buttermilk Marinade

  • 4 lb bone-in, skin-on chicken pieces (thighs, drumsticks, breast halves)
  • 3 cups buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Crispy Dredge

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper

To Fry and Finish

  • 8 cups (2 qt) peanut or vegetable oil
  • Flaky sea salt, for sprinkling
  • Dill pickles, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 3 cups buttermilk, 2 tbsp hot sauce, 2 tsp salt and 1 tsp pepper, then soak 4 lb chicken pieces in it for at least 4 hours or overnight.

Step 2

Whisk 2 1/2 cups flour, 1/2 cup cornstarch, 1 tbsp baking powder and all the dredge spices in a wide shallow bowl.

Step 3

Press each piece firmly into the flour, dip back in the buttermilk, then dredge again for a thick craggy crust.

Step 4

Heat 8 cups oil to 340 F in a deep heavy pot. A pinch of dredge should sizzle and rise in 1 second.

Step 5

Fry the dark meat in uncrowded batches, 12 to 14 minutes, turning once, until deep golden.

Step 6

Fry the breast halves next, 10 to 12 minutes, keeping the oil at 320 to 340 F, until every piece reads 165 F at the bone.

Step 7

Drain on a wire rack, never paper towels, and shower with flaky salt while the crust is still hot.

Step 8

Rest 5 minutes, then serve hot with dill pickles.
