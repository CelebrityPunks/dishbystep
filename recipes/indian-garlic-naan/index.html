Indian Garlic Naan

India cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 2 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 3/4 cup warm whole milk
  • 1/4 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp flaky salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 3 cups flour, 2 tsp yeast, 1 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl.

Step 2

Stir in 3/4 cup warm milk, 1/4 cup yogurt, and 2 tbsp oil, then knead 8 minutes until smooth.

Step 3

Cover and let the dough rise in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled in size.

Step 4

Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces and rest them under a towel for 10 minutes.

Step 5

Roll each piece into a teardrop about 1/4 inch thick on a floured surface.

Step 6

Cook each naan in a dry hot cast iron skillet for 1 to 2 minutes per side until blistered.

Step 7

Stir 4 tbsp melted butter with 4 minced garlic cloves, then brush it over each hot naan.

Step 8

Stack the naan, shower with 2 tbsp cilantro and 1/2 tsp flaky salt, and serve warm.
