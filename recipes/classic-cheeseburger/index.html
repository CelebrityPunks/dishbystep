Classic Cheeseburger

United States cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Patties and Buns

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) ground beef (80/20)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 brioche burger buns
  • 4 slices American cheese

Toppings and Sauce

  • 1 large tomato, sliced
  • 1 small red onion, sliced
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 8 dill pickle chips
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp finely diced pickles

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 1 1/2 lb beef with Worcestershire, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.

Step 2

Shape into 4 patties and press a thumbprint in the center of each.

Step 3

Sear the patties 3 to 4 minutes per side in a hot cast iron skillet.

Step 4

Add a cheese slice to each patty and cover 1 minute until melted.

Step 5

Toast the buns cut side down for 1 to 2 minutes until golden.

Step 6

Stir the mayo, ketchup, mustard, and diced pickles into the burger sauce.

Step 7

Build each burger with sauce, a cheesy patty, lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles.

Step 8

Close the burgers and serve hot right away.
