Risotto alla Milanese

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Risotto Base

  • 1 1/2 cups arborio rice (300 g)
  • 5 1/2 cups hot chicken stock
  • 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste

Saffron And Finish

  • 1/2 tsp saffron threads
  • 3/4 cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Warm 5 1/2 cups chicken stock and steep 1/2 tsp saffron in 1/2 cup of the hot stock.

Step 2

Soften 1 finely chopped small onion in 2 tbsp butter and 2 tbsp olive oil for 3 minutes.

Step 3

Toast 1 1/2 cups arborio rice for 2 minutes, stirring, until the grains turn translucent at the edges.

Step 4

Pour in 1/2 cup dry white wine and stir until it is fully absorbed.

Step 5

Add the saffron stock and hot stock one ladle at a time, stirring, for 18 to 20 minutes.

Step 6

Keep the risotto loose and creamy, letting each ladle absorb before the next goes in.

Step 7

Off the heat, beat in 2 tbsp cold butter and 3/4 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Step 8

Rest 2 minutes, then plate and finish with extra Parmesan and a few saffron strands.
