Classic German Soft Pretzels

Germany cuisine · Serves 8 pretzels · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Baking Soda Bath + Finish

  • 1/2 cup baking soda
  • 9 cups water
  • Coarse sea salt, for topping
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • Grainy mustard, for serving

Dough

  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 cups warm water (about 100°F)
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 3/4 to 4 cups all-purpose flour

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Dissolve 2 1/4 tsp yeast and 1 tbsp sugar in 1 1/2 cups warm water; rest until foamy.

Step 2

Whisk in 1 tbsp melted butter and 1 tsp salt, then mix in 3 3/4 to 4 cups flour.

Step 3

Knead until the dough is smooth, cover, and rest 10 minutes.

Step 4

Divide into 8 pieces, roll each into a 20-inch rope, and twist into pretzels.

Step 5

Boil each pretzel 20-30 seconds in 9 cups water with 1/2 cup baking soda.

Step 6

Sprinkle with coarse salt and bake at 450°F for 12-14 min until deep golden.

Step 7

Brush the warm pretzels with 3 tbsp melted butter.

Step 8

Serve warm with grainy mustard.
