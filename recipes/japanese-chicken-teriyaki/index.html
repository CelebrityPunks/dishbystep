Japanese Chicken Teriyaki

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Teriyaki Sauce

  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup mirin
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

To Serve

  • 4 cups cooked short-grain rice
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced
  • Pickled ginger, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1/3 cup soy sauce, 1/4 cup mirin, 2 tbsp sake, and 2 tbsp sugar until the sugar dissolves.

Step 2

Pat the chicken thighs dry and season both sides with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.

Step 3

Sear the chicken skin-side down in 1 tbsp hot oil for 5-6 min until crisp and golden.

Step 4

Flip and cook 5 min more until the thickest part reads 165°F.

Step 5

Pour in the sauce and simmer 2-3 min, spooning it over until thick and glossy.

Step 6

Rest the chicken 5 min, then slice it into strips against the grain.

Step 7

Serve the sliced chicken over 4 cups of hot rice and spoon over the extra glaze.

Step 8

Sprinkle with sesame seeds and scallions, and serve hot with pickled ginger.
