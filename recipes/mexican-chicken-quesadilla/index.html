Chicken Quesadillas

Mexico cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Filling And Wrap

  • 1 medium red onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 cup corn kernels
  • 4 large 10-inch flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded Monterey Jack cheese (8 oz)
  • 2 tbsp butter, divided

Spiced Chicken

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

To Serve

  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup guacamole
  • 1/2 cup pico de gallo or salsa
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss the sliced chicken with the olive oil, chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.

Step 2

Sear the chicken in a hot skillet 5 to 6 minutes, turning once, until cooked through with charred edges.

Step 3

Add the onion, bell pepper, and corn to the same skillet and saute 3 to 4 minutes until just softened.

Step 4

Melt a little butter in the skillet. Lay in one tortilla and scatter a quarter of the cheese, chicken, and vegetables over one half.

Step 5

Fold the tortilla over and cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until deep golden and the cheese is fully melted.

Step 6

Repeat with the remaining tortillas, butter, and filling to make 4 quesadillas.

Step 7

Rest 1 to 2 minutes, then cut each quesadilla into 4 wedges.

Step 8

Serve hot with sour cream, guacamole, pico de gallo, cilantro, and lime wedges.
