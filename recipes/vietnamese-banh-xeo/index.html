Vietnamese Banh Xeo

Vietnam cuisine · Serves Serves 4 (makes 8 crepes) · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups rice flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup full-fat coconut milk, well shaken
  • 1 1/4 cups cold water
  • 3 scallions, thinly sliced

Ingredients

  • 6 oz pork belly or pork shoulder, very thinly sliced
  • 8 oz medium shrimp (20 to 26 count), peeled and deveined
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small yellow onion, very thinly sliced
  • 4 cups bean sprouts, rinsed and well drained
  • 1/3 cup neutral oil, divided

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup fish sauce
  • 1/4 cup fresh lime juice
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 Thai bird chili, thinly sliced

Ingredients

  • 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated
  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1 cup cilantro sprigs
  • 1/2 cup Thai basil leaves

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk the rice flour, cornstarch, turmeric, salt, coconut milk and water into a thin batter; stir in the scallions and rest 20 minutes.

Step 2

Toss the pork and shrimp with the fish sauce, sugar and garlic; marinate 10 minutes, then pat dry.

Step 3

Stir the fish sauce, lime juice, water, sugar, garlic and chili into the nuoc cham dipping sauce; set aside.

Step 4

Sear the pork in a hot oiled skillet until lightly golden, about 2 minutes; add the shrimp and cook until pink, 1 minute.

Step 5

Heat 1 tbsp oil in the skillet; ladle in a thin layer of batter and swirl to coat the whole pan.

Step 6

Scatter the pork, shrimp, onion and a handful of bean sprouts over one half of the crepe.

Step 7

Cover and steam 2 minutes; uncover and cook until the edges turn golden and lacy, then fold the crepe in half.

Step 8

Wrap warm wedges in lettuce leaves with mint, cilantro and basil, then dip in the nuoc cham.
