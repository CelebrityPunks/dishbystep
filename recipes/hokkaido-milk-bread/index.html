Cloud-Soft Hokkaido Milk Bread

Japan cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp (23 g) bread flour
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) whole milk

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups (298 g) bread flour
  • 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp (6 g) fine sea salt
  • 1 tbsp (9 g) instant yeast
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) whole milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup (57 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk (egg wash)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 3 tbsp flour with 1/2 cup water and 1/2 cup milk until thick and paste-like, then cool.

Step 2

Add 2 1/2 cups bread flour, 1/4 cup sugar, 1 tsp salt and 1 tbsp yeast, then stir in 1/2 cup milk, 1 egg and 1/4 cup melted butter.

Step 3

Knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

Step 4

Cover and let rise 1 hour, until doubled in size.

Step 5

Divide into 4 equal pieces and roll each into a smooth ball.

Step 6

Place seam-down in a greased 9x5-inch pan, rise 40-50 minutes until puffy, then brush with egg wash.

Step 7

Bake at 350°F for 25-30 minutes until deep golden.

Step 8

Cool 10 minutes in the pan, turn out, and tear a warm cloud-soft slice.
