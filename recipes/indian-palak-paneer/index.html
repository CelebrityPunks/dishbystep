Palak Paneer

India cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Spinach & Paneer

  • 500 g fresh baby spinach, washed
  • 250 g paneer, cut into 2 cm cubes
  • 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 green chilli, halved

Spices & Finish

  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil, divided
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 120 ml water
  • 60 ml heavy cream, plus extra
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Boil 500 g spinach for 2 minutes, plunge into ice water, then drain well.

Step 2

Blend the spinach with 1 green chilli until smooth.

Step 3

Fry 250 g paneer cubes in 1 tbsp ghee for 2 minutes per side until golden, then set aside.

Step 4

Sizzle 1 tsp cumin seeds in 1 tbsp oil, cook the onion 6 minutes, then add garlic, ginger, and tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.

Step 5

Stir in the turmeric, coriander, chilli powder, and salt, and cook 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 6

Pour in the spinach puree and 120 ml water, and simmer 5 minutes.

Step 7

Fold in the paneer, 60 ml cream, and 1 tsp garam masala, and simmer 2 minutes.

Step 8

Spoon into a bowl, swirl in extra cream, squeeze over lemon, and serve with rice or naan.
