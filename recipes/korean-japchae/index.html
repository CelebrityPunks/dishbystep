Korean Japchae

South Korea cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 oz dangmyeon (sweet potato starch noodles)
  • 4 oz beef filet mignon, in thin strips
  • 2 large dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 large egg

Ingredients

  • 4 oz fresh spinach
  • 1 medium carrot, cut into matchsticks
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 to 3 green onions, cut in 2-inch pieces
  • 4 to 5 white mushrooms, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, cut into thin strips

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp plus 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp plus 2 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Vegetable oil, for stir-frying

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 2 dried shiitake mushrooms in warm water for 2 to 3 hours, then cut into thin strips.

Step 2

Toss 4 oz beef strips with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp sesame oil; rest 10 minutes.

Step 3

Boil 4 oz dangmyeon noodles for 6 to 7 minutes until soft, drain, rinse cold, and toss with 1 tbsp sesame oil.

Step 4

Blanch 4 oz spinach for 30 seconds, rinse in cold water, squeeze dry, and season with a pinch of salt.

Step 5

Stir-fry the beef, mushrooms, onion, carrot, and bell pepper separately with a pinch of salt, 2 to 3 minutes each.

Step 6

Toss the noodles with 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 minced garlic cloves, and the remaining sesame oil.

Step 7

Fry 1 beaten egg into a thin omelet, roll it up, and slice into thin strips.

Step 8

Combine everything with the spinach and scallions, top with egg strips and 1 tbsp sesame seeds, and serve.
