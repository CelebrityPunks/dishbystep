Better Than Takeout Beef and Broccoli

China cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Ingredients

  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 cup beef broth
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Steamed rice, to serve
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 1 lb thinly sliced flank steak with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp oil, then rest for 15 minutes.

Step 2

Whisk 1/3 cup soy sauce, 1/4 cup oyster sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp cornstarch and 1/2 cup beef broth.

Step 3

Blanch 4 cups broccoli florets in boiling water for 2 minutes, then drain well.

Step 4

Sear the beef in 2 tbsp hot vegetable oil for 2-3 minutes, then set it aside.

Step 5

Stir-fry 3 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger in the same pan for 30 seconds.

Step 6

Return the beef and broccoli to the pan and pour in the sauce.

Step 7

Toss for 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.

Step 8

Serve hot over steamed rice, topped with sliced green onions.
