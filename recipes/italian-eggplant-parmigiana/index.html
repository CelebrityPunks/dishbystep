Eggplant Parmigiana

Italy cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Eggplant & Sauce

  • 2 large eggplants, about 1.1 kg (2.5 lb), cut into 6 mm (1/4 in) rounds
  • 1 tbsp fine sea salt, for sweating the eggplant
  • 120 ml (1/2 cup) extra-virgin olive oil, plus 2 tbsp for the sauce
  • 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 x 400 g cans (800 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano and 1/2 tsp black pepper

Cheese & Finish

  • 500 g fresh mozzarella, torn and well drained
  • 80 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
  • 1 large bunch fresh basil leaves, torn

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 2 large eggplants, sliced 6 mm thick, with 1 tbsp salt and rest 30 minutes, then pat very dry.

Step 2

Brush both sides with olive oil and roast at 220°C (425°F) for 25 minutes, flipping once, until golden.

Step 3

Sizzle the onion in 2 tbsp olive oil for 5 minutes, add the garlic, then the crushed tomatoes, oregano, and pepper. Simmer 15 minutes.

Step 4

Spread sauce in a baking dish, then layer roasted eggplant, torn mozzarella, Parmesan, and basil.

Step 5

Repeat the layers, ending with sauce and a thick blanket of mozzarella and Parmesan.

Step 6

Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 30 minutes until bubbling and golden on top.

Step 7

Rest 10 minutes so the layers set, then scatter fresh basil over the top.

Step 8

Scoop out a molten square, golden and bubbling, and serve warm.
