Classic British Scones

United Kingdom cuisine · Serves Makes 8 scones · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dry

  • 350g self-raising flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar

Wet

  • 85g cold unsalted butter, cut into cubes
  • 175ml whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Squeeze of lemon juice
  • 1 egg, beaten, to glaze

To serve

  • Strawberry jam, to serve
  • Clotted cream, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Tip 350g flour, 1 tsp baking powder and 1/4 tsp salt into a big bowl, then mix.

Step 2

Rub in 85g cold cubed butter until it looks like fine crumbs, then stir in the sugar.

Step 3

Warm 175ml milk for 30 seconds, add 1 tsp vanilla and a squeeze of lemon, then set aside.

Step 4

Make a well, pour in the milk and mix quickly with a knife into a soft, wet dough.

Step 5

Pat the dough 4cm thick and stamp out 8 rounds straight down, no twisting.

Step 6

Brush the tops with beaten egg and bake at 220C for 10 minutes until risen and golden.

Step 7

Cool 5 minutes, then split a warm scone for the cream and jam filling.

Step 8

Serve warm with strawberry jam and clotted cream.
