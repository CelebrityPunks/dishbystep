Classic French Tarte Tatin

France cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Apples And Caramel

  • 6 large firm apples (Honeycrisp or Braeburn), peeled, cored and halved
  • 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp fine sea salt

Crust And Serving

  • 1 sheet (14 oz) all-butter puff pastry, thawed
  • All-purpose flour, for dusting
  • Creme fraiche or vanilla ice cream, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel, core and halve 6 large firm apples.

Step 2

Melt 1/2 cup butter with 3/4 cup sugar in a 10-inch skillet until a deep amber caramel forms.

Step 3

Off the heat, stir in 1 tsp vanilla and 1/4 tsp salt until smooth.

Step 4

Pack the apple halves tightly into the caramel, rounded side down.

Step 5

Cook 12-15 minutes until the apples soften and the caramel thickens.

Step 6

Roll one puff pastry sheet into a 12-inch round, drape over the apples and tuck the edges.

Step 7

Bake at 375F for 35-40 minutes until the pastry is deep golden.

Step 8

Rest 5 minutes, invert onto a plate and serve warm with creme fraiche.
