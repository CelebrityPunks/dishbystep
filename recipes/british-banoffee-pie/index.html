Classic British Banoffee Pie

United Kingdom cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Crust

  • 2 cups (200 g) digestive biscuit crumbs, about 14 biscuits
  • 1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • Pinch of fine salt

Filling And Topping

  • 1 can (14 oz / 397 g) dulce de leche
  • 3 large ripe bananas, sliced 1/4 inch thick
  • Squeeze of lemon juice
  • 2 cups (480 ml) heavy whipping cream, chilled
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp grated dark chocolate or cocoa, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 2 cups biscuit crumbs with 1/2 cup melted butter and 1 tbsp sugar.

Step 2

Press into a 9-inch tart pan and chill the crust 30 min.

Step 3

Spread one 14 oz can of dulce de leche over the cold crust.

Step 4

Layer 3 sliced bananas over the caramel, with a squeeze of lemon.

Step 5

Whip 2 cups cold cream with 2 tbsp powdered sugar and 1 tsp vanilla to soft peaks.

Step 6

Pile the whipped cream over the bananas in generous swirls.

Step 7

Dust with grated dark chocolate, then chill the pie 2 hours.

Step 8

Slice with a hot dry knife and serve the gooey wedges chilled.
