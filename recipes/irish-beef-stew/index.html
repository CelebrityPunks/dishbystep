Irish Beef Stew

Ireland cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 2 1/2 lb beef chuck cubes with 1/3 cup flour, salt, and pepper.

Step 2

Brown the beef in 3 tbsp hot oil in batches, 3-4 minutes per side.

Step 3

Soften 2 diced onions for 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic and tomato paste.

Step 4

Pour in the Guinness, scraping up the browned bits, and simmer 2 minutes.

Step 5

Add 3 cups beef stock, Worcestershire, thyme, and bay; simmer covered 1 hour 30 minutes.

Step 6

Stir in the carrots and potatoes; simmer 45 minutes until fork-tender.

Step 7

Uncover and simmer 10 minutes more until the gravy turns glossy and thick.

Step 8

Ladle into warm bowls, scatter fresh parsley, and serve with crusty bread.
