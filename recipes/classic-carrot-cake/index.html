Classic Carrot Cake

United States cuisine · Serves 12 slices · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

CAKE

  • 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 2 cups (397 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups (248 g) vegetable oil
  • 4 large eggs
  • 3 cups (326 g) carrots, finely grated
  • 1 1/2 cups (170 g) walnuts, toasted and chopped

CREAM CHEESE FROSTING

  • 8 tbsp (113 g) unsalted butter, room temperature
  • one 8-oz package (227 g) cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 1/2 cups (283 g) confectioners sugar

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 cups flour, 1 tbsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp cloves, 1 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp baking soda and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl.

Step 2

Beat 2 cups sugar, 1 1/4 cups vegetable oil and 4 eggs until smooth, then stir in the dry mix until just blended.

Step 3

Fold in 3 cups grated carrots and 1 1/2 cups toasted walnuts until evenly combined.

Step 4

Divide the batter between two greased 9-inch round pans and smooth the tops.

Step 5

Bake at 350 F for 35 to 40 minutes until a tester comes out clean, then cool the layers completely.

Step 6

Beat 8 oz cream cheese, 8 tbsp butter, 2 tsp vanilla and 1/4 tsp salt, then beat in 2 1/2 cups confectioners sugar until silky.

Step 7

Stack the cooled layers with frosting between them, then frost the top and sides in smooth swirls.

Step 8

Slice at room temperature and serve, with a few extra chopped walnuts on top.
