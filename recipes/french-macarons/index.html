French Macarons

France cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Shells

  • 100g egg whites, at room temperature (about 3 large)
  • 50g granulated sugar (1/4 cup)
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 100g fine almond flour (1 cup)
  • 200g powdered sugar (1 3/4 cups)
  • Gel food coloring (a few drops)

Buttercream Filling

  • 115g unsalted butter, softened (1/2 cup)
  • 190g powdered sugar (1 1/2 cups)
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream
  • Pinch of salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Sift 100g almond flour and 200g powdered sugar twice, discarding any lumps.

Step 2

Whip 100g room-temp egg whites with 1/4 tsp cream of tartar to soft peaks, add 50g sugar slowly, then whip to stiff glossy peaks.

Step 3

Fold in the dry mix and a few drops of gel color until the batter flows in a ribbon and makes a figure 8.

Step 4

Pipe 1.5-inch rounds onto a lined tray, then tap the tray hard 5 times to pop air bubbles.

Step 5

Rest 30 to 45 minutes until the shells form a dry matte skin.

Step 6

Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 15 to 18 minutes until feet form and the shells do not wiggle.

Step 7

Beat the butter, 190g powdered sugar, vanilla, cream and salt smooth; pipe filling on one shell and sandwich.

Step 8

Chill the filled macarons 24 hours for chewy centers, then serve at room temperature.
