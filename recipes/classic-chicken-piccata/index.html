Classic Chicken Piccata

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, about 1 1/2 lb
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour, for dredging
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/3 cup fresh lemon juice, about 2 lemons
  • 3 tbsp capers, drained
  • 2 tbsp cold unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • Lemon slices, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Pound 4 chicken breasts to 1/4 inch thick, then season with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.

Step 2

Dredge each cutlet in 1/2 cup flour, shaking off the excess.

Step 3

Heat 3 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

Step 4

Sear the chicken 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden, then transfer to a plate.

Step 5

Pour in 1/2 cup white wine, scraping up the browned bits, and simmer 1 minute.

Step 6

Add 1 cup chicken broth, 1/3 cup lemon juice and 3 tbsp capers, then simmer 3 to 4 minutes until slightly reduced.

Step 7

Off the heat, swirl in 2 tbsp cold butter until the sauce turns silky and glossy.

Step 8

Spoon the lemon caper sauce over the chicken, scatter 2 tbsp parsley and lemon slices, then serve hot.
