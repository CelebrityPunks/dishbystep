Homemade Sushi Rolls

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Rolls And Serving

  • 4 sheets toasted nori
  • 1/2 lb sushi-grade tuna, cut into thin strips
  • 1/2 lb sushi-grade salmon, cut into thin strips
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce, for serving
  • Pickled ginger and wasabi, for serving

Sushi Rice

  • 2 cups Japanese short-grain rice
  • 2 1/2 cups water
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rinse 2 cups short-grain rice, add 2 1/2 cups water, and simmer covered 15 minutes. Rest 10 minutes.

Step 2

Fold the warm rice vinegar, sugar, and salt into the rice. Cool to room temperature.

Step 3

Cut the tuna and salmon into strips, julienne the cucumber, slice the avocado, and stir mayo with sriracha.

Step 4

Lay a nori sheet on a bamboo mat and spread 3/4 cup rice over it, leaving a 1-inch top border.

Step 5

Line up tuna, salmon, cucumber, avocado, spicy mayo, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.

Step 6

Roll tightly with the mat, moistening the bare nori border with water to seal.

Step 7

Slice each roll into 8 pieces with a wet, sharp knife.

Step 8

Plate with soy sauce, pickled ginger, and wasabi. Serve right away.
