Classic Chocolate Mousse

France cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 7 oz dark chocolate (60-70%), chopped
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 large eggs, separated
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup heavy cream, cold
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of fine sea salt

Ingredients

  • Whipped cream, to top
  • Chocolate shavings, to top
  • 4 small glasses or ramekins

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Melt 7 oz dark chocolate with 3 tbsp butter over a bain-marie until smooth.

Step 2

Whisk 3 egg yolks into the warm chocolate, one at a time, until glossy.

Step 3

Beat 3 egg whites with a pinch of salt to soft peaks, then beat in 1/4 cup sugar to stiff peaks.

Step 4

Whip 1 cup cold heavy cream with 1 tsp vanilla to soft peaks.

Step 5

Fold one third of the whipped cream into the chocolate to loosen it.

Step 6

Gently fold in the remaining cream and egg whites until just combined.

Step 7

Spoon the mousse into 4 glasses and chill for at least 2 hours.

Step 8

Serve chilled, topped with whipped cream and chocolate shavings.
