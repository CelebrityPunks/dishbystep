Argentine Chimichurri Steak

Argentina cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley leaves, packed
  • 1/4 cup fresh oregano leaves
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb flank steak
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Flaky sea salt, to finish
  • Crusty bread or grilled vegetables, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Blitz the parsley, oregano, garlic, olive oil, vinegar, and chili flakes into chimichurri.

Step 2

Pat the flank steak dry and season all over with 1 tsp kosher salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper.

Step 3

Sear the steak in 1 tbsp hot olive oil for 4 to 5 minutes per side until deeply browned.

Step 4

Rest the steak for 5 minutes so the juices settle before slicing.

Step 5

Slice the steak thinly against the grain.

Step 6

Spoon the bright chimichurri generously over the sliced steak.

Step 7

Add lemon wedges, a final drizzle of olive oil, and a pinch of flaky sea salt.

Step 8

Serve the chimichurri steak hot with crusty bread or grilled vegetables.
