Greek Chicken Souvlaki

Greece cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken & Marinade

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (about 680 g)
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper

To Serve

  • 4 large pita breads
  • 1 cup tzatziki sauce
  • 2 medium tomatoes, diced
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1/4 cup olive oil, 1/4 cup lemon juice, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp oregano, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.

Step 2

Cut 1 1/2 lb chicken into 1-inch cubes, coat in the marinade and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.

Step 3

Thread the chicken onto skewers, 5 to 6 pieces per skewer.

Step 4

Grill over medium-high heat, 5-6 minutes per side, until golden with char and cooked through.

Step 5

Warm the 4 pita breads on the grill, about 30 seconds per side.

Step 6

Spread a spoonful of tzatziki over each warm pita.

Step 7

Pile on the chicken, tomatoes, red onion and feta.

Step 8

Fold the pitas, squeeze over lemon wedges and serve.
