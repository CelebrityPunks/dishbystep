Croque-Monsieur

France cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

The sandwich

  • 8 thick slices brioche or pain de mie
  • 8 slices cooked ham (about 12 oz / 340 g)
  • 2 cups shredded Gruyere (8 oz / 225 g), divided
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, softened

The bechamel cap

  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups whole milk (480 ml)
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Butter one side of all 8 bread slices with 4 tbsp softened butter.

Step 2

Melt 2 tbsp butter, whisk in 3 tbsp flour for 1 minute, then pour in 2 cups milk.

Step 3

Stir in 2 tbsp Dijon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, salt, and pepper until thick and silky.

Step 4

Layer 8 ham slices and 1 1/2 cups Gruyere between the bread, buttered sides out.

Step 5

Spread bechamel over the sandwich tops and add the remaining 1/2 cup Gruyere.

Step 6

Bake at 425F for 12 to 15 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Step 7

Rest 2 minutes, then slice each sandwich diagonally.

Step 8

Serve hot while the cheese is melty and the tops are crisp.
