French Chocolate Éclairs

France cuisine · Serves makes 12 éclairs · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chocolate Glaze

  • 4 oz semisweet chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Choux Pastry

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter (8 tbsp)
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 large eggs

Pastry Cream

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Bring 1 cup water, 1/2 cup butter, 1 tbsp sugar, and 1/4 tsp salt to a rolling boil.

Step 2

Stir in 1 cup flour and cook 2 minutes until a smooth ball films the pan.

Step 3

Cool 3 minutes, then beat in 4 eggs one at a time until glossy.

Step 4

Pipe twelve 4-inch logs onto parchment-lined sheets.

Step 5

Bake at 425°F for 10 minutes, then 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden.

Step 6

Whisk 2 cups hot milk into the yolks and cook until thick and bubbling.

Step 7

Cool the cream 1 hour, then pipe it into each cooled éclair.

Step 8

Dip the tops in warm chocolate ganache, set 15 minutes, then serve.
