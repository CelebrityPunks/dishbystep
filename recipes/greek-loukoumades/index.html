Greek Loukoumades

Greece cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

DOUGH

  • 2 1/4 tsp (1 packet) active dry yeast
  • 1 cup warm water (110 F)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil

FOR FRYING

  • 4 cups neutral oil

HONEY SYRUP

  • 1 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 cinnamon stick

TO FINISH

  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 1/4 tsp yeast, 1 tsp sugar and 1 cup warm water, then rest 5 minutes until foamy.

Step 2

Stir in 2 cups flour, 1/2 tsp salt and 2 tbsp olive oil, and beat into a thick smooth batter.

Step 3

Cover and proof 1 hour in a warm spot until doubled and bubbly.

Step 4

Heat 4 cups neutral oil in a deep pot to 350 F over medium-high heat.

Step 5

Drop tablespoon scoops into the hot oil and fry 2 to 3 minutes per side until deep golden.

Step 6

Lift the puffs with a slotted spoon and drain on a wire rack.

Step 7

Simmer 1 cup honey, 1/2 cup water, 1 tsp lemon juice and 1 cinnamon stick for 5 minutes.

Step 8

Stack the puffs high, drizzle the warm honey syrup over them, and sprinkle 2 tbsp sesame seeds and cinnamon.
