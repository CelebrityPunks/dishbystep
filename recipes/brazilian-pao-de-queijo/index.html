Brazilian Pão de Queijo

Brazil cuisine · Serves 30 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cheese And Eggs

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups (150 g) freshly grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup (110 g) shredded mozzarella cheese

For The Dough

  • 4 cups (480 g) tapioca flour
  • 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) whole milk
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 6 tbsp (90 ml) vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp kosher salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 400°F. Bring 1 1/4 cups milk, 1/2 cup water, 6 tbsp oil, and 2 tsp salt to a boil.

Step 2

Pour the boiling milk mixture over 4 cups tapioca flour and mix until white and sticky, like fondant.

Step 3

Beat in 2 large eggs one at a time until each is fully absorbed into the sticky dough.

Step 4

Stir in 1 1/2 cups parmesan and 1 cup mozzarella a little at a time until evenly mixed.

Step 5

Rest the soft, sticky dough for 10 minutes so it firms up slightly.

Step 6

With wet hands, roll golf-ball-size balls and set them on a parchment-lined baking tray.

Step 7

Bake 15 to 20 minutes at 400°F until puffed and golden.

Step 8

Serve warm and pull one open to reveal the chewy, cheesy center.
