New England Clam Chowder

United States cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chowder Base

  • 4 slices thick-cut bacon, chopped
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 2 celery stalks, diced (about 3/4 cup)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups bottled clam juice

Clams + Cream Finish

  • 2 (6.5 oz) cans chopped clams, undrained
  • 1 1/2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, cut in 1/2-inch cubes
  • 2 cups half-and-half
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh chives

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cook the chopped bacon in a heavy pot over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until crisp; lift it out and set aside.

Step 2

Add the diced onion and celery to the pot and cook 5-6 minutes until soft and golden.

Step 3

Stir in the minced garlic for 30 seconds, then sprinkle in the flour and stir 1-2 minutes.

Step 4

Slowly whisk in the clam juice until smooth, then add the potatoes, bay leaves, and thyme.

Step 5

Simmer gently for 15-20 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender.

Step 6

Stir in the half-and-half and the chopped clams with their juice; warm 3-5 minutes. Do not boil.

Step 7

Remove the bay leaves and season with the salt and black pepper.

Step 8

Ladle into warm bowls, top with the crispy bacon and chives, and serve hot.
