Classic Quiche Lorraine

France cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Filling

  • 200g smoked streaky bacon, cut into lardons
  • 100g Gruyere, grated
  • 3 large eggs
  • 300ml double cream
  • 100ml whole milk
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Pastry

  • 175g plain flour
  • 85g chilled butter, cubed
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 2 to 3 tbsp cold water

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub 85g cold butter into 175g flour and 1/2 tsp salt until it looks like breadcrumbs.

Step 2

Stir in 2 to 3 tbsp cold water, form a smooth dough, and chill for 30 minutes.

Step 3

Roll out the dough and line a 23cm fluted tart tin, then chill for 20 minutes.

Step 4

Blind bake at 200C for 15 minutes with baking beans, then 5 more minutes without.

Step 5

Fry 200g bacon lardons over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes until crisp and golden.

Step 6

Scatter the bacon and 100g grated Gruyere over the base of the pastry case.

Step 7

Whisk 3 eggs with 300ml cream, 100ml milk, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, salt, and pepper, then pour over the filling.

Step 8

Bake at 180C for 30 to 35 minutes until golden and just set, then rest 10 minutes and serve warm.
