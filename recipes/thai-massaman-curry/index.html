Thai Massaman Curry

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 dried red chiles, seeded and soaked 20 min
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh galangal, chopped
  • 5 shallots, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp shrimp paste
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds, toasted
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, toasted
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 green cardamom pods

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs, 2-inch pieces
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp massaman curry paste (from above)
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 lb small potatoes, quartered
  • 2 yellow onions, in wedges
  • 1/2 cup roasted unsalted peanuts
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped, to finish
  • Steamed jasmine rice, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Blend the soaked chiles, lemongrass, galangal, shallots, garlic, shrimp paste and spices into a smooth paste.

Step 2

Fry 3 tbsp of the paste in 1 tbsp coconut oil and the thick coconut cream until fragrant, about 2 minutes.

Step 3

Add the chicken thighs and toss to coat in the fried paste, about 2 minutes.

Step 4

Pour in the remaining coconut milk and bring the curry to a gentle simmer.

Step 5

Stir in the fish sauce, tamarind paste and brown sugar, and add the kaffir lime leaves.

Step 6

Add the potatoes and onion wedges and simmer gently 25 minutes until tender.

Step 7

Stir in the roasted peanuts and simmer 5 minutes more to finish the sauce.

Step 8

Serve the massaman curry over steamed jasmine rice and finish with fresh cilantro.
