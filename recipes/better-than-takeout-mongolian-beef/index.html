Better-Than-Takeout Mongolian Beef

China cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) flank steak, sliced very thinly against the grain
  • 1/4 cup (30 g) cornstarch
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) vegetable oil, for searing

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup (110 g) packed dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 4 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

Ingredients

  • Hot cooked white rice

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Slice 1 lb flank steak very thinly against the grain.

Step 2

Toss the beef with 1/4 cup cornstarch until evenly coated.

Step 3

Sear the beef in 1/3 cup hot oil over high heat, 2-3 minutes, until crisp at the edges.

Step 4

Sizzle 1 tbsp minced ginger and 4 minced garlic cloves for 30 seconds.

Step 5

Add 1/2 cup soy sauce, 1/2 cup brown sugar and 1/2 cup water; simmer 2-3 minutes until slightly thickened.

Step 6

Return the beef to the wok and toss 1-2 minutes until every slice is glazed.

Step 7

Stir in the green onions and 1 tsp sesame oil off the heat.

Step 8

Spoon the glossy beef over hot steamed rice and serve right away.
