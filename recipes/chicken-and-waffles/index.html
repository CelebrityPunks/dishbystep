Crispy Chicken and Waffles

United States cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 6)
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce, plus more to serve
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 2 tsp salt, 1 tsp pepper
  • Neutral oil, for frying

Waffle batter

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 3/4 cups buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted, plus 1 tsp vanilla

To serve

  • Warm maple syrup
  • Hot honey
  • Softened butter

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 cups buttermilk with 1 tbsp hot sauce, coat the chicken and chill at least 1 hour.

Step 2

Heat 2 to 3 inches of oil in a heavy pot to 350F over medium-high heat.

Step 3

Whisk 2 cups flour with the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, 2 tsp salt and 1 tsp pepper.

Step 4

Dredge the marinated chicken in the seasoned flour, press to coat, then rest 10 minutes.

Step 5

Fry the chicken 7 to 9 minutes until deep golden and 165F inside, then drain on a wire rack.

Step 6

Whisk the waffle batter: dry ingredients first, then eggs, buttermilk, melted butter and vanilla. Rest 5 minutes.

Step 7

Cook waffles in a hot buttered iron 4 to 5 minutes until crisp and golden.

Step 8

Stack the crispy chicken on warm waffles and drizzle generously with warm maple syrup and hot honey.
