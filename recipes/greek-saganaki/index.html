Greek Saganaki

Greece cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cheese

  • 8 oz kefalograviera or halloumi cheese
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Finish

  • 3 tbsp ouzo or brandy
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Cracked black pepper
  • Lemon wedges and warm crusty bread, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Slice 8 ounces kefalograviera or halloumi into 3/4-inch thick slabs.

Step 2

Rinse the slabs, pat dry, and coat both sides in 1/3 cup flour, shaking off the excess.

Step 3

Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a small cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

Step 4

Sear the cheese 2 to 3 minutes per side until deeply golden and crisp at the edges.

Step 5

Pour 3 tablespoons ouzo around the cheese, tip the pan, and flame it for 30 seconds.

Step 6

Kill the flame, squeeze over 2 tablespoons lemon juice, and shower with 1 teaspoon oregano and cracked pepper.

Step 7

Rest 1 minute in the hot skillet so the cheese settles but stays molten.

Step 8

Serve sizzling with lemon wedges and warm crusty bread for scooping.
