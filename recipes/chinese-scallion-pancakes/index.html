Flaky Crispy Scallion Pancakes

China cuisine · Serves 4 (makes 4 large pancakes) · 7 visual steps

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 260 g (2 cups) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
  • 180 ml (3/4 cup) just-boiled hot water
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

For the scallion filling

  • 5 scallions (about 1 cup), thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt

For frying

  • 3 to 4 tbsp vegetable oil

For the dipping sauce

  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
