Soft Swedish Cinnamon Buns

Sweden cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Warm whole milk - 1 cup
  • Active dry yeast - 2 1/4 tsp
  • Granulated sugar - 1/3 cup
  • Large egg - 1
  • Unsalted butter, melted - 1/2 cup
  • All-purpose flour - 4 cups
  • Fine salt - 1 tsp
  • Ground cardamom - 1 tsp

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter, soft - 1/2 cup
  • Brown sugar - 3/4 cup
  • Ground cinnamon - 2 tbsp
  • Ground cardamom - 1/2 tsp

Ingredients

  • Large egg, beaten - 1
  • Pearl sugar - 2 tbsp

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Warm 1 cup milk, stir in 2 1/4 tsp yeast and 1/3 cup sugar, then rest 5 minutes until foamy.

Step 2

Add 1 egg, 1/2 cup melted butter, 4 cups flour, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tsp cardamom, then knead 8 minutes until smooth.

Step 3

Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour until doubled in size.

Step 4

Roll the dough into an 18 by 12 inch rectangle and spread the cinnamon filling evenly over it.

Step 5

Roll the dough up tightly, cut 12 even buns, and place them on a lined baking tray.

Step 6

Cover and rise 30 minutes more, then brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with 2 tbsp pearl sugar.

Step 7

Bake at 375F for 18 to 20 minutes until deep golden.

Step 8

Serve the buns warm, soft and sticky in the middle.
