Sicilian Cannoli

Italy cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Shells

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
  • 3 tbsp cold butter, cubed
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1/2 cup dry Marsala wine
  • 1 egg white, beaten (to seal)
  • 2 quarts oil, for frying

Filling

  • 2 cups ricotta, drained overnight
  • 3/4 cup confectioners' sugar, plus more for dusting
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips
  • 1/3 cup chopped pistachios

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, cinnamon, and salt with the butter, then knead in the egg and Marsala for 8-10 min until smooth.

Step 2

Wrap the dough and rest it at room temperature for 1 hour so it rolls thin.

Step 3

Roll the dough paper-thin, cut 4-inch rounds, wrap each around a tube, and seal the edge with egg white.

Step 4

Fry the shells at 350°F for 2-3 min until golden and bubbly, then drain and slide them off the tubes.

Step 5

Beat the drained ricotta with the confectioners' sugar, vanilla, and cinnamon, then fold in the chocolate chips.

Step 6

Pipe the filling into both ends of each completely cooled shell.

Step 7

Dip each end in chopped pistachios and dust the shells with confectioners' sugar.

Step 8

Serve within 2 hours so the shells stay crisp and crackly.
