Sweet and Sour Pork

China cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Pork and Coating

  • 1 1/2 lb pork tenderloin, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1/3 cup cornstarch
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 2 cups neutral oil, for frying

Vegetables and Fruit

  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 green bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 small yellow onion, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks, drained

Sweet and Sour Sauce

  • 1/2 cup rice vinegar
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/3 cup water

To Serve

  • Steamed white rice, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 1 1/2 lb pork tenderloin into 1-inch cubes, then season with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp white pepper.

Step 2

Whisk 1/3 cup cornstarch, 1/3 cup flour, and 1 beaten egg into a thick batter, then coat every pork cube.

Step 3

Fry the coated pork in 2 cups oil at 350°F until deep golden and crisp, 4 to 5 minutes per batch.

Step 4

Stir-fry 1 red bell pepper, 1 green bell pepper, and 1 small onion in 1 tbsp oil for 2 to 3 minutes.

Step 5

Whisk 1/2 cup rice vinegar, 1/2 cup sugar, 1/4 cup ketchup, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp cornstarch, and 1/3 cup water until smooth.

Step 6

Pour the sauce into the wok and simmer 2 to 3 minutes until thick and glossy.

Step 7

Toss the crispy pork, peppers, onion, and 1 cup pineapple chunks in the glossy sauce until coated.

Step 8

Spoon the sweet and sour pork over hot steamed rice and serve right away.
