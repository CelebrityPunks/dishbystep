French Madeleines

France cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 10 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and cooled

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp softened butter, for the pan
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour, for dusting the pan
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 eggs, 2/3 cup sugar, 1 tsp vanilla and 1 tsp lemon zest until pale and thick, about 3 minutes.

Step 2

Sift in 1 cup flour, 1 tsp baking powder and 1/4 tsp salt, then fold gently until just combined.

Step 3

Fold in 10 tbsp melted cooled butter until the batter is smooth and glossy.

Step 4

Cover and chill the batter in the fridge for 30 minutes.

Step 5

Brush the madeleine pan with 1 tbsp softened butter and dust with 1 tbsp flour, tapping out the excess.

Step 6

Spoon 1 tbsp batter into each shell mold, filling about three-quarters full.

Step 7

Bake at 375°F for 10-12 minutes until golden with a tall bump.

Step 8

Cool 2 minutes, unmold, and dust the warm madeleines with 2 tbsp powdered sugar before serving.
