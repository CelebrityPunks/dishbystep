Indian Mango Kulfi

India cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mango pulp (about 400 g)
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 1/2 cups cold heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup whole milk powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of fine sea salt

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup chopped pistachios
  • Extra mango slices, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Blend 2 cups mango pulp, 1 can condensed milk, 1/2 cup milk powder, 1/2 tsp cardamom and a pinch of salt until completely smooth.

Step 2

Whip 1 1/2 cups cold heavy cream in a chilled bowl until soft peaks form.

Step 3

Fold the mango mixture gently into the whipped cream until smooth and silky.

Step 4

Stir in half of the 1/4 cup chopped pistachios.

Step 5

Pour the mixture into 6 kulfi molds and tap gently to remove air bubbles.

Step 6

Insert the sticks and freeze for at least 8 hours until completely firm.

Step 7

Dip each mold briefly in warm water and unmold the kulfi carefully.

Step 8

Top with the remaining pistachios and mango slices, and serve immediately before it melts.
