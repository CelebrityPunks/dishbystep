Halo-Halo

Philippines cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

FOR THE LAYERS

  • 2 cups cooked sweetened red munggo beans
  • 1 cup macapuno (coconut sport) strings
  • 1 cup kaong (sugar palm fruit)
  • 1 cup nata de coco
  • 1/2 cup sweetened saba bananas, diced

FOR THE TOP

  • 1 cup ube halaya (purple yam jam)
  • 4 slices leche flan (about 1/2 cup)
  • 2 cups shaved or finely crushed ice
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 1/4 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 scoops ube ice cream
  • 2 tbsp pinipig (toasted pounded rice), for crunch

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Add 2 tablespoons each of munggo beans, macapuno, and kaong to each tall glass.

Step 2

Top each glass with 2 tablespoons each of nata de coco and diced saba.

Step 3

Spoon 2 tablespoons of ube halaya over the layers.

Step 4

Add a slice of leche flan on top of the ube.

Step 5

Pack shaved ice to the top of each glass, mounding it slightly.

Step 6

Drizzle 2 tablespoons of evaporated milk and 1 tablespoon of condensed milk over the ice.

Step 7

Top with a scoop of ube ice cream and a sprinkle of pinipig.

Step 8

Serve with a long spoon.
