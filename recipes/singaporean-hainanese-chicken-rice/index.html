Hainanese Chicken Rice

Singapore cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.5 kg), excess fat trimmed and reserved
  • 1 tbsp sea salt
  • 5 slices fresh ginger
  • 3 spring onions, roughly chopped
  • 2 tsp sesame oil

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (400 g) jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 2 tbsp rendered chicken fat (or vegetable oil)
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 2 slices fresh ginger
  • 2 1/2 cups reserved chicken broth
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt

Ingredients

  • Ginger sauce: 2 tbsp minced ginger, 2 tbsp hot neutral oil, pinch of salt
  • Chilli sauce: 2 red chillies, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp lime juice, 1 tsp sugar, 2 tbsp broth
  • Soy dressing: 2 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tbsp broth, few drops sesame oil
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub 1 whole chicken (1.5 kg) all over with 1 tbsp sea salt, rinse, and stuff with ginger and spring onions.

Step 2

Lower the chicken into a pot of boiling water, then simmer gently 20 minutes and rest off the heat 20 minutes.

Step 3

Plunge the poached chicken into an ice bath for 10 minutes, then rub the skin with 2 tsp sesame oil.

Step 4

Toast 2 cups rinsed jasmine rice in 2 tbsp rendered chicken fat with garlic and ginger for 2 minutes.

Step 5

Steam the rice in 2 1/2 cups of the poaching broth until fluffy, about 15 minutes.

Step 6

Blend 2 red chillies, garlic, lime juice, sugar and broth into the chilli sauce.

Step 7

Mix 2 tbsp minced ginger with hot oil and a pinch of salt into the ginger sauce.

Step 8

Slice the chicken over the fragrant rice with cucumber, the sauces, and a bowl of hot broth.
