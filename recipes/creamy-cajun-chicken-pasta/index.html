Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta

United States cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 oz fettuccine

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan
  • Sliced green onions, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Season 2 lb chicken breasts with 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning, salt and pepper.

Step 2

Sear the chicken in 2 tbsp olive oil for 5 to 6 minutes per side, then slice it.

Step 3

Melt 2 tbsp butter and cook 1 diced onion and 1 sliced bell pepper for 4 minutes.

Step 4

Add 4 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp tomato paste, cooking 1 minute more.

Step 5

Pour in 2 cups heavy cream and 1 cup chicken broth, then simmer 3 minutes.

Step 6

Stir in 1 cup grated Parmesan until the sauce turns silky.

Step 7

Toss in 12 oz cooked fettuccine and the sliced chicken.

Step 8

Serve hot, topped with sliced green onions and extra Parmesan.
