Japanese Okonomiyaki

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (120 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp each of salt, sugar and baking powder
  • 2-3 inch nagaimo, grated (optional)
  • 3/4 cup (180 ml) dashi stock

Ingredients

  • 1 cabbage head (8-9 cups shredded)
  • 1/2 lb (227 g) sliced pork belly
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup tenkasu
  • 1/4 cup beni shoga, chopped
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 4 tbsp ketchup
  • 3 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Japanese mayonnaise
  • Katsuobushi (bonito flakes)
  • Aonori (seaweed powder)
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 cup flour, 1/4 tsp each salt, sugar and baking powder with the grated nagaimo and 3/4 cup dashi.

Step 2

Cover and rest the batter in the fridge for at least 1 hour.

Step 3

Whisk 1 1/2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp oyster sauce, 4 tbsp ketchup and 3 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire into the okonomiyaki sauce.

Step 4

Shred the cabbage into 8 to 9 cups and chop the beni shoga; cut the pork belly in half.

Step 5

Fold 4 eggs, 1/2 cup tenkasu, the beni shoga and all the cabbage into the batter.

Step 6

Oil a hot pan, pour the batter into a round and lay the pork belly on top.

Step 7

Cook 5 to 6 minutes per side, flipping once, until golden and set.

Step 8

Plate and zigzag with okonomiyaki sauce and mayo, then shower with katsuobushi, aonori and green onions.
