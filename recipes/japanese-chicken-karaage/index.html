Japanese Chicken Karaage

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken and Marinade

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp grated garlic

Coating, Frying, and Serving

  • 3/4 cup potato starch
  • 4 cups neutral oil, for deep frying
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 cup shredded green cabbage
  • 2 tbsp Japanese mayonnaise, optional

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs into 2 inch pieces.

Step 2

Mix the soy sauce, sake, ginger, and garlic, then marinate the chicken for 30 minutes.

Step 3

Drain the chicken and toss in 3/4 cup potato starch until evenly coated.

Step 4

Heat 4 cups oil to 340F in a deep pot.

Step 5

Fry in batches for 3 to 4 minutes until light golden.

Step 6

Rest 4 minutes, then fry again for 1 minute at 360F until deep golden and crisp.

Step 7

Drain well on a wire rack.

Step 8

Serve hot with lemon wedges, cabbage, and mayonnaise.
