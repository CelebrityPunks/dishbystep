Classic Banana Pudding

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Ingredients

  • 5 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 1 box (11 oz) vanilla wafer cookies
  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 3/4 cup sugar, 1/3 cup flour, and 1/4 tsp salt in a saucepan, then whisk in 3 cups milk and 4 egg yolks.

Step 2

Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 8 to 10 minutes until thick and bubbling.

Step 3

Remove from heat and stir in 2 tbsp butter and 1 1/2 tsp vanilla until smooth.

Step 4

Layer vanilla wafers in a 9x13 dish, then add a layer of sliced bananas.

Step 5

Pour one third of the warm pudding over the wafers and bananas.

Step 6

Repeat the layers twice more, ending with pudding on top.

Step 7

Whip 2 cups cream with 3 tbsp powdered sugar and 1 tsp vanilla to soft peaks.

Step 8

Spread the whipped cream over the top, chill 4 hours, then serve cold.
