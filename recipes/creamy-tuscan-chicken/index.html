Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp butter

Ingredients

  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 cup sun-dried tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Fresh basil, for garnish

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Season 4 chicken breasts with 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning.

Step 2

Sear the chicken in 2 tbsp olive oil over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden, then set aside.

Step 3

Melt 3 tbsp butter in the same skillet and cook 3 minced garlic cloves for 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 4

Pour in 1 cup heavy cream and 1/2 cup chicken broth, then simmer gently for 2 minutes.

Step 5

Stir in 1/2 cup grated Parmesan and 1 cup sliced sun-dried tomatoes until the sauce thickens.

Step 6

Wilt 2 cups baby spinach into the sauce with 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes.

Step 7

Return the chicken to the skillet and simmer for 5 minutes until it reaches 165°F.

Step 8

Rest for 5 minutes, garnish with fresh basil, and serve hot over pasta or rice.
