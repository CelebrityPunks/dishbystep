Red Velvet Cake

United States cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dry

  • 2 1/2 cups cake flour
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine salt

Wet

  • 1 cup buttermilk, room temperature
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tbsp red food coloring
  • 1 tsp white vinegar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Frosting

  • 16 oz cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 4 cups powdered sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt

Finish

  • Red velvet crumbs, from leveling the cake layers

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 2 1/2 cups cake flour, 1 1/2 cups sugar, 2 tbsp cocoa, 1 1/2 tsp baking soda and 1 tsp salt.

Step 2

Whisk 1 cup buttermilk, 1 cup oil, 2 eggs, 2 tbsp red food coloring, 1 tsp vinegar and 2 tsp vanilla.

Step 3

Pour the wet mix into the dry and stir until just smooth with no lumps.

Step 4

Divide between two greased 9 inch pans and bake at 350°F for 28 to 32 minutes until a tester comes out clean.

Step 5

Cool 10 minutes in the pans, then turn out and cool fully on a wire rack.

Step 6

Beat 16 oz cream cheese with 1/2 cup butter until fluffy, then whip in 4 cups powdered sugar, vanilla and salt.

Step 7

Level the layers, spread frosting between and over the cake, then press crumbs onto the sides.

Step 8

Chill 30 minutes, slice with a hot dry knife, and serve each slice with extra frosting.
