Thai Pineapple Fried Rice

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 cups day-old jasmine rice
  • 2 cups fresh pineapple chunks
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup roasted cashews
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large wok over high heat and sear 1 lb diced chicken for 4 to 5 minutes until golden, then remove it.

Step 2

Stir fry 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 diced onion and 1 diced red bell pepper for 2 minutes until soft.

Step 3

Add 3 cups day-old jasmine rice, 2 cups pineapple chunks and 1 tbsp curry powder, then toss for 3 minutes until golden.

Step 4

Push the rice aside, scramble 2 beaten eggs for 1 minute, then mix them in.

Step 5

Stir in 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tsp sugar for 1 minute to coat evenly.

Step 6

Return the chicken, fold in 1/2 cup cashews and sliced green onions, and cook for 1 minute.

Step 7

Spoon the rice into a hollowed pineapple half for serving.

Step 8

Serve hot with lime wedges and extra cashews on top.
