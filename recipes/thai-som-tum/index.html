Thai Som Tum

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 medium green papaya, peeled and shredded (about 4 cups)
  • 1 large carrot, shredded
  • 1 cup green beans, cut into 2 inch pieces
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts

Ingredients

  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 Thai bird chilies
  • 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar

Ingredients

  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 1/4 cup extra roasted peanuts
  • 4 large cabbage leaves

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel, seed and shred the green papaya and carrot into thin strips.

Step 2

Pound 3 garlic cloves and 2 Thai chilies in a mortar until fragrant.

Step 3

Stir in 3 tbsp lime juice, 2 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tbsp palm sugar until dissolved.

Step 4

Add 1 cup green beans and 1 cup cherry tomatoes and pound lightly to bruise.

Step 5

Add the shredded papaya and carrot and toss until well coated.

Step 6

Fold in 1/2 cup roasted peanuts and adjust the lime, fish sauce and sugar.

Step 7

Pile the salad onto cabbage leaves on a serving plate.

Step 8

Top with extra peanuts and lime wedges and serve right away.
