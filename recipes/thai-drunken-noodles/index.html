Thai Drunken Noodles

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 12 oz dried flat rice noodles
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 Thai chilies, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • Lime wedges, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 12 oz dried flat rice noodles in warm water for 20 minutes, then drain well.

Step 2

Whisk 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp dark soy sauce, 2 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tbsp sugar.

Step 3

Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat and sear 1 lb sliced chicken for 3-4 minutes.

Step 4

Add 4 sliced garlic cloves and 2 sliced Thai chilies and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.

Step 5

Add 1 sliced onion and 1 sliced bell pepper and stir-fry for 2 minutes until just tender.

Step 6

Add the noodles and sauce and toss for 2-3 minutes until glossy and fully coated.

Step 7

Stir in 1 cup Thai basil leaves until just wilted, about 30 seconds.

Step 8

Serve hot with lime wedges and extra basil on top.
