Classic Lemon Bars

United States cuisine · Serves 16 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2/3 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 350F and line a 9x13 inch pan with parchment paper.

Step 2

Whisk 2 cups flour, 1/2 cup powdered sugar and 1/2 tsp salt in a bowl.

Step 3

Cut in 1 cup cold cubed butter until coarse crumbs form, then press firmly into the pan.

Step 4

Bake the crust for 20 minutes until lightly golden at the edges.

Step 5

Whisk 4 eggs, 1 1/2 cups sugar, 1/4 cup flour, 2/3 cup lemon juice and 1 tbsp lemon zest until smooth.

Step 6

Pour the lemon filling over the hot crust.

Step 7

Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until the center is just set.

Step 8

Cool completely, chill, dust with powdered sugar and slice into 16 bars.
