Basque Burnt Cheesecake

Spain cuisine · Serves 10 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cheesecake Batter

  • 2 lb (900 g) full-fat cream cheese, at room temperature
  • 1 1/2 cups (300 g) granulated sugar
  • 6 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 cups (480 ml) heavy cream
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/3 cup (40 g) all-purpose flour, sifted

To Bake And Serve

  • 9-inch springform pan
  • 2 large sheets parchment paper
  • Fresh berries to serve (optional)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a 9-inch springform pan with two crumpled sheets of parchment.

Step 2

Beat 2 lb room-temp cream cheese with 1 1/2 cups sugar until smooth and fluffy, about 3 minutes.

Step 3

Add 6 eggs one at a time, beating well and scraping the bowl after each.

Step 4

Pour in 2 cups heavy cream, 1 tsp vanilla, and 1 tsp salt; mix until completely smooth.

Step 5

Sift in 1/3 cup flour and fold just until smooth, then pour the batter into the pan.

Step 6

Bake 50 to 55 minutes at 425°F until deeply browned with a jiggly center.

Step 7

Cool to room temperature, then chill at least 4 hours before unmolding.

Step 8

Slice and serve at room temperature, with the center still creamy and custardy.
