Crispy Pork Carnitas Tacos

Mexico cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 lb boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 orange, halved
  • 1/2 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, for crisping

Ingredients

  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 cup salsa verde

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Season 3 lb pork chunks with 1 tbsp salt, 1 tsp pepper, 1 tsp cumin, and 1 tsp oregano.

Step 2

Add the pork to a heavy pot with the quartered onion, 6 garlic cloves, 2 bay leaves, halved orange, 1/2 cup orange juice, and 1 cup water.

Step 3

Simmer covered on low for 2 1/2 to 3 hours, until the pork shreds easily.

Step 4

Shred the pork with two forks and discard the onion, garlic, bay leaves, and orange.

Step 5

Crisp the pork in a hot skillet with 2 tbsp oil, undisturbed, for 3 to 4 minutes per side until the edges turn golden.

Step 6

Warm 12 corn tortillas in a dry skillet for 30 seconds per side and keep them wrapped.

Step 7

Fill each tortilla with crisp pork, diced onion, and chopped cilantro.

Step 8

Serve with lime wedges and salsa verde, and squeeze lime over the top.
