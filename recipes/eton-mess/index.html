Eton Mess

United Kingdom cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Meringues

  • 3 large egg whites (room temperature)
  • 175g caster sugar (superfine sugar)

Strawberry and cream

  • 600g ripe strawberries, hulled
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar (powdered sugar), divided
  • 600ml double cream (heavy cream)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 110°C (225°F) and line a large baking tray with parchment paper.

Step 2

Whisk the 3 egg whites to stiff peaks, then add the 175g caster sugar a spoonful at a time until thick and glossy.

Step 3

Spoon 8 to 10 meringue mounds onto the tray and bake for 1 hour 30 minutes until crisp and dry.

Step 4

Let the meringues cool completely on the tray, then set aside while you prepare the fruit.

Step 5

Hull and halve the 600g strawberries, toss with 1 tbsp of the icing sugar, and rest for 15 minutes.

Step 6

Whip the 600ml double cream with the remaining 1 tbsp icing sugar and 1 tsp vanilla extract to soft peaks.

Step 7

Crush three quarters of the meringues into chunks and fold through the cream with half of the strawberries.

Step 8

Spoon into glasses, top with the remaining strawberries, their syrupy juices and the last meringue shards, then serve right away.
