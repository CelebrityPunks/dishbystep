Cuban Ropa Vieja

Cuba cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb flank steak
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 red + 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin + 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup reserved beef broth
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/2 cup pimento-stuffed green olives, halved

Ingredients

  • Cooked white rice
  • Black beans
  • Sweet plantains (maduros)
  • Fresh cilantro

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cover 2 lb flank steak with water in a large pot with 1 bay leaf and 1 tsp salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently for 2 hours until the beef shreds easily.

Step 2

Lift out the beef, reserve 1 cup of the cooking broth, and shred the meat with two forks into long strands.

Step 3

Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat and soften 1 sliced onion, 1 sliced red pepper and 1 sliced green pepper for 6 minutes.

Step 4

Stir in 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp oregano; cook 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 5

Add 8 oz tomato sauce, 2 tbsp tomato paste and 1 cup of the reserved beef broth. Simmer 5 minutes until slightly thickened.

Step 6

Fold in the shredded beef and 1 tbsp white vinegar; simmer 10 minutes so the sauce clings to every strand.

Step 7

Stir in 1/2 cup halved pimento-stuffed green olives and warm through for 2 minutes.

Step 8

Serve over fluffy white rice with black beans and sweet plantains; scatter fresh cilantro on top.
