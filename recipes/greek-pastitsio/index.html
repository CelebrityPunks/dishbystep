Greek Pastitsio

Greece cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) bucatini, broken in half
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan, divided
  • 1 tbsp salt for the pasta water

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) lean ground beef
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup dry red wine
  • 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp allspice, 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter
  • 3/4 cup (90 g) all-purpose flour
  • 4 cups (1 L) whole milk, warmed
  • 3 large eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup grated kefalotyri or Parmesan
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Boil 1 lb bucatini in salted water until just al dente, about 9 minutes, then drain.

Step 2

Toss the hot bucatini with 2 beaten eggs and 1/2 cup Parmesan until every strand is coated.

Step 3

Brown 1 1/2 lb ground beef with the diced onion and garlic in 2 tbsp olive oil, 8 to 10 minutes.

Step 4

Stir in 2 tbsp tomato paste, the cinnamon, allspice and cloves, then pour in 1/2 cup red wine.

Step 5

Add the crushed tomatoes, 1 cup beef stock and the bay leaf, then simmer 25 minutes until thick.

Step 6

Melt 1/2 cup butter, whisk in 3/4 cup flour, then whisk in 4 cups warm milk until thick, then off the heat whisk in 3 eggs, 1/2 cup Parmesan and the nutmeg.

Step 7

Layer the pasta, meat sauce and bechamel in a buttered dish, then bake at 375 F for 40 minutes until deeply golden.

Step 8

Rest 20 minutes, then slice into clean squares and serve warm.
