Classic Swiss Cheese Fondue

Switzerland cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cheese

  • 8 oz Gruyere cheese, shredded (2 cups)
  • 8 oz Emmentaler cheese, shredded (2 cups)
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch

Pot And Dippers

  • 1 garlic clove, halved
  • 1 1/4 cups dry white wine
  • 1 tbsp kirsch
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of nutmeg and black pepper
  • 1 loaf crusty bread, cubed
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, boiled
  • Cornichons and apple slices

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss the Gruyere, Emmentaler, and cornstarch together in a large bowl.

Step 2

Rub the inside of the pot with the halved garlic clove, then discard it.

Step 3

Warm the wine, kirsch, and lemon juice until steaming but not boiling.

Step 4

Add the cheese a handful at a time, stirring a figure eight until each melts.

Step 5

Keep stirring over medium-low heat until the fondue is smooth, glossy, and gently bubbling.

Step 6

Stir in the nutmeg and black pepper for the classic finish.

Step 7

Move the pot to its stand over a low flame, just enough to keep the cheese fluid.

Step 8

Twist each bread cube in the cheese and serve with potatoes, cornichons, and apple slices.
