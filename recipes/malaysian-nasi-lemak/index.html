Malaysian Nasi Lemak

Malaysia cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken & Sides

  • 8 chicken wings
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1/2 cup dried anchovies (ikan bilis)
  • 1/2 cup raw peanuts
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced
  • Banana leaves, for serving
  • Neutral oil, for frying

Coconut Rice

  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 1/2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 3 pandan leaves, knotted
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt

Sambal

  • 10 dried red chilies, soaked
  • 4 shallots
  • 1 red onion, quartered
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp belacan (shrimp paste)
  • 1/4 cup neutral oil
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tamarind juice
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rinse 2 cups rice; simmer with coconut milk, water, pandan, lemongrass and salt for 15 to 18 minutes until fluffy.

Step 2

Soak 10 dried chilies; blend with shallots, red onion, garlic and belacan into a smooth paste.

Step 3

Fry the paste in 1/4 cup oil 5 minutes; add the sliced onion, tamarind juice, sugar and salt; simmer 15 minutes into a deep-red sambal.

Step 4

Rub 8 wings with turmeric, salt and white pepper; rest 15 minutes.

Step 5

Shallow-fry the wings in hot oil 6 to 7 minutes per side until deep golden and cooked through.

Step 6

Crisp the anchovies and peanuts in hot oil about 3 minutes; drain on paper.

Step 7

Boil 4 eggs for 9 minutes; cool, peel and halve.

Step 8

Mound the coconut rice on banana leaves; fan with wings, sambal, egg, cucumber, peanuts and anchovies, then serve.
