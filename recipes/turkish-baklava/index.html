Turkish Baklava

Turkey cuisine · Serves 24 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Baklava

  • 1 lb phyllo dough (about 40 sheets), thawed overnight in the fridge
  • 2 1/2 cups finely chopped pistachios
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted

For The Syrup

  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Melt 1 cup butter and keep the phyllo sheets covered with a damp towel.

Step 2

Layer 10 phyllo sheets in a 9x13 pan, brushing each with melted butter.

Step 3

Spread half of the 2 1/2 cups chopped pistachios over the buttered phyllo.

Step 4

Layer 5 more buttered phyllo sheets and top with the rest of the pistachios.

Step 5

Cover with the remaining phyllo, buttering each sheet, then cut into diamonds.

Step 6

Bake at 325°F for 45-50 minutes until deep golden and crisp.

Step 7

Simmer 1 1/2 cups sugar, 1 1/2 cups water, and 1 tbsp lemon juice for 10 minutes.

Step 8

Pour the warm syrup over the hot baklava, cool 4 hours, then serve.
