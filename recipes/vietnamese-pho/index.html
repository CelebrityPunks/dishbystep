Vietnamese Pho Bo

Vietnam cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Broth

  • 3 lb beef marrow and knuckle bones
  • 1 lb beef brisket
  • 2 yellow onions, halved
  • 1 (4-inch) piece fresh ginger, halved
  • 2 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 4 whole star anise
  • 1 (3-inch) cinnamon stick
  • 1 tbsp fennel seeds
  • 4 quarts (16 cups) water
  • 1/4 cup fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Noodles + Toppings

  • 12 oz dried flat rice noodles (banh pho)
  • 1 lb beef sirloin, sliced paper thin
  • 6 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups fresh bean sprouts
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 1 jalapeno, thinly sliced
  • Hoisin sauce and sriracha, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Char 2 halved onions and a 4-inch piece of ginger in a dry skillet, 5 minutes per side, until blackened.

Step 2

Toast 2 tbsp coriander seeds, 4 cloves, 4 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick, and 1 tbsp fennel seeds for 1 minute.

Step 3

Parboil 3 lb beef bones for 10 minutes, then rinse and return to the pot with 1 lb brisket.

Step 4

Add 4 quarts water, the charred aromatics, and toasted spices. Simmer gently for 6 hours, skimming.

Step 5

Stir in 1/4 cup fish sauce, 1 tbsp salt, and 1 tbsp sugar. Soak 12 oz rice noodles in hot water 10 minutes.

Step 6

Blanch the noodles 1 minute, divide into 6 bowls, and top with sliced brisket and paper-thin raw sirloin.

Step 7

Ladle the boiling broth over each bowl so the raw beef cooks through.

Step 8

Top with basil, sprouts, lime, scallions, and chilies. Serve steaming hot.
