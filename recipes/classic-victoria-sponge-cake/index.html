Classic Victoria Sponge Cake

United Kingdom cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Sponge

  • 200g unsalted butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Filling & Finish

  • 200g seedless strawberry jam
  • 200ml double cream, chilled
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Beat 200g butter with 200g caster sugar until pale and fluffy, then beat in 4 eggs one at a time.

Step 2

Fold in 200g flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 2 tbsp milk and 1 tsp vanilla until just smooth.

Step 3

Divide the batter between two lined 8-inch tins. Heat the oven to 190C (375F).

Step 4

Bake 20 to 25 minutes until golden and springy.

Step 5

Cool 10 minutes in the tins, then turn out onto a rack to cool fully.

Step 6

Whip 200ml double cream to soft peaks.

Step 7

Spread 200g jam on one sponge, add the cream, then sandwich with the second sponge.

Step 8

Dust with icing sugar, slice, and serve.
