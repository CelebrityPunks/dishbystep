Lebanese Shish Tawook

Lebanon cuisine · Serves 4 · Makes 8 skewers · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chicken and marinade

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1/2 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

Toum (garlic sauce)

  • 1/2 cup peeled garlic cloves (about 2 heads)
  • 1 1/2 cups neutral oil, chilled
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 1/2 tsp fine sea salt

To serve

  • 4 large pita breads, warmed
  • 1 cup pickled turnips or dill pickles
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 lemons, halved

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cut 2 lb chicken thighs into 1 1/2-inch cubes and pat dry.

Step 2

Whisk the yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, tomato paste, oil, vinegar, and all the spices.

Step 3

Coat the chicken, cover, and marinate 2 hours or overnight in the fridge.

Step 4

Blend the garlic, salt, and lemon juice, then drizzle in the oil until fluffy toum forms.

Step 5

Thread the marinated chicken snugly onto 8 skewers.

Step 6

Grill over medium-high heat 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until charred and 165F inside.

Step 7

Rest 5 minutes. Grill the lemon halves cut-side down for 2 minutes.

Step 8

Serve on warm pita with toum, pickles, parsley, and grilled lemon.
