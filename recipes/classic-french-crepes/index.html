Classic French Crepes

France cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) whole milk
  • 2 tbsp (25 g) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter, melted

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (to grease the pan)
  • Nutella, sliced strawberries, powdered sugar, lemon wedges (to serve)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk the flour, sugar, and salt in a large bowl until combined.

Step 2

Whisk in the eggs and milk until the batter is completely smooth.

Step 3

Whisk in the vanilla and the melted butter, then rest the batter 10 minutes.

Step 4

Heat a 10-inch nonstick pan over medium heat and grease lightly with oil.

Step 5

Pour 1/4 cup of batter into the pan and swirl to coat the bottom evenly.

Step 6

Cook 1 to 1 1/2 minutes until the edges lift and turn lightly golden.

Step 7

Flip, cook 20 to 30 seconds more, then slide onto a warm plate. Repeat with the remaining batter.

Step 8

Fill with Nutella and strawberries, fold, dust with powdered sugar, and serve.
