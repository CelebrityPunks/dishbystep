Silky French Lemon Tart

France cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 1 1/4 cups (160 g)
  • Powdered sugar - 1/4 cup (30 g)
  • Fine salt - 1/4 tsp
  • Cold unsalted butter, cubed - 1/2 cup (115 g)
  • Large egg yolk - 1
  • Ice water - 1 to 2 tbsp

Ingredients

  • Fresh lemon juice - 1/2 cup (120 ml), 2 to 3 lemons
  • Lemon zest - 1 tbsp
  • Granulated sugar - 3/4 cup (150 g)
  • Large eggs - 2, plus 2 yolks
  • Unsalted butter, cubed - 1/2 cup (115 g)
  • Pinch of salt

Ingredients

  • Lightly sweetened whipped cream
  • Fresh berries
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub 1/2 cup cold butter into 1 1/4 cups flour, 1/4 cup powdered sugar, and 1/4 tsp salt until sandy.

Step 2

Mix in 1 egg yolk and 1 to 2 tbsp ice water, then chill the dough 30 minutes.

Step 3

Blind bake at 375°F for 18 to 20 minutes with weights, then 8 to 10 minutes more until golden.

Step 4

Whisk 2 eggs, 2 yolks, 3/4 cup sugar, 1 tbsp zest, and 1/2 cup lemon juice in a heatproof bowl.

Step 5

Cook over simmering water, whisking constantly, until thick enough to coat a spoon, about 10 minutes.

Step 6

Strain the curd, whisk in 1/2 cup butter until silky, then pour into the cooled shell.

Step 7

Chill the tart at least 4 hours until the curd is fully set.

Step 8

Slice and serve with whipped cream, fresh berries, and a dusting of powdered sugar.
