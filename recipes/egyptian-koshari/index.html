Egyptian Koshari

Egypt cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Base

  • 1 cup brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 cup long-grain rice
  • 1 cup elbow macaroni
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil, for frying onions
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour, for the onions

For The Daqqa Dressing

  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin

For The Tomato Sauce

  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 2 thinly sliced onions with 1 tbsp flour and fry in 1/4 cup hot oil until deep golden, 12 to 15 minutes; drain.

Step 2

Simmer 1 cup rinsed brown lentils in 4 cups water for 20 minutes until just tender, then drain.

Step 3

Cook 1 cup rice in 2 cups salted water for 15 minutes, then fold in the cooked lentils.

Step 4

Boil 1 cup elbow macaroni until al dente, 7 to 8 minutes; drain and toss with 1 tbsp oil.

Step 5

Saute 4 cloves garlic in 2 tbsp oil for 1 minute, then simmer 28 oz crushed tomatoes with tomato paste, cumin, coriander and pepper flakes for 12 to 15 minutes.

Step 6

Warm 15 oz chickpeas with a splash of water and a pinch of cumin, and whisk the vinegar, water, garlic and cumin into the daqqa dressing.

Step 7

Layer the rice and lentils, macaroni and chickpeas in wide bowls; spoon over the tomato sauce.

Step 8

Top with a heap of crispy fried onions, drizzle with the daqqa dressing, and serve.
