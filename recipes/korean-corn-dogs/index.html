Korean Corn Dogs

South Korea cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Batter

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup cold milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups panko breadcrumbs

Fillings & finish

  • 4 hot dogs, halved
  • 4 low-moisture mozzarella sticks, halved
  • 6 wooden skewers
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • Granulated sugar, for rolling
  • Ketchup, mustard, and spicy mayo, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Thread half a hot dog and half a mozzarella stick onto each of 6 wooden skewers.

Step 2

Whisk 1 cup flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, and 1/2 tsp salt.

Step 3

Stir in 3/4 cup cold milk and 1 egg until thick and smooth; chill 10 minutes.

Step 4

Heat 3 inches of oil to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C) over medium-high heat.

Step 5

Dip each skewer in batter, then roll in 2 cups panko, pressing gently so it sticks.

Step 6

Fry two at a time for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until deep golden and crisp.

Step 7

Drain on a rack for 1 minute, then roll each hot corn dog in granulated sugar.

Step 8

Serve hot with ketchup, mustard, and spicy mayo for dipping.
