Singapore Chilli Crab

Singapore cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

The Crab

  • 1 large mud crab, 2.6 lb (1.2 kg)

Chilli Paste

  • 8 dried red chillies, soaked
  • 4 fresh red chillies
  • 8 shallots, peeled
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 1/2 in ginger, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

The Sauce

  • 1/2 cup tomato ketchup
  • 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil

The Finish

  • 2 tbsp cornstarch + 3 tbsp water
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Fried mantou buns

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Clean the crab: pull off the top shell, discard the gills, quarter the body and crack the claws.

Step 2

Blend the soaked chillies, fresh chillies, shallots, garlic and ginger with 2 tbsp oil into a smooth paste.

Step 3

Fry the chilli paste in 1/4 cup hot oil for 3 to 4 minutes until fragrant and deep red.

Step 4

Stir in the ketchup, sweet chilli sauce, chicken stock, sugar and salt, then bring to a lively simmer.

Step 5

Add the crab pieces, cover and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until the shells turn bright red.

Step 6

Stir in the cornstarch slurry and simmer 1 minute until the sauce turns glossy and thick.

Step 7

Drizzle in the beaten eggs, stirring slowly to form silky ribbons, about 1 minute.

Step 8

Plate the crab with plenty of sauce, scatter scallions and cilantro, and serve with fried mantou buns.
