Beef Rendang

Indonesia cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Spice Paste

  • 8 dried red chilies, deseeded
  • 6 shallots, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 1/2 in ginger, sliced
  • 1 1/2 in galangal, sliced
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, sliced
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ground coriander

For The Braise

  • 2.2 lb beef chuck, 2 in cubes
  • 3 1/2 cups coconut milk
  • 4 kaffir lime leaves, 2 bay leaves
  • 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cloves, 2 star anise
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp palm sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup toasted desiccated coconut

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Blend the chilies, shallots, garlic, ginger, galangal, lemongrass, turmeric, coriander, and 3 tbsp water into a smooth paste.

Step 2

Toast the desiccated coconut in a dry pan until deep golden, then grind it into a paste.

Step 3

Bring the coconut milk, spice paste, lime leaves, bay leaves, cinnamon, cloves, and star anise to a boil.

Step 4

Add the beef and simmer gently 2 hours, stirring every 15 minutes, until the beef is tender.

Step 5

Stir in the tamarind paste, palm sugar, salt, and toasted coconut paste.

Step 6

Keep cooking uncovered until the sauce thickens and turns dark brown, about 45 minutes.

Step 7

Cook until the oil separates and the rendang is dark, glossy, and nearly dry; discard the whole spices.

Step 8

Serve the rendang hot over steamed jasmine rice.
