Thai Mango Sticky Rice

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Sticky Rice

  • 1 1/2 cups (300 g) glutinous sweet rice

Coconut Rice

  • 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) full-fat coconut milk, divided
  • 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt, plus a pinch for the sauce

To Finish

  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 1 tsp rice flour
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 1 1/2 cups glutinous sticky rice in plenty of water for at least 4 hours or overnight, then drain well.

Step 2

Steam the drained rice in a cheesecloth-lined steamer over boiling water, covered, 20 to 25 minutes until tender and translucent.

Step 3

Gently simmer 1 cup coconut milk with 1/2 cup sugar and 1/2 tsp salt for 2 to 3 minutes until the sugar dissolves. Do not boil.

Step 4

Pour the hot sweet coconut milk over the hot rice, fold gently, cover, and rest 15 minutes so it absorbs.

Step 5

For the sauce, simmer the remaining 1/4 cup coconut milk with a pinch of salt and 1 tsp rice flour for 1 to 2 minutes until lightly thickened.

Step 6

Peel the 2 mangoes and slice into thin strips or cheeks.

Step 7

Scoop the sticky rice into serving bowls and fan the mango slices beside it.

Step 8

Drizzle with the salted coconut sauce, sprinkle toasted sesame seeds, and serve warm.
