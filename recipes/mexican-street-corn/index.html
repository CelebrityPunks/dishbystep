Mexican Street Corn

Mexico cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Corn

  • 4 ears fresh corn, husked
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

Sauce And Topping

  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp chili powder, plus more for dusting
  • 1/2 cup cotija cheese, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Preheat the grill to medium-high heat, about 400°F.

Step 2

Pull off the husks and silks from 4 ears of corn.

Step 3

Rub the corn with 2 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt.

Step 4

Grill, turning every 2 minutes, until charred in spots, about 10 minutes.

Step 5

Whisk 1/4 cup mayonnaise, 1/4 cup crema, minced garlic, and 1 tsp chili powder.

Step 6

Brush each hot ear generously with the creamy chili sauce.

Step 7

Sprinkle 1/2 cup crumbled cotija over each ear so it sticks.

Step 8

Finish with 1/4 cup cilantro, a dust of chili powder, and lime wedges. Serve warm.
