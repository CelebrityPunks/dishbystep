Classic Sticky Toffee Pudding

United Kingdom cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Sponge

  • 225g medjool dates, stoned and chopped
  • 175ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 85g butter, softened, plus extra for greasing
  • 140g demerara sugar
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 tbsp black treacle
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 100ml milk

For The Toffee Sauce

  • 115g butter
  • 115g light muscovado sugar
  • 140ml double cream

To Serve

  • Vanilla ice cream

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Put 225g chopped medjool dates in a bowl, pour over 175ml boiling water, and leave 30 minutes to soak and cool.

Step 2

Mash the soaked dates with a fork until pulpy, then stir in 1 tsp vanilla extract.

Step 3

Beat 85g softened butter with 140g demerara sugar until pale and creamy, then beat in the 2 eggs one at a time.

Step 4

Beat in 2 tbsp black treacle, then gently fold in 175g self-raising flour and 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda.

Step 5

Stir the date mash and 100ml milk into the batter until evenly combined.

Step 6

Pour into a buttered 20cm square tin and bake at 180C (350F) for 30-35 minutes until risen and firm.

Step 7

Gently heat 115g butter, 115g light muscovado sugar and 140ml double cream until dissolved, then bubble 2-3 minutes until thick and glossy.

Step 8

Prick the warm pudding all over, pour in half the warm sauce, and serve with vanilla ice cream and the rest of the sauce.
