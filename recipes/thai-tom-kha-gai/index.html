Creamy Thai Tom Kha Gai

Thailand cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Aromatics and Broth

  • 2 cans (13.5 oz each) full-fat coconut milk, about 3 1/2 cups
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 stalks lemongrass, cut into 2-inch pieces and bruised
  • 1 (3-inch) piece galangal, peeled and sliced
  • 6 kaffir lime leaves, torn in half

Chicken and Vegetables

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into thin bite-size strips
  • 8 oz sliced mushrooms (cremini or oyster)
  • 2 Thai bird chilies, thinly sliced

Seasoning and Garnish

  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp fresh lime juice (about 1 lime)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro leaves, for garnish
  • Lime wedges, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Simmer 3 1/2 cups coconut milk, 2 cups broth, lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves for 5 minutes.

Step 2

Add 1 lb thinly sliced chicken and simmer 5 to 6 minutes until cooked through.

Step 3

Stir in 8 oz sliced mushrooms and cook 2 minutes until tender.

Step 4

Add the sliced chilies, 3 tbsp fish sauce and 1 tsp sugar; stir well.

Step 5

Take off the heat and stir in 2 tbsp fresh lime juice.

Step 6

Fish out the lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves.

Step 7

Ladle the hot soup into warm bowls.

Step 8

Garnish with fresh cilantro and a lime wedge, then serve.
