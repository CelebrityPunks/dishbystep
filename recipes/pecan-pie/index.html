Classic Pecan Pie

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

CRUST

  • 1 (9-inch) unbaked pie crust, thawed if frozen

THE FILLING

  • 1 cup light corn syrup
  • 3/4 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 3 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 1/2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups pecan halves

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Press 1 (9-inch) pie crust into a 9-inch pie dish and crimp the edges.

Step 2

Whisk 1 cup corn syrup, 3/4 cup brown sugar, 1/3 cup melted butter, 3 eggs, vanilla and salt until smooth.

Step 3

Fold in 2 cups pecan halves until every piece is coated.

Step 4

Pour the filling into the unbaked pie crust.

Step 5

Arrange a ring of extra pecan halves on top for a bakery finish.

Step 6

Bake at 350F (175C) for 55 to 60 minutes, until the center is just set.

Step 7

Cool on a rack at least 2 hours so the filling firms up.

Step 8

Slice and serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
