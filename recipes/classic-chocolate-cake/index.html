Classic Chocolate Cake

United States cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup boiling water

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 3/4 cups flour, 3/4 cup cocoa, 2 cups sugar, 1 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1 1/2 tsp baking soda, and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl.

Step 2

Whisk in 2 eggs, 1 cup milk, 1/2 cup oil, and 2 tsp vanilla until smooth.

Step 3

Stir in 1 cup boiling water until the batter is thin and glossy.

Step 4

Divide the batter between two greased 9-inch round pans and bake at 350 degrees F for 30 to 35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

Step 5

Cool the layers for 10 minutes in the pans, then turn out and cool completely.

Step 6

Beat 1 cup butter, 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar, 1/2 cup cocoa, 1/3 cup cream, 2 tsp vanilla, and a pinch of salt until silky.

Step 7

Frost the first layer, stack the second layer, then spread frosting over the top and sides.

Step 8

Slice the cake and serve at room temperature.
