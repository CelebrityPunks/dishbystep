Japanese Tuna-Mayo Onigiri

Japan cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 cups short-grain Japanese rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (5 oz each) tuna in water, drained
  • 3 tbsp Japanese mayonnaise
  • 2 green onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 6 nori sheets
  • 2 tbsp pickled ginger, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rinse 2 cups short-grain rice until the water runs clear, then add 2 cups water and cook until tender.

Step 2

Rest the rice 10 min covered, then fold in 1/2 tsp salt while warm.

Step 3

Drain 2 cans of tuna and mix with 3 tbsp Japanese mayo, 1 tsp soy sauce and the green onions.

Step 4

Wet your hands with salted water and press 1/2 cup rice into a loose ball.

Step 5

Poke a deep pocket in each ball and tuck in 2 tbsp of the tuna-mayo filling.

Step 6

Close the rice around the filling and mold each ball into a neat triangle.

Step 7

Wrap a strip of nori around the base of each triangle.

Step 8

Serve right away with pickled ginger alongside.
