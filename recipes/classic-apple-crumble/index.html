Classic Apple Crumble

United States cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 6 medium apples, about 2 lb
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • Pinch of salt

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • Vanilla ice cream, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel, core, and slice 6 apples into thin wedges.

Step 2

Toss the apples with 1/2 cup sugar, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, 1 tbsp cornstarch, and a pinch of salt.

Step 3

Tip the apples into a buttered baking dish and spread them out evenly.

Step 4

Rub 1 cup flour, 1 cup oats, 3/4 cup brown sugar, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/2 cup cold cubed butter into coarse crumbs.

Step 5

Scatter the crumble topping evenly over the apples.

Step 6

Bake at 375°F for 40 to 45 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Step 7

Rest the crumble for 10 minutes so the juices settle.

Step 8

Serve warm scoops with vanilla ice cream.
