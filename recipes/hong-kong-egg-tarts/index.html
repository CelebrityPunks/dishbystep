Hong Kong Egg Tarts

Hong Kong cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cookie Crust

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour (160 g)
  • 1/3 cup powdered sugar (40 g)
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter (113 g), cubed
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp ice water

Silky Custard

  • 1/2 cup hot water (120 ml)
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar (100 g)
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk (120 ml)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub the cold butter into the flour mixture until crumbly, then mix in the egg yolk and ice water to form a dough.

Step 2

Press the dough evenly into 12 lined tart pans and chill 30 minutes.

Step 3

Stir the sugar into 1/2 cup hot water until dissolved, then mix in the evaporated milk and vanilla.

Step 4

Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt, whisk into the milk mixture, and strain it twice.

Step 5

Fill each chilled shell two-thirds full with the strained custard.

Step 6

Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes until the custard is just set.

Step 7

Cool 10 minutes in the pans, then unmold the tarts.

Step 8

Serve warm with hot tea and enjoy the silky, jiggly custard.
