Hearty Classic Beef Chili

United States cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Ground beef, 80/20 - 2 lb
  • Yellow onion, diced - 1 large
  • Red bell pepper, diced - 1
  • Garlic, minced - 4 cloves
  • Olive oil - 1 tbsp

Ingredients

  • Chili powder - 3 tbsp
  • Ground cumin - 2 tsp
  • Smoked paprika - 1 tsp
  • Dried oregano - 1 tsp
  • Tomato paste - 2 tbsp
  • Crushed tomatoes - 1 can (28 oz)
  • Kidney beans, drained - 2 cans (15 oz each)
  • Beef stock - 1 cup
  • Brown sugar - 1 tsp
  • Fine salt - 1 1/2 tsp
  • Black pepper - 1/2 tsp

Ingredients

  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Sour cream
  • Sliced green onions
  • Lime wedges

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot, brown 2 lb ground beef for 8 minutes, then drain the fat.

Step 2

Cook 1 diced onion, 1 diced bell pepper, and 4 minced garlic cloves until soft, about 5 minutes.

Step 3

Stir in 2 tbsp tomato paste, 3 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 1 tsp oregano for 1 minute.

Step 4

Add 28 oz crushed tomatoes, 2 cans kidney beans, 1 cup beef stock, 1 tsp brown sugar, salt, and pepper, then bring to a boil.

Step 5

Cover, reduce to low, and simmer gently for 30 minutes, stirring now and then.

Step 6

Uncover and simmer 15 minutes more until thick and rich.

Step 7

Taste and adjust the salt, then squeeze in lime juice for brightness.

Step 8

Ladle into bowls and top with cheddar, sour cream, green onions, and lime wedges.
