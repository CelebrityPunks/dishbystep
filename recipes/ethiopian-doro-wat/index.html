Ethiopian Doro Wat

Ethiopia cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Berbere Sauce

  • 4 cups finely chopped red onion (about 4 large)
  • 1 cup niter kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced clarified butter)
  • 1/4 cup berbere spice blend
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 1/2 tsp fine sea salt

Chicken And Eggs

  • 3 lb bone-in skinless chicken legs (about 8 pieces)
  • 6 large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled

To Serve

  • 8 to 10 pieces injera flatbread

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cook 4 cups finely chopped red onion in 2 tbsp oil until deep golden, about 15 minutes.

Step 2

Stir in 1/4 cup berbere, 1 cup niter kibbeh, and 2 tbsp tomato paste. Cook 2 minutes until fragrant.

Step 3

Pour in 2 cups chicken stock and simmer gently for 15 minutes until slightly thickened.

Step 4

Add 3 lb chicken legs, 1 tbsp minced garlic, and 1 tbsp grated ginger. Cover and simmer 25 minutes.

Step 5

Stir in 6 peeled hard-boiled eggs and simmer uncovered 10 more minutes until the sauce clings.

Step 6

Taste, adjust salt, and rest 5 minutes off the heat so the flavors settle.

Step 7

Ladle the stew over injera and arrange the eggs on top with plenty of sauce.

Step 8

Serve family-style with extra injera for scooping.
