Brazilian Acai Bowl

Brazil cuisine · Serves 2 bowls · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Base

  • 2 packets (7 oz / 200 g) frozen unsweetened acai puree
  • 2 small frozen bananas, sliced
  • 1/2 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1/4 cup coconut water
  • 1 Tbsp honey (optional)

Toppings

  • 1/2 cup granola
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1/2 cup strawberries, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp shredded coconut
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • Honey, for drizzling

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Run the frozen acai packets under warm water for 5 seconds; break the puree into pieces.

Step 2

Slice the bananas; keep half for topping and freeze the rest overnight if not frozen yet.

Step 3

Add the acai pieces, frozen banana, frozen berries and coconut water to a blender.

Step 4

Blend, adding coconut water a splash at a time, until thick like soft-serve ice cream.

Step 5

Spoon the thick blend into two chilled bowls.

Step 6

Arrange the granola, sliced banana, strawberries, coconut and chia seeds over the bowls in rows.

Step 7

Drizzle honey over the top of each bowl.

Step 8

Serve immediately with a long spoon.
