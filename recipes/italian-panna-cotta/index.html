Silky Vanilla Panna Cotta

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500ml double cream (heavy cream)
  • 100ml whole milk
  • 80g caster sugar
  • 1 vanilla pod, split and scraped (or 1 tsp vanilla extract)
  • 7g unflavored powdered gelatin (1 packet)
  • 60ml cold water, for blooming

Ingredients

  • 250g fresh raspberries
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Ingredients

  • Fresh raspberries
  • Small mint sprigs

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Sprinkle 7g powdered gelatin over 60ml cold water and let it bloom for 5 minutes.

Step 2

Warm 500ml cream, 100ml milk, 80g sugar and the vanilla seeds in a saucepan until the sugar dissolves. Do not boil.

Step 3

Take the pan off the heat and whisk in the bloomed gelatin until it melts completely.

Step 4

Strain through a fine sieve, then pour the mixture evenly into 4 lightly oiled molds.

Step 5

Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight, until fully set.

Step 6

Simmer 250g raspberries with 2 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp lemon juice for 3 minutes, then strain smooth.

Step 7

Dip each mold briefly in hot water, then invert the panna cotta onto a plate.

Step 8

Spoon over the raspberry coulis and finish with fresh raspberries and mint.
