Classic Cacio e Pepe

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Pasta

  • 1 lb spaghetti or tonnarelli
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt for the pasta water

For The Sauce

  • 2 cups (5 oz) finely grated Pecorino Romano
  • 2 tsp whole black peppercorns, coarsely cracked
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil and cook 1 lb spaghetti until very al dente, 1 minute less than the package says.

Step 2

Crack 2 tsp black peppercorns coarsely and toast them in a large dry skillet over medium heat until fragrant, about 2 minutes.

Step 3

Before draining the pasta, scoop out and reserve 2 cups of the starchy pasta water.

Step 4

Pour 1 cup of the hot pasta water into the skillet and swirl it with the toasted pepper.

Step 5

Add the drained spaghetti to the skillet and toss in the peppery water for 1 minute over medium-low heat.

Step 6

Take the skillet off the heat and shower in half the pecorino, tossing constantly so it melts into a creamy sauce.

Step 7

Add the remaining pecorino and 1 tbsp butter, tossing and adding more pasta water until the sauce is silky and coats every strand.

Step 8

Twirl into warm bowls, finish with extra pecorino and cracked pepper, and serve immediately.
