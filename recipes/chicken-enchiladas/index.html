Chicken Enchiladas

Mexico cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Assembly & Topping

  • 8 flour tortillas (8-inch), warmed
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion
  • Chopped cilantro, to serve
  • Sour cream, to serve

Filling

  • 3 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 4 oz diced green chiles, drained

Sauce

  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 3 tbsp chili powder, 1/2 tsp cumin, the flour and seasonings into the hot oil, then stir in 2 cups broth and the tomato sauce.

Step 2

Stir 3 cups shredded chicken, 1 cup Monterey Jack, 1/2 cup sour cream and the green chiles together.

Step 3

Warm the 8 flour tortillas in a dry skillet so they roll without cracking.

Step 4

Spread 3 tbsp filling down each tortilla and roll tightly.

Step 5

Spread 1/2 cup sauce in the baking dish and nestle the rolls seam-side down.

Step 6

Pour over the remaining sauce and scatter 1 cup Monterey Jack on top.

Step 7

Bake at 375F for 20-25 min until the cheese bubbles and browns.

Step 8

Rest 5 min, then serve with red onion, cilantro and sour cream.
