Mexican Chilaquiles Rojos

Mexico cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Chips And Toppings

  • 12 corn tortillas, cut into 8 triangles
  • 1 cup vegetable oil, for frying
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup crema mexicana
  • 3/4 cup crumbled queso fresco
  • 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced

Salsa Roja

  • 2 lb Roma tomatoes (about 8), halved
  • 1/2 white onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 2 dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 1 serrano chile, stemmed
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp dried Mexican oregano

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Fry the tortilla triangles in 1 cup hot oil until golden and crisp, about 2 minutes per side.

Step 2

Simmer the tomatoes, onion, garlic, guajillo, and serrano for 10 minutes until soft.

Step 3

Blend the softened vegetables with 1 cup broth, 1 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp oregano until smooth.

Step 4

Simmer the salsa 5 minutes until slightly thickened and brick-red.

Step 5

Toss the crisp chips into the hot salsa just until coated, about 1 minute.

Step 6

Fry 4 eggs sunny-side up until the whites set and the yolks stay runny.

Step 7

Top with 1/2 cup crema, queso fresco, red onion, cilantro, and avocado.

Step 8

Serve at once while the chips are still a little crisp.
