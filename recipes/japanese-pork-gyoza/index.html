Japanese Pork Gyoza

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Filling

  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1 cup finely chopped green cabbage
  • 2 green onions, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp sake (optional)
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Wrapping, Frying & Dipping

  • 30 round gyoza wrappers
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt (for the cabbage)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, per batch
  • 1/4 cup water, per batch
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, per batch
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp la-yu chili oil (optional)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Salt the chopped cabbage with 1/2 tsp salt, rest 10 minutes, then squeeze out every drop of water.

Step 2

Mix the pork, cabbage, green onions, ginger, garlic, sake, sesame oil, soy sauce, and pepper until sticky.

Step 3

Place 1 tbsp filling in the center of a wrapper and wet the edge with a little water.

Step 4

Fold over and crimp 5 to 6 pleats along the edge, pressing firmly to seal.

Step 5

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high, set the gyoza flat side down, and fry 2 to 3 minutes until golden.

Step 6

Pour in 1/4 cup water, cover at once, and steam 3 to 4 minutes until the wrappers turn translucent.

Step 7

Uncover, drizzle in 1 tsp sesame oil, and crisp 1 to 2 minutes until the bottoms are deeply browned.

Step 8

Stir the dipping sauce and serve the gyoza crisp side up with steamed rice.
