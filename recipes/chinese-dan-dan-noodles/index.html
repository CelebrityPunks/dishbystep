Dan Dan Noodles

China cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 12 oz dried Chinese wheat noodles
  • 2 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise

Ingredients

  • 8 oz ground pork
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup preserved mustard greens (yacai), rinsed
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Chinese sesame paste
  • 1/4 cup light soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup Chinese black vinegar
  • 1/2 cup chili oil with sediment
  • 2 tsp ground Sichuan pepper
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup hot noodle water

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 8 oz ground pork with 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine and 1 tbsp light soy sauce; rest 15 minutes.

Step 2

Whisk 1/2 cup sesame paste, 1/4 cup soy sauce, 1/4 cup black vinegar, 1/2 cup chili oil, 2 tsp Sichuan pepper and 1 tsp sugar; divide among 4 bowls.

Step 3

Sear the pork with 1/4 cup yacai, 2 garlic cloves and 1 tsp ginger until browned and crisp, 5 to 6 minutes.

Step 4

Boil 12 oz wheat noodles 3 to 4 minutes; blanch 2 baby bok choy in the last minute.

Step 5

Ladle 1/4 cup hot noodle water into each bowl and stir the sauce silky smooth.

Step 6

Twirl the drained noodles into the bowls right over the sauce.

Step 7

Top with the pork, bok choy, 1/4 cup crushed peanuts and 2 sliced scallions.

Step 8

Toss everything together at the table and slurp while it is hot.
