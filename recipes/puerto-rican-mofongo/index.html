Classic Puerto Rican Mofongo

Puerto Rico cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Garlic Butter Shrimp

  • 0.25 tsp black pepper
  • 0.25 tsp fine sea salt
  • 0.25 cup fresh cilantro (chopped)
  • 3 garlic cloves (thinly sliced)
  • 1 lb large shrimp (peeled and deveined) [450g]
  • 1 lime (cut into wedges)
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter

Mofongo

  • 1 cup canola oil (for frying)
  • 0.5 lb chicharrones (broken into small pieces) [225g]
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 6 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 4 large green plantains (peeled and cut into 1 1/2-inch rounds)
  • 0.5 cup pork or chicken broth (warmed)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Peel 4 green plantains and cut them into 1 1/2-inch rounds.

Step 2

Fry the plantains in 1 cup hot canola oil, 5-6 minutes, until golden.

Step 3

Mash the fried plantains in a mortar with 6 minced garlic cloves and 1 tsp salt.

Step 4

Fold in 1/2 lb crushed chicharrones, 3 tbsp olive oil, and 1/2 cup warm broth.

Step 5

Pack the mixture into a small bowl and unmold to make firm domes.

Step 6

Sear 1 lb shrimp in 4 tbsp butter with 3 sliced garlic cloves, 2 minutes per side.

Step 7

Spoon the shrimp and garlicky butter over the mofongo domes.

Step 8

Shower with 1/4 cup chopped cilantro, add lime wedges, and serve warm.
