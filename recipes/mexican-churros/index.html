Mexican Churros

Mexico cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Churro Dough

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 cups vegetable oil, for frying

Coating And Chocolate

  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 oz semi-sweet chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Bring 1 cup water, 1/2 cup butter, 1 tbsp sugar, and 1/2 tsp salt to a boil.

Step 2

Stir in 1 cup flour all at once and cook for 2 min until the dough pulls away from the pan.

Step 3

Cool the dough 5 min, then beat in 2 eggs one at a time plus 1 tsp vanilla until smooth.

Step 4

Pipe 6-inch ridged strips of dough onto a parchment-lined tray.

Step 5

Melt 4 oz chocolate with 3/4 cup hot cream, 1 tbsp butter, and 1/2 tsp vanilla until glossy.

Step 6

Fry in 375°F oil for 3-4 min per batch until deeply golden, turning once.

Step 7

Lift the churros out and drain them on a wire rack for 2 min.

Step 8

Toss the warm churros in cinnamon sugar and serve at once with the chocolate.
