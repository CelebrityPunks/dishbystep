Indian Rasmalai

India cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 litres whole milk
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 cup sugar

Ingredients

  • 1 litre whole milk
  • 6 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch saffron strands
  • 5 green cardamom pods, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped pistachios
  • 1 tbsp chopped almonds

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Boil 2 litres whole milk, then stir in 3 tbsp lemon juice until it curdles.

Step 2

Drain the chenna in muslin, rinse, and hang for 30 minutes.

Step 3

Knead the chenna with 1 tsp cornflour until smooth, then shape 12 flat discs.

Step 4

Boil 4 cups water with 1 cup sugar, then simmer the discs for 12 minutes.

Step 5

Simmer 1 litre milk with 6 tbsp sugar, saffron and crushed cardamom for 20 minutes.

Step 6

Cool the milk slightly, then gently squeeze the discs and soak them in it.

Step 7

Chill the rasmalai for at least 3 hours until cold and soft.

Step 8

Serve chilled, topped with chopped pistachios and almonds.
