Sizzling Spanish Gambas al Ajillo

Spain cuisine · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Shrimp

  • 1 lb (450 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined (about 24)
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Garlic oil

  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) extra-virgin olive oil
  • 8 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes (or 2 dried guindilla chiles)

Finish and serve

  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp dry sherry
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Crusty bread, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Pat the peeled shrimp dry and season with 1/2 tsp salt.

Step 2

Thinly slice 8 garlic cloves and measure 1/2 cup olive oil.

Step 3

Heat the olive oil, add the garlic and 1 tsp red pepper flakes, and sizzle 1 minute until golden.

Step 4

Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook 2 minutes per side until pink and curled.

Step 5

Dust the shrimp with 1/2 tsp smoked paprika.

Step 6

Pour in 2 tbsp dry sherry and let it bubble 1 minute.

Step 7

Scatter 2 tbsp chopped parsley and tuck in the lemon wedges.

Step 8

Serve sizzling with crusty bread for dipping.
