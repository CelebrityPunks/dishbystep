Jiggly Japanese Cheesecake

Japan cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cheese Batter

  • 250g (8 oz) cream cheese, room temperature
  • 50g (3.5 tbsp) unsalted butter
  • 100ml whole milk
  • 6 large egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 60g (1/2 cup) cake flour, sifted
  • 20g (2.5 tbsp) cornstarch, sifted
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Meringue

  • 6 large egg whites
  • 140g (2/3 cup) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Line an 8-inch round pan with parchment, then wrap the outside in two layers of foil.

Step 2

Melt 250g cream cheese, 50g butter and 100ml milk over a double boiler until smooth, then whisk in the yolks, lemon, flour and cornstarch.

Step 3

Beat 6 egg whites with 1/4 tsp cream of tartar, then add 140g sugar and whip to stiff peaks.

Step 4

Fold one third of the meringue into the batter, then gently fold in the rest.

Step 5

Pour into the pan, tap out the air bubbles, and set the pan in a deep hot-water bath.

Step 6

Bake at 320F for 70 minutes, until golden on top and jiggly in the center.

Step 7

Rest 10 minutes in the oven with the door ajar so the cake does not collapse.

Step 8

Unmold, dust with powdered sugar and slice to reveal the cloud-soft crumb.
