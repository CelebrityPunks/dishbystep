Spaghetti and Meatballs

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Meatballs

  • 1/2 lb ground beef (80/20)
  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Sauce And Pasta

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 1/2 cup torn fresh basil

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix the beef, pork, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, eggs, parsley, garlic, salt, and pepper until just combined.

Step 2

Roll the mixture into 16 golf-ball-size meatballs, about 1 1/2 inches each.

Step 3

Brown the meatballs in 2 tbsp olive oil over medium-high heat, 6 to 8 minutes, turning until golden on all sides.

Step 4

Soften the onion in the same pot 4 to 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes for 30 seconds.

Step 5

Add the crushed tomatoes, sugar, and salt, return the meatballs, and simmer gently for 20 minutes.

Step 6

Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water 9 to 11 minutes until al dente, then reserve 1 cup of the pasta water.

Step 7

Toss the spaghetti with the sauce and a splash of pasta water until glossy, then fold in the fresh basil.

Step 8

Twirl into warm bowls, top with meatballs and extra sauce, and finish with a shower of Parmesan.
