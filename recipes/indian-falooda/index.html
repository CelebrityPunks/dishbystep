Easy Royal Falooda

India cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Basil seeds (sabja) - 2 tbsp
  • Falooda vermicelli - 1.8 oz (50 g)
  • Cold whole milk - 4 cups (950 ml)
  • Granulated sugar - 2 tbsp
  • Water, for soaking - 1 cup

Ingredients

  • Rose syrup - 1/2 cup (120 ml), plus more to drizzle
  • Vanilla ice cream - 4 scoops
  • Chopped pistachios - 1/4 cup (30 g)
  • Dried rose petals - 1 tbsp

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Soak 2 tbsp basil seeds in 1 cup water for 15 minutes until they swell into a clear gel.

Step 2

Boil 1.8 oz falooda vermicelli 4 to 5 minutes until soft, then drain and rinse cold.

Step 3

Stir 2 tbsp sugar into 4 cups cold milk until dissolved, then chill well.

Step 4

Pour 2 tbsp rose syrup into the bottom of each tall glass.

Step 5

Layer in the soaked basil seeds and a nest of the cooked vermicelli.

Step 6

Pour the chilled sweet milk over the layers, leaving 2 inches at the top.

Step 7

Crown each glass with a generous scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Step 8

Drizzle with extra rose syrup, scatter pistachios and rose petals, and serve at once with a long spoon.
