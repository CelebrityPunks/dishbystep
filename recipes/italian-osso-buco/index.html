Italian Osso Buco

Italy cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Shanks and sear

  • 4 veal shanks, cut 1 1/2 inches thick
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, divided

The braise

  • 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium carrots, finely diced
  • 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1 1/2 cups crushed San Marzano tomatoes
  • 2 cups beef or veal stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 sprigs fresh thyme

Gremolata

  • 2 lemons, zested
  • 1/2 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced

To serve

  • Creamy polenta or risotto alla milanese

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Season 4 veal shanks with salt and pepper, then dredge lightly in 1/2 cup flour.

Step 2

Sear in 3 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp butter, 4 minutes per side, until deeply golden.

Step 3

Soften the onion, carrots, celery, and garlic in 2 tbsp butter for 8 minutes.

Step 4

Pour in 1 cup dry white wine, scrape up the browned bits, and simmer 3 minutes.

Step 5

Add the tomatoes, 2 cups stock, bay leaves, and thyme. Cover and braise at 325F for 2 hours.

Step 6

Mix the lemon zest, parsley, and garlic into a fresh gremolata.

Step 7

Simmer the sauce uncovered 10 minutes until silky, then return the shanks to the pot.

Step 8

Spoon over creamy polenta and finish with a spoonful of gremolata.
