Argentine Alfajores

Argentina cuisine · Serves 12 alfajores · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Cookies

  • 1 2/3 cups (200 g) cornstarch
  • 1 1/4 cups (155 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 3/4 cup (170 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2/3 cup (135 g) granulated sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest

Filling & Finishing

  • 1 1/2 cups (450 g) thick dulce de leche
  • 1/2 cup shredded coconut

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 1 2/3 cups cornstarch, 1 1/4 cups flour, 1 tsp baking powder and 1/2 tsp salt.

Step 2

Beat 3/4 cup softened butter with 2/3 cup sugar 3 minutes until pale and fluffy.

Step 3

Beat in 4 egg yolks one at a time, then 2 tsp vanilla and 1 tsp lemon zest.

Step 4

Mix in the dry ingredients on low just until a soft dough forms, then chill 30 minutes.

Step 5

Roll the dough 1/4 inch thick and cut 2-inch rounds with a floured cutter.

Step 6

Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes until just set but still pale.

Step 7

Cool completely, then pipe 1 heaping tsp dulce de leche onto half the cookies.

Step 8

Sandwich the cookies, roll the edges in shredded coconut, and serve.
