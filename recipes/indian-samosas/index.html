Crispy Indian Samosas

India cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp ajwain (carom seeds)
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 4 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/2 cup water, plus more as needed

Filling

  • 1 lb Yukon Gold potatoes (about 3 medium), boiled and crumbled
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil plus 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 onion, diced; 1 tbsp ginger; 1 green chili, minced
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp amchur (dried mango powder)
  • 3/4 tsp fine sea salt; 2 tbsp cilantro

Fry

  • 4 cups neutral oil, for deep frying (350°F)

Serve

  • Tamarind chutney, for serving
  • Mint chutney, for serving

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub 4 tbsp oil into 2 cups flour with 1 tsp ajwain and 1 tsp salt, then knead with 1/2 cup water. Rest 30 minutes.

Step 2

Boil 1 lb potatoes until tender, 15 to 20 minutes, then cool, peel, and crumble.

Step 3

Sizzle 1 tsp cumin seeds in 1 tbsp oil, then soften the onion, ginger, and green chili for 5 minutes.

Step 4

Fold in the peas, spices, and crumbled potatoes. Cook 3 minutes more, then cool completely.

Step 5

Roll each dough ball into a 6 inch oval, cut in half, and form a cone.

Step 6

Fill each cone with 2 tbsp filling and seal the edge with a dab of water.

Step 7

Fry in 350°F oil, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until deep golden and crisp.

Step 8

Serve hot with tamarind and mint chutneys for dipping.
