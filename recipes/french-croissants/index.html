Classic French Croissants

France cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 1/4 cups cold whole milk
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened

Butter + Finish

  • 1 1/4 cups cold unsalted butter
  • 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 4 cups flour, 1/4 cup sugar, 2 tsp salt and the yeast, then add 1 1/4 cups cold milk.

Step 2

Knead 8-10 min until smooth, then chill the dough 1 hour.

Step 3

Encase a 1 1/4 cup slab of cold butter inside the rolled dough.

Step 4

Roll, fold in thirds, and chill 1 hour. Repeat 3 times for 27 layers.

Step 5

Roll the dough 1/4 inch thick and cut it into long triangles.

Step 6

Roll each triangle from base to tip, curve into crescents, and proof 2 hours.

Step 7

Brush with egg wash and bake at 400°F for 18-22 min until deep golden.

Step 8

Cool 10 min on a rack, then tear one open and enjoy the flaky layers.
