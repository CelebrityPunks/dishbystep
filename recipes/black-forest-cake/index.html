Black Forest Cake

Germany cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

The sponge

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 cup (120 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup (50 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt

The cherry filling

  • 2 cups (500 g) pitted sour cherries, drained, juice reserved
  • 1/4 cup reserved cherry juice
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar

The kirsch syrup

  • 1/2 cup reserved cherry juice
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 3 tbsp kirsch

The whipped cream

  • 3 cups (720 ml) cold heavy whipping cream
  • 1/3 cup (40 g) powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

To finish

  • 3 oz (85 g) dark chocolate, shaved
  • 12 whole fresh cherries

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Beat 6 eggs with 1 cup sugar for 4 to 5 minutes until pale and tripled in volume.

Step 2

Sift 1 cup flour, 1/2 cup cocoa, 1 tsp baking powder and 1/4 tsp salt, then fold gently into the eggs.

Step 3

Bake in a greased 9-inch springform at 350 F for 30 to 35 minutes. Cool completely, then slice into 3 layers.

Step 4

Simmer the cherries with 2 tbsp sugar, then thicken with 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed into 1/4 cup cherry juice. Cool.

Step 5

Warm 1/2 cup cherry juice with 2 tbsp sugar, cool, then stir in 3 tbsp kirsch for the soaking syrup.

Step 6

Whip 3 cups cold heavy cream with 1/3 cup powdered sugar and 1 tsp vanilla to stiff peaks.

Step 7

Brush each layer with kirsch syrup, spread cream, spoon over cherries, then stack all three layers.

Step 8

Frost the top and sides with the remaining cream, press on chocolate shavings and the 12 cherries, then chill 2 hours before slicing.
