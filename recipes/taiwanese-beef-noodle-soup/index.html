Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup

Taiwan cuisine · Serves 4 bowls · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Beef and Aromatics

  • 2 lb boneless beef shank, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 2 Tbsp neutral oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1 (2-inch) piece fresh ginger, sliced
  • 3 scallions, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 8 cups water or beef broth

Sauces and Spices

  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp doubanjiang (spicy bean paste)
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup light soy sauce
  • 2 Tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup Shaoxing wine
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 Tbsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves

Noodles and Greens

  • 12 oz dried thick wheat noodles
  • 4 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
  • Sliced scallions, chili oil and pickled mustard greens, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Cover 2 lb beef shank chunks with cold water; boil 3 minutes, drain and rinse clean.

Step 2

Heat 2 Tbsp oil; saute the garlic, ginger, scallions and onion 2 minutes, then stir in the tomato, tomato paste, doubanjiang and sugar.

Step 3

Add the beef, 1/4 cup soy sauce, 2 Tbsp dark soy sauce and 1/4 cup Shaoxing wine; stir 2 minutes to coat.

Step 4

Add 8 cups water or broth with the star anise, cinnamon, Sichuan peppercorns and bay leaves; simmer covered 2 hours until tender.

Step 5

Cook 12 oz wheat noodles per package directions; blanch the baby bok choy 1 to 2 minutes.

Step 6

Divide the noodles among bowls; ladle over the hot broth, beef and bok choy.

Step 7

Top with sliced scallions, chili oil and pickled mustard greens.

Step 8

Serve hot with chopsticks.
