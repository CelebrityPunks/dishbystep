Turkish Lahmacun

Turkey cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground lamb or beef
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Ingredients

  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 1 cup fresh parsley leaves
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, 1 cup warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil, then knead for 8 minutes until smooth.

Step 2

Cover the dough and rest for 1 hour until doubled in size.

Step 3

Mix the lamb, vegetables, tomato paste, spices and parsley into a smooth topping.

Step 4

Divide the dough into 4 balls and roll each one paper-thin.

Step 5

Spread the topping thinly over each round, right to the edges.

Step 6

Bake at 500°F for 7 to 9 minutes until the edges are crisp and browned.

Step 7

Top with fresh parsley, sliced red onion and lemon wedges.

Step 8

Roll up and serve hot with extra lemon.
