Indian Gulab Jamun

India cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 tbsp ghee, melted
  • 1/3 cup warm milk, as needed

Fry

  • 3 cups neutral oil or ghee, for frying (300-320°F)

Serve

  • 2 tbsp crushed pistachios
  • 1 tbsp dried rose petals

Syrup

  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 4 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 8 saffron strands
  • 1 tsp rose water
  • 1/2 tsp lemon juice

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Rub 2 tbsp ghee into 1 cup milk powder, 1/4 cup flour, and 1/4 tsp baking soda, then knead with warm milk.

Step 2

Roll the soft dough into 18 smooth, crack-free balls.

Step 3

Simmer 1 1/2 cups sugar, 1 1/2 cups water, cardamom, and saffron into a sticky syrup.

Step 4

Stir in 1 tsp rose water and 1/2 tsp lemon juice; keep the syrup warm.

Step 5

Fry on LOW heat at 300-320°F for 8-10 minutes until deep golden, stirring gently.

Step 6

Slide the hot jamuns into the warm syrup and soak 1-2 hours.

Step 7

Top with crushed pistachios and dried rose petals.

Step 8

Serve warm so the syrup center melts in your mouth.
