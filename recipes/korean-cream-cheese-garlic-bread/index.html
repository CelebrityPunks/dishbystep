Korean Cream Cheese Garlic Bread

South Korea cuisine · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

The rolls

  • 4 soft dinner rolls (about 3 oz each)

Cream cheese filling

  • 7 oz (200 g) cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 3 tbsp heavy cream

Garlic butter

  • 1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp minced garlic (about 6 cloves)
  • 2 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp dried parsley
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

Step 2

Whisk the cream cheese, sugar and heavy cream until completely smooth, then spoon it into a piping bag.

Step 3

Whisk the melted butter, egg, minced garlic, condensed milk, honey, parsley and salt in a wide bowl.

Step 4

Cut each roll into 6 wedges, slicing almost through but keeping the base attached.

Step 5

Pipe the cream cheese filling deep into every cut of each roll.

Step 6

Dip each filled roll in the garlic butter, coating every wedge generously.

Step 7

Bake 10 to 12 minutes until deeply golden and crisp at the edges.

Step 8

Serve warm, pull a roll apart and watch the cream cheese ooze.
