Crispy Golden Tonkatsu

Japan cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For Frying And Serving

  • 4 cups neutral oil, for frying
  • 1/2 cup tonkatsu sauce, warmed
  • 2 cups finely shredded cabbage
  • Lemon wedges, to serve (optional)

For The Pork

  • 4 boneless pork loin chops, about 1 inch thick (150 to 180 g each)
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 cups panko breadcrumbs

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Score the fat edge of 4 pork chops, then pound them to 3/4 inch thick.

Step 2

Season with salt and pepper, then coat each chop in 1/2 cup flour.

Step 3

Dip in 2 beaten eggs, then press firmly into 2 cups panko.

Step 4

Heat 4 cups oil to 340°F (170°C) and fry 2 chops at a time.

Step 5

Fry 3 to 4 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp.

Step 6

Rest the cutlets 2 minutes on a wire rack.

Step 7

Slice into thick strips to show the juicy center.

Step 8

Serve over shredded cabbage with warm tonkatsu sauce.
