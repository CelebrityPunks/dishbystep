Salvadoran Pupusas

El Salvador cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Curtido

  • 2 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 1 medium carrot, grated
  • 1/2 white onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt

Filling

  • 1 1/2 cups refried beans
  • 2 cups shredded quesillo or mozzarella

Masa

  • 2 cups masa harina
  • 1 1/2 cups warm water, plus more if needed
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt

Salsa Roja

  • 4 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 white onion, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 2 cups shredded cabbage with 1 grated carrot and 1/2 sliced onion.

Step 2

Pour 1 cup boiling water over the vegetables, drain, then mix with 1/2 cup vinegar, 1 tsp oregano and 1/2 tsp salt. Chill 2 hours.

Step 3

Knead 2 cups masa harina with 1 1/2 cups warm water and 1 tsp salt until soft and smooth, then rest 15 minutes.

Step 4

Stir 1 1/2 cups refried beans with 2 cups shredded cheese for the filling.

Step 5

Flatten a 1/4 cup masa ball, add 2 tbsp filling, and pinch the edges to seal it closed.

Step 6

Pat each filled ball into a 1/2 inch thick disc about 4 inches wide.

Step 7

Cook on a lightly oiled griddle over medium heat, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden spots appear.

Step 8

Serve the pupusas hot with curtido and warm salsa roja on the side.
