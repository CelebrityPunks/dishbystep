Classic Chicken Fajitas

Mexico cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Ingredients

  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup prepared guacamole
  • Lime wedges, to serve
  • Fresh cilantro, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 1.5 lb sliced chicken with 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp lime juice and the spices.

Step 2

Marinate the chicken for 20 minutes while you slice the peppers and onion.

Step 3

Sear the chicken in a hot skillet for 5 to 6 minutes until charred at the edges.

Step 4

Remove the chicken, then cook 1 sliced onion and 2 sliced bell peppers for 5 minutes.

Step 5

Return the chicken to the pan and toss everything together for 1 minute.

Step 6

Warm 8 flour tortillas in a dry skillet for 30 seconds per side.

Step 7

Fill each tortilla with chicken, peppers and onions.

Step 8

Serve hot with sour cream, guacamole, lime wedges and fresh cilantro.
