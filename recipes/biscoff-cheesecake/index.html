Biscoff Cheesecake

Belgium cuisine · Serves 10 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

The crust

  • 250g Biscoff speculoos cookies (about 32 cookies)
  • 100g unsalted butter, melted

The filling

  • 600g full-fat cream cheese, softened
  • 250g smooth Biscoff spread
  • 80g powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300ml heavy whipping cream, chilled

The topping

  • 150g smooth Biscoff spread, melted
  • 2 tbsp crushed Biscoff cookie crumbs

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Crush 250g Biscoff cookies into fine crumbs with a food processor.

Step 2

Stir the crumbs with 100g melted butter, press into a 9-inch springform, and chill 20 minutes.

Step 3

Beat 600g cream cheese with 250g Biscoff spread, 80g powdered sugar and 1 tsp vanilla until smooth.

Step 4

Whip 300ml cold heavy cream to soft peaks, then fold it gently into the filling.

Step 5

Spread the filling over the chilled crust and smooth the top with a spatula.

Step 6

Pour 150g melted Biscoff spread over the top and tilt the pan to coat it edge to edge.

Step 7

Refrigerate at least 6 hours, or overnight, until fully set.

Step 8

Release the springform, sprinkle with cookie crumbs, and slice with a warm knife.
