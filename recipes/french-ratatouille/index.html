French Ratatouille

France cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Pantry & Herbs

  • 1/3 cup olive oil, divided
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, divided
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped

Vegetables

  • 1 large eggplant (1 lb), cut in 1/2 in cubes
  • 2 medium zucchini (1 lb), cut in 1/2 in cubes
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut in 1 in pieces
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, cut in 1 in pieces
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 1 lb cubed eggplant with 1 tsp salt and rest 20 min.

Step 2

Saute the eggplant in 2 tbsp olive oil until golden, then set aside.

Step 3

Cook the onion and peppers 8 min until soft, then stir in 4 minced garlic cloves.

Step 4

Add 1 lb cubed zucchini and cook 5 min until just tender.

Step 5

Pour in 28 oz crushed tomatoes, thyme, basil, and the bay leaf.

Step 6

Return the eggplant and simmer 15 min until everything melts together.

Step 7

Rest 10 min off the heat, remove the bay leaf, and shower with fresh basil.

Step 8

Spoon into warm bowls and serve with crusty bread.
