Classic Chicken Tortilla Soup

Mexico cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jalapeno, seeded and diced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • Juice of 1 lime

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp oil, for brushing
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • Fresh cilantro, to serve
  • Lime wedges, to serve

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pot and cook 1 diced onion for 5 minutes until soft.

Step 2

Stir in 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 diced jalapeno, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp chili powder and cook 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 3

Pour in 4 cups chicken broth and 1 can diced tomatoes and bring to a simmer.

Step 4

Add 1 can black beans, 1 cup corn and 2 cups shredded chicken and simmer 15 minutes.

Step 5

Stir in the juice of 1 lime and season with salt and pepper to taste.

Step 6

Brush 4 corn tortillas with oil, cut into strips, and bake at 400F for 8 to 10 minutes until crisp.

Step 7

Ladle the soup into bowls and top with the crispy tortilla strips.

Step 8

Finish with diced avocado, shredded cheese, sour cream and cilantro and serve with lime wedges.
