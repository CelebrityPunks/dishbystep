Korean Kimchi Fried Rice

South Korea cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked day-old short-grain rice, chilled
  • 1 cup kimchi, chopped
  • 2 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 1 cup diced Spam
  • 1/2 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 6 large eggs
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Heat 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat, then fry 1 cup diced Spam and 1/2 diced onion for 4 minutes until golden.

Step 2

Add 1 cup chopped kimchi and 2 minced garlic cloves, then cook for 2 minutes until fragrant.

Step 3

Add 3 cups chilled rice, 1 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 2 tbsp kimchi juice, then stir-fry for 4 to 5 minutes until steaming.

Step 4

Push the rice aside, scramble 2 eggs in the empty space, then fold them through the rice.

Step 5

Stir in 1 tsp sesame oil and half the sliced green onions.

Step 6

Fry the remaining 4 eggs sunny-side up in 1 tbsp oil for 2 to 3 minutes.

Step 7

Spoon the hot rice into warm bowls.

Step 8

Top each bowl with a fried egg, sesame seeds, and green onions, then serve hot.
