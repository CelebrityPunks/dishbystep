Korean Bibimbap

South Korea cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Marinated Beef

  • 8 oz beef tenderloin or sirloin, very thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

For The Vegetables

  • 2 large carrots, julienned
  • 1 zucchini, julienned
  • 1 bunch spinach
  • 4 cups bean sprouts
  • 4 oz shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • Toasted sesame oil, to finish

For The Bibimbap Sauce

  • 1/4 cup gochujang
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce
  • 3 tsp sugar
  • 1 clove garlic, finely grated
  • 2 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tsp sesame seeds

For Serving

  • 4 cups cooked short-grain rice
  • 4 eggs

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 8 oz thinly sliced beef with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 2 tsp sesame oil, 3 minced garlic cloves and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Marinate 30 minutes.

Step 2

Blanch 1 bunch spinach 30 seconds and 4 cups bean sprouts 2 minutes in salted boiling water. Squeeze dry, then toss with sesame oil, soy sauce and salt.

Step 3

Stir fry 2 julienned carrots in 1 tsp oil with 1/4 tsp salt for 2 to 3 minutes until just tender.

Step 4

Sear 4 oz sliced shiitake with 1 tsp soy sauce for 3 to 4 minutes, then 1 julienned zucchini for 2 minutes more, until bright.

Step 5

Sear the marinated beef in a hot oiled skillet in a single layer, 2 to 3 minutes per side, until caramelized.

Step 6

Fry 4 eggs sunny side up in 1 tbsp oil for 2 to 3 minutes until the whites set and the edges turn crisp.

Step 7

Whisk 1/4 cup gochujang with 2 tbsp mirin, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce, 3 tsp sugar, grated garlic and 2 1/2 tsp sesame oil.

Step 8

Mound 1 cup hot rice per bowl, arrange the beef and vegetables in bright rainbow sections, crown with a fried egg, sesame seeds and a spoon of sauce.
