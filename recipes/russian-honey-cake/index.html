Russian Honey Cake

Russia cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Cake Layers

  • 3/4 cup (255 g) honey
  • 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 3 1/2 cups (440 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt

For The Frosting

  • 2 cups (480 g) sour cream
  • 1 cup (240 ml) heavy whipping cream
  • 1/2 cup (60 g) powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Burn 3/4 cup honey in a saucepan 5 to 7 minutes until deep amber and foamy.

Step 2

Whisk in 3/4 cup sugar, 3 eggs, and 1/2 cup melted butter, then fold in 3 1/2 cups flour, 1 tsp baking soda, and 1/2 tsp salt.

Step 3

Divide the dough into 8 equal portions and roll each into a thin 8-inch circle.

Step 4

Bake the layers at 350°F for 4 to 5 minutes each until deep golden.

Step 5

Trim each layer with a plate and crumble the trimmings into fine crumbs.

Step 6

Beat 2 cups sour cream, 1 cup heavy cream, 1/2 cup powdered sugar, and 1 tsp vanilla until fluffy.

Step 7

Stack all 8 layers with generous frosting between each layer.

Step 8

Coat the whole cake with frosting, press the crumbs all over, chill overnight, then slice and serve.
