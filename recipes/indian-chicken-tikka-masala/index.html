Indian Chicken Tikka Masala

India cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Masala Sauce

  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp ground chilli (or mild paprika)
  • 200ml water
  • 100ml double cream
  • salt and black pepper, to taste

For The Tikka Marinade

  • 600g boneless, skinless chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 150g natural yogurt
  • 2 tbsp tikka curry paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 5cm fresh ginger, grated

To Serve

  • fresh coriander, chopped
  • warm naan bread and basmati rice

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Mix 150g yogurt, 2 tbsp tikka paste, 2 tbsp lemon juice, the garlic and ginger, then toss in 600g chicken and marinate 30 minutes.

Step 2

Thread the chicken onto skewers and grill on high for 10-12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.

Step 3

Fry 1 large diced onion in 2 tbsp vegetable oil for 8-10 minutes until soft and golden.

Step 4

Stir in 2 tbsp tomato puree and 1 tsp chilli powder; cook 2 minutes until the oil turns red.

Step 5

Pour in 400g chopped tomatoes and 200ml water; simmer 15 minutes until thick and rich.

Step 6

Slide the grilled chicken into the sauce and simmer 5 minutes so the flavors come together.

Step 7

Stir in 100ml double cream, warm through 2 minutes, then season with salt and black pepper.

Step 8

Scatter with fresh coriander and serve with warm naan bread and basmati rice.
