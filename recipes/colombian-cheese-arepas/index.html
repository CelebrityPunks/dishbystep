Colombian Cheese Arepas

Colombia cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter [28g]
  • 2 cups pre-cooked white cornmeal (masarepa) [300g]
  • 2.5 cups warm water [590ml]

Filling

  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella [200g]

To Cook

  • 2 tbsp neutral oil (for the skillet)

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Stir 2 cups (300g) masarepa, 1 tsp salt and 2 tbsp melted butter into 2 1/2 cups (590ml) warm water; rest 10 minutes.

Step 2

Knead the dough briefly until smooth, then divide it into 8 even balls.

Step 3

Press each ball into a 1/2-inch thick disc, about 3 inches wide.

Step 4

Tuck a generous mound of shredded mozzarella into each disc and seal the edges closed.

Step 5

Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat until hot.

Step 6

Cook the arepas 6 to 7 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp.

Step 7

Rest the arepas 2 minutes so the cheese settles into a molten core.

Step 8

Serve warm with extra butter or hogao salsa and pull one open for the cheese stretch.
