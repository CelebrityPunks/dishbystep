French Chocolate Soufflé

France cuisine · Serves 4 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

For The Ramekins

  • 1 tbsp softened butter, for coating
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar, for coating

For The Chocolate Base

  • 4 oz (115 g) dark chocolate (70%), chopped
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

For The Meringue

  • 4 large egg whites, at room temperature
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Brush 4 ramekins with softened butter, then coat with 2 tbsp sugar.

Step 2

Melt 4 oz dark chocolate with 3 tbsp butter until smooth.

Step 3

Whisk 1/2 cup milk with 2 tbsp flour into a smooth thick paste.

Step 4

Stir the chocolate into the paste, then whisk in 3 yolks and 1 tsp vanilla.

Step 5

Beat 4 egg whites with 1/4 tsp cream of tartar, adding 1/4 cup sugar, to stiff glossy peaks.

Step 6

Fold a spoonful of meringue in to lighten, then gently fold in the rest.

Step 7

Fill the ramekins, level the tops, and wipe the rims clean.

Step 8

Bake at 375°F for 12 to 15 minutes, dust with powdered sugar, and serve immediately.
