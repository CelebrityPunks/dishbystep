Classic French Crème Brûlée

France cuisine · Serves 6 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Caramel Topping

  • 1/4 cup superfine sugar, for caramelizing
  • Hot water, for the water bath

Custard

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/8 tsp fine sea salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Simmer 2 cups cream with the vanilla bean until steaming, then steep off heat for 15 min.

Step 2

Preheat the oven to 325°F and set 6 ramekins in a deep roasting pan.

Step 3

Whisk 5 egg yolks with 1/2 cup sugar and 1/8 tsp salt until pale and smooth.

Step 4

Whisk the warm cream into the yolks in a thin stream, then strain into a pitcher.

Step 5

Divide the custard among the 6 ramekins and pour hot water halfway up the pan sides.

Step 6

Bake at 325°F for 30-35 min until the edges set and the centers still wobble.

Step 7

Cool 30 min, then chill at least 4 hours or overnight until firm.

Step 8

Dust each custard with sugar and torch in slow circles until amber and crisp, then crack and serve.
