Sachertorte

Austria cuisine · Serves 12 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

The chocolate sponge

  • 130 g dark chocolate (min. 55% cocoa), chopped
  • 150 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 100 g icing sugar
  • 6 large eggs, separated
  • 100 g granulated sugar
  • 140 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt

The apricot filling

  • 200 g apricot jam, warmed until smooth

The chocolate glaze

  • 200 g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Melt 130 g dark chocolate until glossy, then set aside to cool slightly.

Step 2

Beat 150 g butter with 100 g icing sugar and 1 tsp vanilla until pale, then whisk in the 6 egg yolks one at a time.

Step 3

Beat the 6 egg whites with 100 g sugar to soft peaks, then gently fold through the chocolate mixture with 140 g flour and a pinch of salt.

Step 4

Bake in a lined 9-inch springform at 340 F for 50 minutes until a skewer comes out clean, then cool completely.

Step 5

Halve the cooled cake, warm the apricot jam and spread it over the base layer, then sandwich the top layer back on.

Step 6

Brush a thin coat of warm jam over the top and sides of the cake so the glaze sets perfectly smooth.

Step 7

Heat 200 ml cream to a bare simmer, pour over 200 g chopped chocolate, then stir into a glossy glaze and pour it over the cake.

Step 8

Let the glaze set, slice with a hot dry knife, and serve with softly whipped cream.
