Golden Crispy Knafeh

Palestine cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Pastry And Cheese

  • 1 lb (450g) kataifi shredded phyllo dough, thawed
  • 12 tbsp (1 1/2 sticks, 170g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cups shredded low-moisture mozzarella
  • 1 cup whole-milk ricotta

Syrup And Topping

  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water
  • 1/2 cup crushed pistachios

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Toss 1 lb shredded kataifi with 12 tbsp melted butter until every strand is coated.

Step 2

Press half the buttered kataifi firmly and evenly into a buttered 12-inch round pan.

Step 3

Mix 2 cups shredded mozzarella with 1 cup ricotta and spread it over the base, leaving a 1/2-inch border.

Step 4

Cover the cheese with the remaining kataifi and press down firmly to seal the edges.

Step 5

Bake at 375°F for 30-35 min until deeply golden and crisp on top.

Step 6

Simmer 1 1/2 cups sugar, 3/4 cup water and 1 tbsp lemon juice for 10 min, then stir in 1 tsp orange blossom water.

Step 7

Flip the hot knafeh onto a platter and pour 1 cup of warm syrup over it.

Step 8

Scatter 1/2 cup crushed pistachios over the top and serve immediately.
