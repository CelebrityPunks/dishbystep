Italian Garden Focaccia

Italy cuisine · Serves 8 · 8 visual steps

Ingredients

Dough

  • 4 cups (500g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp (7g) instant dry yeast
  • 2 tsp (12g) fine sea salt
  • 2 cups (480ml) warm water
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus 3 tbsp for the top

Garden Toppings

  • 1 pint (300g) cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup pitted Kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary leaves
  • 1 tsp flaky sea salt

Method, in pictures

Step 1

Whisk 4 cups flour, 2 tsp yeast, and 2 tsp salt in a large bowl.

Step 2

Add 2 cups warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil; stir into a shaggy no-knead dough.

Step 3

Cover and rest 2 hours (or overnight) until bubbly and doubled.

Step 4

Pour into an oiled 9x13 pan and stretch the dough into the corners.

Step 5

Dimple the dough with oiled fingertips and drizzle 3 tbsp olive oil on top.

Step 6

Arrange the tomatoes, red onion, olives, and rosemary like a garden; sprinkle 1 tsp flaky salt.

Step 7

Bake at 425F for 22-25 minutes until deeply golden and crisp.

Step 8

Cool 10 minutes, slice into 8 squares, and serve warm.
